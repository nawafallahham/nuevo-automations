Questions before you book

Is the audit really free?

Yes. Thirty minutes, no cost, no obligation, and you keep the plan we put together even if you never work with us.

What does M2S cost if I go ahead?

One fixed monthly fee, based on your ad budget, your channels and how many products we load. You get the number in writing after the audit. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta or Google.

Do I need a new WhatsApp number?

No. We work with the number you already use, and your team can still use the WhatsApp app.

Does M2S reply in Arabic?

Yes. The assistant replies in English or Arabic, in the language your customer writes in.

How long does the setup take?

We give you a realistic timeline in the plan. It depends on how many products we load and which store and channels we connect.
