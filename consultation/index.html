Is the audit really free?

Yes. Twenty minutes, no cost, no obligation, and you keep the plan we put together even if you never work with us.

What does it cost if I go ahead?

M2S is one fixed monthly fee, based on your ad budget, your channels and how many products or services we load. You get the number in writing after the audit. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta.

Do I need to change my WhatsApp number?

No. We work with the number you already use, and your team can still use the WhatsApp app.

Does it handle Arabic?

Yes. The assistant replies in English or Arabic, matching the language your customer writes in.

How long does setup take?

We'll give you a realistic timeline in the plan. It depends on how many products we load and which store and channels we connect.
