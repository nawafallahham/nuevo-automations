Short answer

For businesses in Sharjah and the Northern Emirates, we run Meta ads that open a WhatsApp chat, and M2S, our Message to Sale system, answers in seconds in English or Arabic, takes the order or booking in the chat and follows up people who went quiet. Then it handles the admin: the order goes to your team with the customer's details, the sales pipeline updates itself, hot leads trigger an instant alert, and you get a monthly report of which ads made the sales. Nuevo Automations FZE LLC is registered in Sharjah Publishing City Free Zone.

The problem

Where Sharjah businesses lose sales and hours

In a small business, the owner and a few staff carry every step. Ads bring messages from Al Nahda, Muwaileh and Ajman late in the evening, and they're answered the next morning, if at all. Customers who asked the price and went quiet are never chased, because there's no list of who is waiting.

When a sale does close, the admin starts: someone copies the address into the delivery list, forwards the order to the warehouse, updates a spreadsheet and tries to remember which customer came from which ad. None of it is difficult. All of it is repetitive, and it takes time away from customers.

What it looks like

A late-night order, handed over by morning

Khalid asked at 11 PM, when nobody was working. The assistant confirmed delivery to his area, quoted the price from your product list and took a cash-on-delivery order. By the time your team started, the order was waiting on WhatsApp with his name, address and items, and logged in the pipeline against the ad he came from.

If he had asked for a bulk price, a custom size or anything outside your product list, your team would have got an instant WhatsApp alert with a summary of the chat. The assistant never invents prices or products.

After the sale

The sales admin that runs on its own

Business process automation doesn't need an ERP project. Most of the admin around a sale follows the same steps every time, so M2S runs them on a trigger: an order is confirmed, a payment comes through, a lead goes quiet.

We start with the steps that cost you the most, usually order hand-off and follow-up, and connect them to the tools you already use. Being registered in Sharjah, we know how local customers buy: late-evening questions, cash on delivery, and a mix of English and Arabic in the same chat.

FAQ

Common questions from Sharjah businesses

Are you based in Sharjah?

Yes. Nuevo Automations FZE LLC is registered in Sharjah Publishing City Free Zone. We work with businesses in Sharjah and across the UAE.

Do you work with businesses outside Sharjah?

Yes. We work with businesses in Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah, Umm Al Quwain, Fujairah, Dubai and Abu Dhabi as well, in English and Arabic.

Which sales admin can be automated?

The work that follows a sale in the chat: handing the order to your team with the customer's details, alerts for hot leads and new orders, keeping the sales pipeline up to date, writing orders to your store or sheet, and the monthly results report.

Do I need new software or a new number?

No. You keep your own WhatsApp number and can still use the WhatsApp app, and we connect M2S to the tools you already use, such as your store, Google Sheets or your calendar.

Will customers get too many messages?

No. Follow-ups stop the moment a customer buys or replies, never go out at night, stop for anyone who says "stop", and run once per customer per week.
