Business process automation means having repeatable steps in your business run by software on a trigger, instead of by hand. For a small sales business, those steps usually follow an order: sending it to the person who fulfils it, recording it in a sheet or store, and alerting the team. The steps after a sale that we automate

Each step as it's done by hand today, and what runs on its own instead.

  • Order hand-off

    By hand: someone forwards screenshots to the person who packs.

    Automated: each order or booking reaches your team on WhatsApp with name, address and items.

  • Order records

    By hand: orders are typed into a spreadsheet at the end of the day.

    Automated: a row is written to your Google Sheet or an order created in your store, as it happens.

  • Team alerts

    By hand: a hot lead waits until someone checks the phone.

    Automated: an instant WhatsApp alert with a chat summary when a lead or order needs a person.

One late order in Muwaileh, no morning admin
