Short answer

Business process automation means having repeatable steps in your business run by software on a trigger, instead of by hand. For a small sales business, those steps usually follow an order: sending it to the person who fulfils it, recording it in a sheet or store, and alerting the team. What it looks like

One late order, no morning admin

The order came in after closing. By the time your team arrived, it had already been handed to the warehouse and recorded, and the pipeline showed it as Ordered against the ad it came from.

Anything unusual, such as a trade price or a custom size, would have gone to a person with a summary instead.

Local ads

Ads aimed at the areas you actually serve

We run click-to-WhatsApp ads on Instagram and Facebook aimed at the parts of Sharjah and Ajman you deliver to or serve, such as Al Nahda, Muwaileh, Al Majaz or Al Nuaimiya, and leave out areas you don't. The assistant answers in English or Arabic and confirms delivery or visit details from your own information.

Each chat is tagged with its ad, so the monthly M2S Results report shows which campaigns and areas produced orders, not just messages.
