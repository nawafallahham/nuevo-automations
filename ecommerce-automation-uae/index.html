Short answer

For UAE online stores, we run Meta ads that open a WhatsApp chat, and M2S, our Message to Sale system, answers every message in seconds from your own catalogue, takes the order with a card payment link or cash on delivery, and follows up with abandoned carts, unpaid links and shoppers who went quiet. You get a monthly report showing which ads made the sales.

The problem

Where UAE online stores lose orders

Shoppers find you on Instagram, TikTok or your website, then message on WhatsApp: is it in stock, do you have it in M, how much is delivery to Al Ain, can I pay cash. Every slow answer sends the order to a faster store, and late evening is the busiest time for these questions and the quietest for your team.

Then the sale slips away in small steps. Someone asks the price and never replies. A payment link goes out and never gets paid. A shopper adds to cart on your website and leaves. Nobody follows up, because the team is busy packing today's orders. And when the month ends, you can't tell which ad actually made money.

What it looks like

Question to payment in one chat

The assistant answers from your product list, never invents a price, and asks for what it needs to complete the order. When the payment comes through, the order is marked paid and your team gets it with the customer's details.

If Sara had asked for cash on delivery, the assistant would have confirmed a COD order instead. If she'd asked about a return, a wholesale order or anything outside your product list, your team would get an instant WhatsApp alert with a summary of the chat.

Follow-ups

Winning back carts, links and quiet shoppers

Most shoppers who leave aren't saying no. M2S follows up with three groups: people who left a cart on your website, people who asked for a payment link and didn't pay, and people who asked a price and went silent.

Every follow-up stops the moment the customer buys or replies, and the assistant picks up the conversation knowing exactly what was in their cart. Nothing goes out at night, anyone who says "stop" is never messaged again, and each customer gets at most one sequence a week.

Good to know: cart recovery connects to your store during onboarding, and we confirm it's live for your store before it counts in your report. On the official WhatsApp API, messages sent more than 24 hours after the customer's last message use a Meta-approved template, which we set up for you.

FAQ

Common questions from store owners

Can customers pay inside the WhatsApp chat?

Yes. Once we connect your payment provider, such as Strabl or Ziina, the assistant sends a secure card payment link with the right amount, and the order is marked paid when the payment comes through.

Does it handle cash on delivery?

Yes. If the customer prefers to pay at the door, the assistant confirms a cash-on-delivery order with their details and passes it to your team.

Does abandoned cart recovery work with my store?

It works with Shopify, and with other websites through our API. We connect it during onboarding and confirm it's live for your store before it counts in your report.

Will customers get spammed?

No. Follow-ups stop the moment a customer buys or replies, never go out at night, stop for anyone who says "stop", and run once per customer per week.

How much does it cost?

M2S is one fixed monthly fee based on your ad budget, your channels and the size of your catalogue, quoted after a free audit. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta.
