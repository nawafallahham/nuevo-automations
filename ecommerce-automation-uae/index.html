E-commerce automation for UAE stores: catalogue, checkout and carts that come back

Shoppers ask "do you deliver to Al Ain?" or "does it come in black?" before they buy. M2S answers from your synced catalogue, takes the order by card or cash on delivery and chases the carts left behind.

Short answer

E-commerce automation is the use of software to handle store tasks that would otherwise be done by hand, such as answering product questions, taking orders and recovering abandoned carts. For UAE stores selling on WhatsApp, it means the assistant reads your live catalogue, answers delivery questions, and closes by payment link or cash on delivery.

Catalogue sync

Your store's catalogue, kept in sync

Shopify, directly

Products, variants and prices come straight from your Shopify store, so the assistant quotes what you actually sell.

Other stores via API

Other store platforms and custom websites connect through our API.

No guessing on availability

If a customer asks about something the assistant can't confirm, like a restock date, it says so and passes the question to your team.

COD or card

Cash on delivery and payment links, side by side

Many UAE shoppers still want to pay on delivery, while others prefer to pay by card straight away. The assistant offers whichever you allow. A card order gets a secure payment link from your payment provider; a COD order is confirmed with the address and sent to your team.

You decide the rules: COD only up to a set order value, only in certain emirates, or not at all for sale items.

Delivery questions it answers

  • Which emirates and areas you deliver to
  • Delivery times by area, from your own terms
  • Delivery fees and free-delivery thresholds
  • Same-day or next-day cut-off times, if you offer them
  • Return and exchange policy

Abandoned carts

Carts followed up where shoppers actually reply

When a shopper leaves a cart on Shopify, or on another store connected through our API, M2S follows up on WhatsApp, knowing exactly what was in it. If they reply with a question, the assistant answers it and finishes the order in the chat.

The sequence stops when they reply or buy, nothing is sent at night, and no shopper gets more than one sequence a week. We confirm cart recovery is live for your store before it counts in your report.

FAQ

Questions from online store owners

Does it work with Shopify?

Yes. Shopify connects directly for products, prices and abandoned carts. Other stores connect through our API.

Can customers pay cash on delivery?

Yes, if you allow it. The assistant can offer cash on delivery, a card payment link from your payment provider, or both, under the rules you set.

Can it answer delivery-area and delivery-time questions?

Yes. We load your delivery areas, fees, times and cut-offs, and the assistant answers from them. Anything not covered goes to your team.

How does abandoned cart recovery work?

When a shopper leaves a cart, M2S follows up on WhatsApp with what was in it. The sequence stops as soon as they reply or buy.
