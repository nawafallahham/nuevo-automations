Salon and spa automation in Dubai that books the right chair for the right time

A blow-dry takes 45 minutes, a keratin treatment three hours, and Maria only works afternoons. M2S knows your menu, durations and team, so it books slots that actually fit.

Short answer

Salon and spa automation is booking and client messaging handled by software instead of by the front desk. It quotes services from your menu, books a slot long enough for the treatment with the stylist or therapist the client prefers, sells packages, sends reminders to cut no-shows and invites regular clients back when they're due.

Menu and durations

Your service menu, with the time each service takes

Prices from your menu

Every service, variation and add-on quoted as you price it, never invented.

Real durations

Slots offered only where there's time for the whole service, including colour processing.

Stylist or therapist

"Can I have Maria again?" It books with the person requested, or offers someone else at the same time.

Packages

Courses of treatments and bundles explained and sold in the chat, with a payment link if you want one.

Regulars

Bringing regulars back when they're due

Most services repeat: roots every few weeks, nails every couple of weeks, a massage each month. We set a rebooking interval per service, and M2S sends a friendly invitation when a regular is due, with free times from their usual stylist.

Invitations follow the same rules as every follow-up: none at night, never to anyone who said stop, and at most one sequence per client per week.

No-shows

Fewer empty chairs from people who forget

An empty chair in the middle of a busy Saturday is lost income. M2S sends a reminder before each appointment and makes it easy to move the booking instead of simply not turning up, which frees the slot for someone else.

For long or high-value services, you can require a deposit through a payment link from your payment provider before the booking is confirmed.

New clients

Offer ads that fill quiet days

We run offer and package ads on Instagram and Facebook that open a WhatsApp chat. A new client asks about the offer, gets the exact price and duration from your menu, and books a slot, often on the weekday mornings you want to fill.

Every chat is tagged with the ad that started it, so the monthly report shows which offers brought clients who booked and came back, not only which got the most messages.

FAQ

Questions from salon and spa owners

Can clients choose their stylist or therapist?

Yes. The assistant books with the person they ask for when that person is free, or offers someone else at the same time.

Does it understand that services take different lengths of time?

Yes. We load the duration of each service, so it only offers slots with enough time for the whole treatment.

Can it sell packages?

Yes. Packages and courses from your menu are explained in the chat, and can be paid with a payment link from your payment provider.

How does it reduce no-shows?

It sends a reminder before each appointment with an easy way to confirm or move it, and can take a deposit for services you choose.
