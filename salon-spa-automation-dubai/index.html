Short answer

For salons and spas in Dubai, we run Meta ads for your offers and packages that open a WhatsApp chat, and M2S, our Message to Sale system, answers "how much?" from your own menu in seconds, in Arabic or English, offers real open times, books the appointment into your calendar and, if you want, sends a deposit link. It then sends reminders, follows up with people who asked and went quiet, and nudges regulars when they're due to rebook.

The problem

The messages that pile up while your team is busy

Salon messages arrive when nobody can answer them: in the middle of a colour, between back-to-back clients, and late at night when someone is scrolling Instagram and decides they want a blow-dry tomorrow. How much is a lash lift? Is anyone free this afternoon? Is the package still on? Can I have the same stylist as last time? Do you have a bridal package for five? Each one that waits is a chair that might stay empty, and a paid ad click that went to another salon.

The bigger leak is quieter. People ask the price, get it and never reply. Clients book and don't show. Regulars who would happily come back for their next colour, refill or facial aren't reminded, so they book wherever had a slot when they finally thought of it. New clients cost you ads and discounts. Regulars only cost a well-timed message.

What it looks like

Rebooking & follow-ups

Bringing back quiet leads and regulars

M2S follows up with two groups. People who asked a price and went silent get a short, friendly message with two open times, a few hours later and again the next day. Regulars get a rebooking nudge timed to the service they had: a few weeks after a colour, a shorter cycle for nails and lash refills, and the next session of a spa or facial package before they forget they have one left.

Every follow-up stops the moment the client books or replies, and the assistant picks up the conversation knowing what they had last time. Nothing goes out at night, anyone who says "stop" is never messaged again, and each client gets at most one sequence a week.

    The AI takes over and books the next visit.

Good to know: you keep your own WhatsApp number and your team can still reply from the WhatsApp app, for example to send before-and-after photos. On the official WhatsApp API, messages sent more than 24 hours after the client's last message, such as rebooking nudges, use a Meta-approved template, which we set up for you.

FAQ

Common questions from salon and spa owners

Can clients book outside opening hours?

Yes. The assistant answers and books at any hour, into open slots within your working hours, so a message at midnight becomes a booking for tomorrow.

Can you take deposits in the chat?

Yes, if you want to. Once we connect your payment provider, the assistant sends a secure card payment link for the deposit or the full package, and the booking is confirmed when it's paid.

Can clients choose a specific stylist or therapist?

The assistant notes the preference and passes it to your team with the booking, rather than promising it. Your staff can see and adjust every booking.

How do rebooking reminders work?

You set the right gap for each service, and a personal message goes out when the client is due, offering open slots. It stops as soon as the client books or replies, and never goes out at night.

Does it reply in Arabic?

Yes. It replies in Arabic and English, in whichever language the client writes.
