Store, CRM and calendar integrations for WhatsApp sales

Every WhatsApp sale lands where your team already works. Your store, CRM, spreadsheet, calendar and payment provider are connected to M2S by API and webhooks during setup. Where a tool has no direct route, n8n, Make or Zapier fills the gap.

Book a free audit Ask on WhatsApp

An integration is a connection that lets two systems share data automatically, through an API or a webhook. For M2S it means products and prices come in from your store or sheet, free times from your calendar, and payment confirmations from your payment provider, while orders, leads and bookings go back out without anyone copying them.

Integrations made during setup

  • Online store

    Shopify connects directly; other stores through our API. Products and prices in; orders and abandoned carts out.

    Without it, the assistant quotes a price you've changed.

  • Payment provider

    Your payment provider confirms the card payment and the order is marked paid automatically.

    Without it, someone checks the bank app to see who paid.

  • Google Sheets

    Orders, leads or bookings written as rows, in the columns your team already uses.

    Without it, orders live only in a phone.

  • Calendar

    Free times read from your calendar; confirmed bookings written back.

    Without it, bookings clash or get double-booked.

  • CRM

    Leads and stage changes sent alongside the M2S pipeline.

    Without it, your CRM and your WhatsApp disagree.

Direct connections first, n8n, Make or Zapier second

M2S runs on OrcaFlo, our own platform, and connects directly to most tools by API or webhook. A webhook is a message one system sends another when something happens, such as "payment received".

When a tool has no direct route, we use a workflow tool as the glue. If you already run n8n, Make or Zapier, we can usually build on your account rather than adding another one. Integrations are part of M2S setup, not sold as a separate project.

Keeping integrations working after launch

Connections usually break without warning: an app renames a field, a password changes, an access token expires. Because we run M2S month to month, we watch for failed runs and fix them, rather than leaving your team to discover a gap at the end of the month.

Abandoned cart recovery is connected during onboarding, and we confirm it's live for your store before it counts in your report.

When n8n, Make or Zapier is the right glue

If you already use one of them, we usually build there, so your team keeps one place to look.

Zapier

Suits simple, step-by-step connections between popular cloud apps.

Make

Handles branching and lists well, such as splitting one order into line items or routing by source.

n8n

Suits higher volumes, custom APIs, or keeping workflows on your own server.

Questions about integrations

Do I need n8n, Make or Zapier to use M2S?

No. Most tools connect directly by API or webhook. We only add a workflow tool where a tool has no direct route, and we can build on an account you already use.

Which tools can you connect?

Typically your online store, your payment provider, Google Sheets, your calendar and your CRM. Shopify connects directly, and other stores connect through our API.

Are integrations priced separately?

No. Connecting your tools is part of M2S setup and included in the fixed monthly fee. We don't sell automation projects on their own.

What happens if an integration breaks?

We watch for failed runs while we run M2S and fix them, for example when an app renames a field or an access token expires.
