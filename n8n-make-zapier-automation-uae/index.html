Short answer

When we set up M2S, our ads-to-WhatsApp sales system, we connect it to the tools your business already runs on: Shopify or other online stores through our API, Google Sheets, your CRM, your calendar and payment providers such as Strabl or Ziina. Most connections run directly; where n8n, Make or Zapier is the right glue for a particular tool, we use it. This is part of M2S setup, not a separately priced build service.

The problem

Why a WhatsApp sale often never reaches your systems

A sale closes in a WhatsApp chat, and then someone has to copy it everywhere else: into the store admin so stock is right, into the sales spreadsheet, into the CRM, into the delivery list. On a busy evening that copying doesn't happen, and orders, addresses and payments end up living only in someone's phone.

The same gap runs the other way. Your website knows who left a cart, your calendar knows which slots are free and your payment provider knows who paid, but the person answering WhatsApp can't see any of it. Without the connections, follow-ups go to the wrong people and reports never add up.

What it looks like

One chat, three systems updated

Reem ordered in the chat and paid by card. The moment the payment came through, the order was created in your store with her address, a row was added to your sales sheet and your team got the order on WhatsApp. Nobody copied anything.

Because the assistant reads prices and stock from your store, it never quotes a product you've sold out of or a price you've changed. If Reem had asked for something outside your product list, your team would have got an instant WhatsApp alert with a summary of the chat.

n8n, Make or Zapier

When we use which, and why

M2S runs on OrcaFlo, our own platform, and most connections are built in. For tools without a direct connection, we use a workflow tool as the glue, and we pick the one that fits your setup rather than the one we resell.

Zapier suits simple, linear steps between popular cloud apps. Make handles branching and lists well, such as splitting one order into line items or routing a lead by source. n8n suits higher volumes, custom APIs or keeping data on your own infrastructure. If you already use one, we usually build there.

Connections tend to break months later, when an app renames a field or a token expires. We watch for failed runs as part of running M2S, so your team isn't debugging a workflow at the end of the month.

FAQ

Common questions about integrations

Do I need n8n, Make or Zapier to use M2S?

No. Most connections run directly through M2S. We only add n8n, Make or Zapier where it's the right glue for a tool you already use, and we can build on an account you already have.

Which online stores does M2S connect to?

Shopify connects directly, and other websites connect through our API. We connect your store during onboarding and confirm cart recovery is live for your store before it counts in your report.

Can orders from WhatsApp go into my CRM or Google Sheet?

Yes. When the assistant takes an order or books an appointment, we can add it to your store, a Google Sheet, your CRM or your calendar, so your team keeps working in the tools they know.

Are integrations priced separately?

No. Connecting your tools is part of M2S setup. We're not a separate workflow-building service, and we don't sell automation projects on their own.

Can you work with workflows someone else built?

Yes, where they're part of your sales flow. Tell us on the audit what exists today and what keeps breaking, and we'll include it in the setup plan.
