CRM automation and lead follow-up that moves every UAE lead towards a sale

Every lead your ads bring lands in a sales pipeline, tagged with the ad that started it, followed up on time and flagged to your team the moment it's ready to buy.

Short answer

CRM automation inside M2S means every lead from your Meta ads, WhatsApp and website chat is added to a sales pipeline automatically (New lead → Qualified → Checkout started → Ordered, or your own stages), tagged with the ad it came from, and moved along as the AI sales assistant qualifies it. Leads who go quiet get polite automatic follow-ups, and your team gets an instant WhatsApp alert for every hot lead. You don't need to buy a CRM, and if you already have one we connect it.

The problem

Where UAE businesses lose leads after the ad

Most lead problems aren't software problems. Leads from an Instagram ad go straight into a busy WhatsApp inbox and never get logged anywhere. A salesperson sees a new enquiry three hours later, replies once, and the lead is never touched again. Lead forms sit in a download nobody imports.

Most leads don't say no. They ask the price, say "let me think about it" and go quiet, and nobody chases them because there's no list of who is waiting. At the end of the month the owner can see what the ads cost, but not which ones brought leads that bought.

What it looks like

One pipeline for every lead your ads bring

Each card is a real conversation. As the assistant learns what the customer wants, the lead moves from New lead to Qualified, then to Checkout started when a payment link or booking is offered, and to Ordered when the sale is done. Nobody has to drag cards around or type notes.

When a lead is ready to buy or needs a person, your team gets a WhatsApp alert with a short summary of the chat, so whoever picks it up knows exactly where things stand. The assistant never invents prices or products: anything it can't answer goes to your team the same way.

Lead follow-up

Following up the leads who went quiet

A lead who asks the price and goes silent is usually busy, comparing, or waiting for payday. M2S follows up on a schedule, for example after 4 hours, 1 day and 3 days, with a message that picks up where the conversation stopped rather than a generic "just checking in".

The sequence stops the moment the lead replies, and the assistant carries on the conversation knowing what they asked about. Nothing goes out at night, anyone who says "stop" is never messaged again, and each lead gets at most one sequence a week. Every follow-up and reply is logged on the lead, so you can see who was chased and what happened.

  1. 4 hours
    Gentle nudge

  2. 1 day
    Helpful follow-up

  3. 3 days

  4. Reply
    Sequence stops

    The AI takes over and the lead moves on in the pipeline.

Good to know: you keep your own WhatsApp number and can still use the WhatsApp app. On the official WhatsApp API, messages sent more than 24 hours after the customer's last message use a Meta-approved template, which we set up for you.

FAQ

Common questions about pipelines and follow-up

Do I need a CRM already?

No. M2S includes a sales pipeline with your stages, so every lead has a place from the first message. If you already use HubSpot, Zoho or a Google Sheet, we can send leads and stage changes there as well.

Can I use my own pipeline stages?

Yes. The default is New lead, Qualified, Checkout started and Ordered, but we set up the stages your business actually uses, such as Viewing booked or Consultation done.

How do I know which ad a lead came from?

Every chat that starts from a click-to-WhatsApp ad is tagged with that ad. You see the tag on the lead in the pipeline, and the monthly report shows which ads brought leads that went on to buy.

How do hot-lead alerts work?

When a lead is ready to buy, asks for a person, or asks something the assistant can't answer, your team gets an instant WhatsApp alert with a summary of the chat and can take over the conversation.

Will automatic follow-ups annoy my leads?

No. Follow-ups stop the moment a customer buys or replies, never go out at night, stop for anyone who says "stop", and run once per customer per week.

How much does it cost?

M2S is one fixed monthly fee based on your ad budget, your channels and how many lead sources and pipelines we set up, quoted after a free audit. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta.
