Sales pipeline · UAE

CRM automation built around one thing: where each lead stands

Every chat your ads start becomes a lead in a pipeline. It moves stage by stage as the conversation goes, the right person is alerted at the right moment, and follow-ups run by rules instead of memory.

Short answer

CRM automation is software keeping your customer records and sales stages up to date without manual entry. In M2S, every WhatsApp chat becomes a lead that moves through four stages as the AI assistant talks to the customer: New lead, Qualified, Checkout started and Ordered, with staff alerts and follow-up rules attached to each.

The stages

Four stages, moved by the conversation

StageWithout itWhat happens

  1. New lead

    chats sit in a phone with no record.

    Created the moment someone messages, tagged with the ad that brought them.

  2. Qualified

    nobody knows who is serious.

    Moved here once the assistant has the answers you care about, such as what they want, quantity, area or date.

  3. Checkout started

    payment links are sent and forgotten.

    A payment link, cash-on-delivery order or booking is in progress, and follow-up rules start watching it.

  4. Ordered

    sales are counted by guesswork.

    Paid, confirmed or booked. The sale is reported back to Meta and counted in your monthly report.

Routing and alerts

Who gets told, and when

A pipeline is only useful if the right person acts on it. We agree routing rules with you: by product, area, language or the ad that started the chat. When a lead needs a person, that person gets a WhatsApp alert with a summary of the chat, not a bare notification.

Typical triggers are a qualified lead asking for something outside the assistant's information, a large order, or a checkout that's been started but not finished.

What your team sees

  • Each lead's stage, source ad and last message
  • A short summary of the chat so far
  • Which leads are waiting on a person
  • Which checkouts were started but not finished
  • Follow-ups due, sent or stopped

Follow-up rules

Rules, not reminders on sticky notes

Follow-ups are tied to stages. A lead who went quiet at Qualified gets a different message from one who left at Checkout started. Each is written around what the customer actually asked.

  • Stops the moment the customer replies or buys
  • Never sends during quiet hours at night
  • Stops for good when someone says stop
  • At most one sequence per customer per week

Already use a CRM? We can send leads and stage changes to it as well; see integrations.

Lead source

Every lead carries the ad that started it

Each chat is tagged with the click-to-WhatsApp ad that opened it, and the tag stays with the lead through every stage. When a lead reaches Ordered, the sale is reported back to Meta through the pixel and Conversions API, so campaigns learn from buyers rather than from clicks.

Once a month, the M2S Results report brings it together: how many leads each ad produced, how far they got in the pipeline, and which ones ended in a sale or a recovered checkout.

FAQ

Questions about the sales pipeline

What are the pipeline stages?

New lead, Qualified, Checkout started and Ordered. Leads move between them automatically as the assistant talks to the customer, and your team can see each lead's stage.

How are leads routed to the right person?

By rules we agree with you, such as product, area, language or the ad that started the chat. The chosen person gets a WhatsApp alert with a summary of the chat.

Do we have to replace our current CRM?

No. M2S has its own pipeline, and where you already use a CRM we can send leads and stage changes to it as well.

When does a follow-up stop?

As soon as the customer replies or buys, or says stop. None go out at night, and no customer gets more than one sequence a week.
