About us

We run the ads, and we own what happens after the click

Nuevo Automations is a UAE marketing agency. Most agencies stop at the click. We built M2S, our Message to Sale system, so the messages your ads bring are answered in seconds, closed in the chat and followed up politely if they go quiet.

Who we are

An agency that builds its own tools

Nuevo Automations was founded by Nawaf Allahham and registered in September 2026 as Nuevo Automations FZE LLC, a free zone establishment in Sharjah Publishing City Free Zone. We work with businesses across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and the rest of the UAE, in English and Arabic.

M2S runs on OrcaFlo, the WhatsApp AI platform we built and run ourselves. That means when something needs changing, from how the assistant answers to how follow-ups are timed, we change it directly instead of waiting on a third-party tool.

We use these systems ourselves too. Khaleej Peptides, an online store owned by our founder and one of the partners on this site, sells through the same WhatsApp setup.
