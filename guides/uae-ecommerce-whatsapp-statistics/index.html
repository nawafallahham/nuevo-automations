UAE e-commerce, social media and WhatsApp statistics, 2026

The numbers we use when planning campaigns for UAE brands, each with its source and date. We only include figures we could trace to the publisher; where a popular statistic didn't check out, we say so at the end.

By Nawaf Allahham, founder of Nuevo Automations · Updated 28 November 2026

The UAE had 12.06 million residents at the end of 2025, according to the Federal Competitiveness and Statistics Centre. DataReportal's October 2026 data puts internet use at 99% and mobile connections at 24.9 million, and 84.1% of internet users aged 16 and over used WhatsApp in the past month (GWI, Q2 2026). EZDubai and Euromonitor value UAE e-commerce at AED 42.2 billion in 2025, about 15.7% of retail, forecast to reach about AED 67.2 billion by 2030. Visa reports that 55% of domestic payment volume is now online or in-app, and Checkout.com that cash on delivery use in the UAE is down 53% since 2020.

Payments

  • Online share of card payments: 55% of domestic Visa payment volume in the UAE is online or in-app, up from 35% in 2019 (Visa, October 2026).
  • Cash on delivery: use in the UAE is down 53% since 2020 (Checkout.com, 2025). Across the region, cash on delivery preference halved from 41% to 20% (Checkout.com, 2024).
  • Digital wallets: 67% of UAE consumers use one at least monthly to buy, budget or manage money (Checkout.com, May 2026).
  • Checkout friction: 32% of UAE consumers have abandoned a cart over payment security concerns (Checkout.com, May 2026).
  • Cash in daily life: 80% of payments are made digitally, and only 16% of consumers mainly use cash for everyday purchases (Visa, January 2026).
  • Buy now, pay later: Tabby and Tamara repayment data has been added to UAE credit reports from July 2026 (Etihad Credit Bureau, via Gulf News).

Messaging and conversational commerce

  • Businesses: 55% of large UAE organisations say rich messaging such as WhatsApp will be their top investment channel over the next five years, against 13% for email (BCG and Meta enterprise survey, April 2026).
  • Consumers, global: 73.3% of people prefer messaging when contacting a business, and 72.4% are more likely to buy from brands that offer it, across 22 markets including the UAE (Meta and Kantar, 2025 fieldwork). These are global averages, not UAE-only figures.
  • Consumers, UAE: 85% of UAE residents want businesses to offer WhatsApp for customer support, and 65% used WhatsApp to ask a business about a product in the past year (Zbooni and YouGov, 2024; vendor-commissioned).

What these numbers mean for a UAE brand

  • Nearly every customer is online and on WhatsApp, so the question isn't whether to sell there but how fast you answer.
  • Mobile and social shopping are mainstream, which suits ads that open a chat rather than a desktop checkout. See our click-to-WhatsApp ads guide.
  • Cash on delivery is shrinking but still present, so offer a payment link in the chat and confirm COD orders.

Statistics we left out

  • UAE supplements market size: the figures circulating online either come from vendor models or couldn't be traced to the publisher. Euromonitor's own reports describe steady growth without public values.
  • "WhatsApp is used by 78%+ of the UAE" and "customers expect a reply within 5 minutes": we found these only on blogs that name no source.
  • Year-on-year e-commerce growth from different editions of the EZDubai report, which may not use the same series.

Common questions

What is the population of the UAE in 2026?

The latest official figure is 12,063,953 at the end of 2025, up 7% on 2024, according to the Federal Competitiveness and Statistics Centre.

How many people in the UAE use WhatsApp?

84.1% of internet users aged 16 and over used WhatsApp in the past month, the highest of any platform, according to GWI survey data for Q2 2026 published by DataReportal. Internet use in the UAE is about 99%.

How big is e-commerce in the UAE?

EZDubai and Euromonitor value the UAE e-commerce market at AED 42.2 billion (US$11.5 billion) in 2025, about 15.7% of retail sales, forecast to reach about AED 67.2 billion by 2030.

Is cash on delivery still popular in the UAE?

It is declining. Checkout.com reported in 2025 that cash on delivery use in the UAE was down 53% since 2020, and Visa reports that 55% of domestic card payment volume is now online or in-app.

How many Instagram users are there in the UAE?

Instagram's advertising reach in the UAE was 8.90 million in October 2026, according to DataReportal. Ad reach isn't the same as monthly active users.
