Shopify WhatsApp integration: the options in 2026

Shopify now has WhatsApp built in, Meta has its own Shopify app, and there are dozens of third-party apps. They don't do the same job. Here is what each one covers for a UAE store, with the prices listed on 28 November 2026.

By Nawaf Allahham, founder of Nuevo Automations · Updated 28 November 2026

There are three ways to connect Shopify to WhatsApp in 2026. Shopify Messaging, built into Shopify, sends WhatsApp marketing campaigns and abandoned cart, checkout and browse automations, with an opt-in at checkout; in the UAE it charges US$0.10 per marketing message. Meta's official WhatsApp app for Shopify is free to install and sends order updates and abandoned cart messages, with Meta billing messages separately. Third-party apps such as Zoko, Interakt, Wati, SuperLemon and Gallabox add a shared team inbox, cash on delivery confirmation and AI chat. Shopify Inbox, Shopify's chat tool, does not connect to WhatsApp.

Option 1: Shopify Messaging (built in)

Shopify added WhatsApp to Shopify Messaging, the tool formerly called Shopify Email, on 29 July 2026, and added WhatsApp marketing automations on 28 October 2026.

  • What it does: campaigns (back in stock, upcoming sale, product showcase, VIP offers) and pre-built abandoned browse, cart and checkout automations.
  • Consent: you switch on a WhatsApp marketing opt-in that shows at checkout, and you can import existing consent by CSV. Messages only go to subscribed customers, and every message carries a STOP line you can't remove.
  • UAE pricing: US$0.100 per marketing message and US$0.0157 per service message, with 1,000 free service messages a month, billed on your Shopify invoice.
  • Limits: it is a marketing tool. Shopify's documentation doesn't cover order confirmations or shipping updates, new accounts are capped at 2,000 messages a day until Meta raises the limit, and if your number is already connected to another WhatsApp provider you must disconnect it first.

Option 2: Meta's official WhatsApp app for Shopify

Meta publishes its own app on the Shopify App Store, launched in July 2025. It was rated 4.4 from 1,002 reviews on 28 November 2026, is free to install, and sends automated order updates and abandoned cart messages in several languages including Arabic. Meta bills the messages directly. It is a notification tool, not a team inbox or a chatbot.

Option 3: third-party WhatsApp apps

These apps run on the WhatsApp Business API and add a shared inbox, automations and, on some plans, AI. Prices are the app's own Shopify App Store listing on 28 November 2026; WhatsApp message charges are extra.

Which messages Meta counts as marketing

This decides both the price and who you can message. Meta's template rules say:

  • Order confirmations and shipping updates are utility messages, as long as they contain no promotion.
  • Abandoned cart reminders are marketing, "even if requested by users", in Meta's words. They need marketing consent and are priced as marketing.
  • A template that mixes order details with a discount or product suggestion counts as marketing. Since April 2025 Meta automatically recategorises utility templates that look like marketing.

Shopify itself treats a checkout as abandoned once it stays incomplete for more than 10 minutes after the customer gives an email address. You can trigger WhatsApp flows from that with Shopify Flow or the checkout webhooks.

What about Zid and Salla?

Zid has WhatsApp built in, with abandoned cart reminders, nine order status notifications and back-in-stock alerts, included in its Professional plan or SAR 109 a month otherwise. Salla relies on third-party WhatsApp apps from its app store.

Which one should you pick?

  • Just order updates: Meta's free app is enough.
  • Campaigns and cart recovery, no inbox: Shopify Messaging keeps billing and consent in Shopify.
  • Customers who ask questions before buying, which is most supplement and wellness buyers: you need an inbox and someone, or an AI, answering in seconds. That is where a third-party platform or a service like M2S comes in.

Common questions

Does Shopify have WhatsApp built in?

Yes, since July 2026. Shopify Messaging can send WhatsApp marketing campaigns and, since October 2026, abandoned cart, checkout and browse automations. In the UAE it costs US$0.10 per marketing message.

Does Shopify Inbox work with WhatsApp?

No. Shopify Inbox is a chat tool for your online store; its help pages don't mention WhatsApp. For WhatsApp conversations you need Shopify Messaging for campaigns, or a WhatsApp app with an inbox.

Is there a free WhatsApp app for Shopify?

Meta's official WhatsApp app is free to install, and Meta bills each message separately. SuperLemon also has a free plan. Every option still pays Meta's per-message fees.

Are abandoned cart messages on WhatsApp utility or marketing?

Marketing. Meta's template guidelines list cart reminders as marketing even when the customer asked for them, so you need marketing consent and pay the marketing rate.

Can I use my existing WhatsApp number with Shopify?

Usually yes, through the WhatsApp Business API. If the number is already connected to another WhatsApp provider, Shopify Messaging requires you to disconnect it first.
