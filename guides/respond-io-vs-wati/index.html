Respond.io vs Wati: which WhatsApp platform fits a UAE brand?

Respond.io and Wati are two of the platforms UAE businesses most often use to run WhatsApp on the official API. Both add a team inbox, automation and AI on top of Meta. Here is how they compare in November 2026, using each company's own pricing and help pages.

By Nawaf Allahham, founder of Nuevo Automations · Updated 27 November 2026

Wati is cheaper to start: Growth is $59 a month billed annually with 3 users, and it has strong e-commerce integrations including Shopify, WooCommerce, Zoho CRM and Klaviyo. Respond.io starts at $79 a month annually ($99 monthly) with 5 users, covers more channels (WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, TikTok, Telegram, LINE, Viber, WeChat, email, SMS and more), and states that it does not mark up Meta's WhatsApp rates. Wati shows its message rates only after login. Both offer 7-day free trials and AI agents. Pick Wati for a Shopify-led store on a budget; pick Respond.io for a multi-channel team that wants transparent message pricing.

Wati's page lists annual prices only and says annual billing saves up to 20%. Respond.io's contact and user limits were laid out ambiguously on its page; confirm them with sales before you sign.

WhatsApp message fees and markups

Both platforms pass on Meta's per-message fees. The difference is transparency:

  • Respond.io says WhatsApp fees aren't included in any subscription and that it does not mark up WhatsApp's official rates.
  • Wati shows its per-message rates inside the account (Wallet, then Pricing Overview), not on the public pricing page, and doesn't state publicly whether it adds a margin.

At low volume the difference is small. If you send tens of thousands of cart reminders or broadcasts a month, ask Wati for its UAE rates in writing and compare them with Meta's rate card.

AI

  • Wati: AI agents ("Astra") on every plan: 1 on Growth, 2 on Pro, unlimited on Business, with 2,000 to 20,000 AI credits a month. Extra credits are $25 per 2,000.
  • Respond.io: AI agents from the Growth plan up, with 5,000 to 40,000 AI credits a month depending on plan. Extra credits are $15 per 1,000.

Credits run out faster than you'd expect once an AI answers every chat, so price the plan at your real volume.

Channels

Respond.io is built as a multi-channel inbox: WhatsApp (including calling), Instagram, Messenger, TikTok, Telegram, LINE, Viber, WeChat, RCS, email, SMS, webchat and VoIP. Wati centres on WhatsApp and adds Instagram, Facebook Messenger, TikTok, webchat and RCS. For most UAE e-commerce brands, WhatsApp and Instagram are what matter, and both cover them.

Integrations

  • Wati: Shopify (an add-on at $4.99 a month on some plans), WooCommerce, Zoho CRM, Klaviyo, Zapier and Make; HubSpot from Pro, Salesforce on Business.
  • Respond.io: HubSpot and Salesforce directly (contacts sync into respond.io); WooCommerce through Zapier; Shopify only as a webchat widget; Zoho not listed.

For a Shopify or WooCommerce store that wants order data in its WhatsApp flows, Wati's native store integrations are the stronger fit. For a sales team living in HubSpot or Salesforce, either works.

Data hosting

Wati says each customer gets its own database with backups on Google Cloud across several regions, and offers dedicated hosting in a location you choose for $1,000 a month. Respond.io's pricing page mentions ISO 27001 certification but doesn't state where data is hosted. If you handle health data, ask both where it's stored.

Which to pick

  • Wati if you're a small Shopify or WooCommerce store, WhatsApp is your main channel, and budget matters most.
  • Respond.io if your team answers several channels in one inbox, you want published, no-markup message pricing, or you need more users per plan.
  • Neither, on its own, will run your ads or close your sales. A platform is the tool; you still need the flows, the AI trained on your products, and someone watching the results.

Common questions

Is Wati cheaper than Respond.io?

At the entry level, yes: Wati Growth is $59 a month billed annually, Respond.io Starter is $79 annually or $99 monthly. Respond.io includes more users per plan, so for a larger team the gap narrows.

Does Respond.io charge extra for WhatsApp messages?

Respond.io says it passes on WhatsApp's official rates without a markup. Meta's message fees are billed on top of the subscription.

Does Wati integrate with Shopify?

Yes. Wati lists a Shopify integration, sold as an add-on at $4.99 a month on some plans, plus WooCommerce, Zoho CRM and Klaviyo.

Do both platforms have AI agents?

Yes. Wati includes AI agents on every plan; Respond.io includes them from the Growth plan up. Both sell AI credits, with monthly allowances that differ by plan.

Can I try them before paying?

Both offer a 7-day free trial. Respond.io's trial needs no card and includes Growth features for 5 users and 1,000 contacts.
