WhatsApp Business API pricing in the UAE, explained for 2026

Meta charges per delivered message, by message type and by the customer's country. Here is how that works for a UAE business, what changed on 1 October 2026, and how to estimate your own bill.

By Nawaf Allahham, founder of Nuevo Automations · Updated 26 November 2026

Since 1 July 2025, Meta bills the WhatsApp Business API per delivered message, not per conversation. The price depends on the message category (marketing, utility, authentication or service) and on the country code of the customer who receives it. The UAE is its own market on Meta's rate card. From 1 October 2026, each business number gets 1,000 free service messages a month and pays for service messages beyond that. Chats that start from a click-to-WhatsApp ad open a free entry point window in which Meta doesn't charge for messages.

How Meta bills WhatsApp messages now

Meta moved the WhatsApp Business Platform to per-message pricing on 1 July 2025. You pay only for messages your business sends that are actually delivered. Messages your customers send you are never charged.

Every message falls into a category, and each category has its own price in each market:

CategoryWhat it's forExample
Marketing templateOffers, launches, reminders that aim to sellAn abandoned-cart reminder, a restock alert
Utility templateUpdates about something the customer already didOrder confirmed, out for delivery
Authentication templateOne-time codesLogin or checkout code
Service messageFree-form replies while the 24-hour customer-service window is openAnswering "do you deliver to Al Ain?"
Meta Business AgentReplies written by Meta's own AI agentBilled by tokens, separately

Meta decides the category when it approves a template, and it can reclassify a "utility" template that is really marketing. Writing a sales message as a utility template to save money rarely works.

The rate depends on your customer's country, not yours

Meta prices each message by the country code of the person receiving it. A UAE business messaging a +971 number pays the UAE rate, but the same business messaging a customer in Saudi Arabia or India pays that country's rate. The UAE is listed as a standalone market on Meta's rate card, and Meta's pricing documentation lists the dirham (AED) as a billing currency from April 2026.

Rates change several times a year, so we don't print them here. The current numbers are on Meta's WhatsApp pricing page; pick "United Arab Emirates" and your billing currency.

What changed on 1 October 2026

  • Service messages are no longer unlimited and free. Each business phone number gets 1,000 free service messages a month. Unused messages don't roll over. After that, service messages are charged. Meta keeps them free beyond the tier only for eligible government and non-profit accounts.
  • Utility templates sent inside an open customer-service window are charged again. They had been free inside the window since July 2025.
  • No payment method, no messages past the tier. If your WhatsApp Business account has no payment method, Meta still delivers service messages within the 1,000 free ones and stops delivering them after that.

For a busy store this matters: a sales chat that runs to fifteen replies now uses fifteen service messages. Most small brands stay inside or near the free tier; brands running ads at volume do not.

The free entry point window after a click-to-WhatsApp ad

When a customer taps a click-to-WhatsApp ad (or a Facebook Page's WhatsApp button) on their phone and you reply within 24 hours, Meta opens a free entry point window. While it's open, Meta does not charge for marketing, utility, authentication or service messages to that customer. Meta Business Agent replies are still charged.

Meta's developer documentation says the window can stay open for up to 7 days; its marketing pages still describe 72 hours. Plan on the shorter number. The window only opens for chats started on Android or iOS, not from desktop or WhatsApp Web.

This is one reason click-to-WhatsApp ads suit UAE brands: the whole first sales conversation, including a follow-up template a day later, can cost nothing in message fees. Read our guide to click-to-WhatsApp ads for the rest.

Other costs besides Meta's message fees

  1. A platform or provider. The API has no inbox of its own. You use a Business Solution Provider or a platform built on Meta's Cloud API, and most charge a monthly fee, sometimes a markup per message.
  2. AI replies. If an AI assistant answers, someone pays for the model, either inside the platform fee or as Meta Business Agent tokens (Meta's own estimate is roughly 4–5 US cents a message at typical lengths).
  3. Setup. Connecting your number, writing and getting templates approved, linking your store and training the assistant.
  4. Ad spend, if chats come from ads. That's paid to Meta separately.

How to estimate your own monthly bill

Take last month's numbers and multiply by the current UAE rate for each line:

  1. Marketing templates: cart reminders, offers and re-engagement messages sent outside a free window.
  2. Utility templates: order and delivery updates.
  3. Service messages: total replies your team or assistant sends, minus 1,000 per number, minus replies inside free entry point windows.
  4. Authentication: one-time codes, if you send them on WhatsApp.

Add the platform fee and you have a realistic monthly number. Customers outside the UAE use their own country's rate.

Five ways to keep the bill down without losing sales

  • Reply fast, so the conversation stays inside the 24-hour window and you don't need a template to restart it.
  • Send ad traffic to WhatsApp from phones, where the free entry point window applies.
  • Write one clear follow-up instead of three; each template costs money and patience.
  • Stop sequences the moment a customer replies or buys, and never message people who said stop.
  • Keep order updates as genuine utility messages and sales messages as marketing; misfiled templates get reclassified.

Sources: Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform pricing documentation and pricing updates, read on 26 November 2026. Meta changes pricing often; check the source before you budget.

Common questions

Is the WhatsApp Business app free?

Yes. The free WhatsApp Business app on your phone has no per-message charges. Per-message pricing applies to the WhatsApp Business API (the Business Platform), which you need for an AI assistant, team inboxes, ad tracking and scheduled follow-ups.

Do I pay when a customer messages my business?

No. Meta only charges for messages your business sends that are delivered. Messages customers send you are free.

Which country's rate applies to a UAE business?

The rate of the country code of the customer receiving the message. Messages to +971 numbers use the UAE rate; messages to customers abroad use their country's rate.

Are replies to customers still free after October 2026?

Each business number gets 1,000 free service messages a month. Beyond that, service messages are charged at Meta's published rate, except for eligible government and non-profit accounts.

How long is the free window after a click-to-WhatsApp ad?

Meta's developer documentation says the free entry point window can stay open for up to 7 days once you reply within 24 hours; its marketing pages say 72 hours. During it, Meta doesn't charge for template or service messages to that customer.
