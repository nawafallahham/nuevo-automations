Arabic WhatsApp chatbots: what works in the UAE

Most chatbot platforms say they "support Arabic". Your customers don't write textbook Arabic, though. They write Gulf dialect, Arabic in Latin letters, and English and Arabic in the same message. Here is what that means for a WhatsApp chatbot, and how to test one properly.

By Nawaf Allahham, founder of Nuevo Automations · Updated 28 November 2026

An Arabic WhatsApp chatbot for the UAE has to handle three kinds of writing: Gulf and Emirati dialect, Arabizi (Arabic written in Latin letters and numbers, like "7abibi"), and messages that mix Arabic and English. Published research shows AI models still do worse in Arabic dialects than in English: in the 2025 DialectalArabicMMLU benchmark, 19 open models averaged about 48% accuracy in dialects, 52% in Modern Standard Arabic and 63% in English. Templates need the right Arabic language code (such as ar_AE) and testing for right-to-left layout. The best test is to send the bot real messages from your own customers before launch.

Three ways UAE customers write Arabic

  • Gulf and Emirati dialect. Everyday words and grammar differ from Modern Standard Arabic, the formal Arabic most AI models are trained on.
  • Arabizi. Arabic typed in Latin letters, with numbers for sounds English lacks: 3 for ع, 7 for ح, 5 for خ. Common on phones.
  • Code-switching. English and Arabic in one message: "Hi, عندكم vitamin D 5000؟ how much". A 2025 academic survey of code-switched Arabic calls this widespread and says handling it is vital for language technology.

A bot that only handles formal Arabic will answer some customers in a register that sounds stiff, and misunderstand others.

What the research says about AI and Arabic dialects

  • DialectalArabicMMLU (IBM, NYU Abu Dhabi and MBZUAI, 2025) asked the same questions in five dialects, Modern Standard Arabic and English. Across 19 open models, average accuracy was about 47.7% in dialects, 51.9% in Modern Standard Arabic and 62.8% in English. Emirati scored highest of the dialects, at 49.8%.
  • AraDiCE (Qatar Computing Research Institute, 2025) found that Arabic-focused models such as Jais and AceGPT beat general multilingual models on dialect tasks, and that models are better at understanding dialects than writing them.
  • The Open Arabic LLM Leaderboard mostly measures Modern Standard Arabic, so a high score there says little about Gulf dialect.
  • We found no published benchmark for Gulf Arabizi or Gulf Arabic-English mixing in chat. Nobody can tell you from a leaderboard how a bot will handle your customers; you have to test it.

The UAE is also building its own models: the Technology Innovation Institute released Falcon Arabic in May 2025 and models trained on the Emirati dialect in October 2026.

Right-to-left templates on WhatsApp

Messages you send outside the 24-hour window use Meta-approved templates, and each language is its own version. Meta offers several Arabic language codes, including ar and ar_AE; it doesn't translate for you. Two practical points:

  • English product names, prices and order numbers inside an Arabic sentence can appear in the wrong order on screen. Put variables at natural break points and preview on a real phone.
  • Variables must be numbered in order and a template can't start or end with one, in any language.

What the main platforms say

On their own websites in November 2026, Respond.io offers a full Arabic interface for agents, Wati demonstrates Arabic voice replies, and Interakt has an Arabic marketing site. None of them makes a written claim about Gulf dialect accuracy for its AI. Meta's AI agent for WhatsApp Business became available globally in June 2026, without published language details. Treat any "supports Arabic" claim as something to verify.

How to test an Arabic chatbot before launch

  1. Collect 50 real customer messages from your WhatsApp history, in every style above.
  2. Send each one to the bot and check three things: did it understand, is the answer correct, and does it sound like a person from here?
  3. Check it replies in the language the customer used, and doesn't switch to formal Arabic for a casual question.
  4. Try the hard ones: prices, stock, delivery to a specific area, a complaint. A good bot hands these to a person when unsure rather than guessing.
  5. Send your templates to yourself and read them on both iPhone and Android.

Common questions

Can a WhatsApp chatbot reply in Arabic?

Yes. AI chatbots on the WhatsApp Business API can reply in Arabic and English. How well they handle Gulf dialect, Arabizi and mixed messages varies a lot, so test with real customer messages.

What is Arabizi?

Arabic written in Latin letters, with numbers standing in for Arabic sounds, for example 3 for ع and 7 for ح. Many people in the UAE type this way on their phones, so a chatbot should understand it.

Is AI worse in Arabic than in English?

On published benchmarks, yes. In the 2025 DialectalArabicMMLU study, 19 open models averaged about 63% accuracy in English, 52% in Modern Standard Arabic and 48% in Arabic dialects.

Which Arabic language code should UAE templates use?

Meta offers several Arabic codes, including ar and ar_AE. Pick one and keep it consistent, then supply your own Arabic text; Meta doesn't translate templates.

Should the chatbot reply in formal Arabic or dialect?

Match the customer. Formal Arabic is safe for templates and policies, but a casual question in Gulf dialect reads better with a natural, simple reply.
