Wati alternatives for UAE businesses in 2026

Wati is one of the best-known WhatsApp Business API platforms, but it isn't the only option, and for some UAE teams it isn't the cheapest. Here are the main alternatives with the prices on their own pricing pages on 28 November 2026.

By Nawaf Allahham, founder of Nuevo Automations · Updated 28 November 2026

The main Wati alternatives in 2026 are Respond.io (from $79 a month billed annually, 5 users), SleekFlow (from AED 399 a month billed annually, 10 users, with a DIFC office), Gallabox (from AED 412 a month billed annually, 3 users, with a Dubai office), Interakt (from $55 a month), Zoko (from $49.99 a month, no markup on Meta fees), AiSensy (from $45 a month), Twilio (no subscription, $0.005 per message on top of Meta's fees), Trengo, Callbell and Kommo. For comparison, Wati's Growth plan is $59 a month billed annually for 3 users. Meta's own message fees are extra on all of them.

Wati for reference

Wati's pricing page lists Growth at $59 a month, Pro at $119 and Business at $279, billed annually. Growth includes 3 users and Pro and Business 5, with extra users at $39 or $89 each. AI agents and AI credits are included on every plan. Wati doesn't state on its pricing page whether it adds a margin to Meta's message fees; a third-party analysis of its October 2026 rate card estimated about 1.6 times Meta's marketing rate.

Which alternative fits which team

  • You want someone local: SleekFlow (DIFC) and Gallabox (Sheikh Zayed Road) list Dubai offices on their contact pages. We didn't find a UAE office for Wati or Respond.io.
  • A bigger team on one inbox: SleekFlow includes 10 seats and Respond.io 5; Interakt, Zoko and AiSensy don't charge per user.
  • Shopify store: Zoko has the highest rating of the WhatsApp apps on the Shopify App Store (4.9 from 486 reviews). See our Shopify WhatsApp guide.
  • Lowest message costs at volume: Zoko and Twilio are clear that Meta's fees pass through, with their own small charge on top.
  • You have developers: Twilio gives you the API without a subscription, and you build the inbox and AI yourself.

What none of them does for you

Every platform here is software. Someone still has to write the answers the AI gives, load your products and prices, set up and tune the follow-ups, run the ads that start the chats, and check every week what the AI got wrong. If you would rather not do that in-house, that's the work M2S covers.

Common questions

What is the best Wati alternative in the UAE?

It depends on the team. SleekFlow and Gallabox have Dubai offices, Respond.io includes 5 users on its entry plan, and Zoko and Twilio pass Meta's message fees through without a markup. Compare total monthly cost, including messages and users.

Is there a cheaper alternative to Wati?

On entry price, AiSensy ($45 a month), Zoko ($49.99) and Interakt ($55) list lower prices than Wati's Growth plan ($59 a month billed annually). Twilio has no subscription at all but needs developers.

Do WhatsApp platforms mark up Meta's message fees?

Some do. Zoko says you pay Meta directly, Twilio adds $0.005 per message, and Interakt and Gallabox mention reduced or no markup only on enterprise plans. Ask each provider for its UAE per-message rate in writing.

Can I move my WhatsApp number from Wati to another platform?

Yes. A WhatsApp Business API number can be migrated between providers. Plan for a short changeover, and note that templates may need to be recreated.

Which WhatsApp platforms have an office in Dubai?

SleekFlow lists an office in DIFC and Gallabox one on Sheikh Zayed Road. We didn't find a UAE office for Wati or Respond.io in November 2026.
