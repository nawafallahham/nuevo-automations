Confirming cash on delivery orders on WhatsApp in the UAE

Cash on delivery is shrinking in the UAE but hasn't gone away, and unconfirmed COD orders are a classic source of failed deliveries. A short WhatsApp confirmation before dispatch is one of the simplest fixes. Here is how to do it within Meta's rules.

By Nawaf Allahham, founder of Nuevo Automations · Updated 28 November 2026

To confirm cash on delivery orders on WhatsApp, send an approved utility template right after the order with the order number, items, total and delivery address, and buttons to confirm or change the order. Meta counts it as a utility message only if it contains no promotion; adding an offer or upsell makes it marketing, and Meta relabels such templates automatically. Checkout.com reported that cash on delivery preference in the region fell from 41% in 2020 to 20% in 2023, and as low as 10% in the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Kuwait, so a confirmation message is also a good moment to offer a payment link.

Why confirm COD orders at all

A cash on delivery order costs you the delivery whether or not the customer pays at the door. Orders placed by mistake, duplicates, wrong addresses and buyers who changed their mind all show up as failed deliveries. Asking the customer to confirm in WhatsApp before you dispatch catches most of these, and the customer gets a chance to fix the address.

What the data says about COD in the region

  • Checkout.com reported in 2024 that cash on delivery preference across the region fell from 41% in 2020 to 20% in 2023, and to as low as 10% in the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Kuwait.
  • Its 2025 report, as covered by Khaleej Times, said COD usage had fallen 53% since 2020.
  • EZDubai and Euromonitor put UAE e-commerce at AED 42.2 billion in 2025, about 15.7% of retail, as reported by WAM.

We found no reliable published figure for COD return rates in the UAE, so we don't quote one.

Stay inside Meta's utility rules

  • An order confirmation is a utility template only if it is about the order and nothing else.
  • Add a discount, "you may also like" or any promotion and it becomes marketing, priced as marketing and subject to marketing consent. Meta relabels misfiled templates automatically, and repeat misuse leads to restrictions.
  • Since 1 October 2026 utility templates are charged even inside an open conversation window, so keep it to one confirmation message.

A simple confirmation flow

  1. Order placed: a utility template with the order number, items, total including VAT, delivery area and expected date, and two buttons: Confirm order, Change something.
  2. Customer taps Confirm: a 24-hour conversation window is now open. Reply with a thank-you and, if you like, a payment link for those who would rather pay now.
  3. Customer taps Change: your team or AI assistant fixes the address or quantity in the chat.
  4. No reply after a few hours: one reminder, then a call or hold the order, whichever your logistics prefer.
  5. Dispatched: a second utility template with the tracking link.

Moving buyers from COD to prepaid

Once the customer replies, you're inside the conversation window and can offer a payment link, including buy now, pay later options where your provider supports them. Keep it optional: buyers who prefer cash will still say yes to the order.

On Shopify, apps such as Interakt and SuperLemon list COD confirmation among their features; see our Shopify WhatsApp guide.

Common questions

Is a COD confirmation message on WhatsApp utility or marketing?

Utility, if it only covers the order: number, items, total, address and confirm or change buttons. Add any offer or upsell and Meta treats it as marketing.

How common is cash on delivery in the UAE now?

Falling. Checkout.com reported that cash on delivery preference was as low as 10% in the UAE, Saudi Arabia and Kuwait in 2023, down from much higher levels in 2020.

Can I ask COD customers to pay online instead?

Yes. Once the customer replies to the confirmation, a 24-hour conversation window opens and you can send a payment link, keeping cash on delivery as an option.

How much does a WhatsApp COD confirmation cost?

Meta charges the utility rate for the customer's country for each delivered template. Since 1 October 2026 utility templates are charged even inside an open conversation window.

Can a Shopify store automate COD confirmation on WhatsApp?

Yes. Several Shopify WhatsApp apps, including Interakt and SuperLemon, list cash on delivery confirmation among their features.
