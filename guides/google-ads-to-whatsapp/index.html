Google Ads to WhatsApp: how to start chats from search ads

Meta ads can open WhatsApp directly. Google Ads can too, with more conditions. Here is what Google's own help pages say in November 2026, and how to measure the chats your search ads start.

By Nawaf Allahham, founder of Nuevo Automations · Updated 28 November 2026

Google Ads can start WhatsApp chats through message assets, a beta feature that adds a WhatsApp button to Search and Performance Max ads. Google requires advertiser verification, a good policy history and an eligible industry, and the WhatsApp link must be verified, either by showing it on the website you advertise or by proving you own the domain. Once approved, a Leads from Messages conversion goal becomes available. Where message assets aren't available, the usual route is a landing page on your own domain with a clear WhatsApp button, tracked with GA4's outbound click events imported into Google Ads.

Option 1: message assets (beta)

  • A message asset adds a WhatsApp (or SMS, Messenger or Zalo) button to your Search or Performance Max ads.
  • Requirements: advertiser verification, a good policy history and an eligible industry.
  • Link verification: the WhatsApp number or link must appear on the website you advertise, on the same domain as the ad, or you must prove you own the domain.
  • Measurement: once approved, Google offers a Leads from Messages conversion goal.
  • Trade-off: while the message button shows, other assets such as calls and lead forms don't show with it.

Google's help pages don't list the countries where message assets are available, and the feature is still in beta, so check eligibility inside your account.

Option 2: a landing page with a WhatsApp button

Google requires an ad's destination to match its domain without redirecting elsewhere, so sending a search ad straight to a wa.me link is risky. The reliable route is a fast landing page on your own domain that answers the search and offers WhatsApp as the main action. That page also carries the price, delivery details and trust signals a searcher wants before they message.

Tracking WhatsApp leads in Google Ads

  1. Clicks: GA4 records clicks on outbound links, including wa.me links, automatically. Mark the click as a key event and import it into Google Ads. A click isn't a message sent, so treat it as a soft conversion.
  2. Leads: when the chat becomes a real lead, record it with the Google click ID (GCLID) captured on the landing page, and send it to Google with enhanced conversions for leads.
  3. Sales: upload sales from the chat as offline conversions with the order value. Since 15 June 2026, Google takes these uploads through its Data Manager API.

The same idea applies on Meta, where chat sales go back through the Conversions API. See tracking sales from WhatsApp ads.

Search or Meta for WhatsApp leads?

  • Google search catches people already looking: "vitamin D delivery Dubai", "collagen powder UAE". Intent is high, volume is limited by what people search.
  • Meta creates demand among people who weren't searching, and opens WhatsApp directly with a free messaging window.

Many brands use both. M2S answers the chats from either in seconds; see how it works.

Sources: Google Ads Help articles on message assets, destination requirements, lead form assets, enhanced conversions for leads and offline conversion imports; GA4 help on enhanced measurement outbound clicks. Read 28 November 2026.

Common questions

Can Google Ads open WhatsApp directly?

Yes, through message assets, a beta feature that adds a WhatsApp button to Search and Performance Max ads. It needs advertiser verification, an eligible industry and a verified WhatsApp link.

Can I use a wa.me link as my Google Ads final URL?

Google requires an ad's destination to match the ad's domain without redirecting to another domain, so a wa.me link as the final URL is risky. Use a landing page on your own domain with a WhatsApp button.

How do I track WhatsApp clicks from Google Ads?

GA4 records outbound link clicks, including wa.me links. Mark them as a key event and import them into Google Ads, then record real leads and sales with the Google click ID for better optimisation.

Are Google message assets available in the UAE?

Google's help pages don't list countries for message assets, which are in beta. Check eligibility in your Google Ads account.

Should I use Google or Meta to get WhatsApp leads?

Google catches people already searching, with high intent but limited volume. Meta reaches people who weren't searching and opens WhatsApp directly. Most brands do best with both.
