Dubai property ad permits and WhatsApp leads: the rules in 2026

Real estate is one of the most regulated categories to advertise in the UAE, and most property leads now arrive on WhatsApp. Here is what the Dubai Land Department and other official sources require before an ad runs, and how to handle the chats it starts.

By Nawaf Allahham, founder of Nuevo Automations · Updated 28 November 2026

Every property ad in Dubai needs a Real Estate Advertisement Permit from the Dubai Land Department, applied for through the Trakheesi system; the DLD lists a fee of AED 1,000 plus AED 20, and about one working day. Since 24 April 2023 each permit comes with a Madmoun QR code that must appear on the ad, and scanning it shows the approved advertiser and the property's status. Press reports describe fines of AED 50,000 per violation for brokers. In Abu Dhabi, Madhmoun permits through ADREC have been required for property ads since July 2025. Social media creators promoting property also need the UAE Advertiser Permit.

General information, not legal advice. Check the current rules with the Dubai Land Department or your emirate's regulator before you advertise.

The DLD advertising permit

  • Every real estate advertisement in Dubai needs a permit from the Dubai Land Department, issued through the Trakheesi system.
  • The DLD lists the fee as AED 1,000 plus AED 20 in fees per permit (AED 5,000 for project launch events), with a processing time of about one working day.
  • Brokers need a marketing agreement with the property owner before they can advertise it.
  • The DLD's service page covers electronic and online ads. It doesn't name social media or WhatsApp specifically, but online ads, including paid social, are where the permit matters most in practice.

The Madmoun QR code

According to the Dubai Media Office, from 24 April 2023 every advertising permit comes with a Madmoun QR code, and companies must show it on their ads. Anyone who scans it sees that the ad is approved, who the advertiser is, and whether the property is still available. Gulf News reported in April 2025 that a DLD AI system automatically adjusted 29% of 279,000 property listings.

Enforcement

  • Press reports describe fines of AED 50,000 per violation, sometimes with suspension of up to three months.
  • In February 2024 agents were given three days to remove misleading listings and 30 firms were fined; in April 2024 brokers were given five days to remove non-compliant portal ads, with no more than three brokers allowed to advertise one property.

Abu Dhabi and creators

  • Abu Dhabi: Madhmoun advertising permits through ADREC and the DARI platform have been required for property ads since 7 July 2025, according to law firm and portal summaries. ADGM runs its own permit.
  • Creators and influencers: anyone promoting products or services on social media in the UAE needs the Advertiser Permit from the national media regulator.

Running WhatsApp lead campaigns within the rules

  1. Permit first, ad second. Every ad that names a property gets its own permit and shows its Madmoun QR code.
  2. Match the ad to the permit. Price, size and location in the ad and in the first WhatsApp reply should match what was approved.
  3. Answer in seconds, qualify in the chat. Ask budget, area, move-in date and whether they are buying or renting, then hand qualified leads to an agent with a summary.
  4. Stop when the unit is gone. Update the reply the moment a property is sold or let; the QR code will say so too.
  5. Keep marketing messages to people who opted in, under WhatsApp's rules and the UAE telemarketing resolution. See our WhatsApp marketing rules guide.

Common questions

Do I need a permit to advertise property in Dubai?

Yes. Every real estate advertisement in Dubai needs a permit from the Dubai Land Department through the Trakheesi system. The DLD lists the fee as AED 1,000 plus AED 20 per permit.

What is the Madmoun QR code?

A QR code issued with every Dubai property advertising permit since 24 April 2023. It must appear on the ad, and scanning it shows the approved advertiser and whether the property is still available.

What is the fine for advertising property without a permit in Dubai?

Press reports describe fines of AED 50,000 per violation, sometimes with suspension. Check the current penalty with the Dubai Land Department.

Do Abu Dhabi property ads need a permit?

Yes. Madhmoun advertising permits through ADREC and the DARI platform have been required for property ads in Abu Dhabi since July 2025. ADGM has its own permit.

Can I use WhatsApp for property leads in Dubai?

Yes. The ad that starts the chat needs its permit and QR code, the details you share should match it, and marketing messages should only go to people who opted in.
