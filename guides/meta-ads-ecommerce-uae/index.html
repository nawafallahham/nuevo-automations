Meta ads for e-commerce brands in the UAE: what to know in 2026

Instagram and Facebook are where most UAE online stores find buyers. This guide covers the campaign setup, tracking, rules and paperwork that matter for a UAE brand in 2026, without the guesswork benchmarks.

By Nawaf Allahham, founder of Nuevo Automations · Updated 27 November 2026

For a UAE online store, Meta ads in 2026 mean Advantage+ sales campaigns (the renamed Advantage+ shopping campaigns) or manual sales campaigns, optimised on purchases tracked through the Meta pixel plus the Conversions API. Health and supplement ads must target people 18 or older and avoid miracle claims. Meta charges 5% UAE VAT on ad spend unless you add your 15-digit tax registration number. Influencers and creators who promote products in the UAE need an advertiser permit from the National Media Authority, enforced from early 2026, and brands should check the permit before paying them.

Pick the right campaign

Meta renamed Advantage+ shopping campaigns to Advantage+ sales campaigns in February 2025. They now share one setup with the Sales objective. You can let Meta automate audience, placements and budget, or turn settings off and run what Meta calls a manual sales campaign.

  • Advantage+ sales works best once your pixel sees steady purchases. Meta's system needs conversions to learn from.
  • Manual sales campaigns give more control when you're testing products, audiences or creative, or when purchase volume is low.
  • Click-to-WhatsApp (messages) campaigns suit products people want to ask about first: supplements, skincare, anything with sizing or dosage questions. They skip the landing page. See our click-to-WhatsApp ads guide.

Track purchases properly: pixel plus Conversions API

Browser tracking alone misses sales because of ad blockers, iOS privacy settings and in-app browsers. Meta's Conversions API sends purchase events from your server, store platform or CRM straight to Meta. Setting it up from Events Manager has no fees. Run it alongside the pixel and make sure events are deduplicated, so one sale isn't counted twice.

If you sell in WhatsApp chats, the pixel never sees the sale at all. You need to send those purchases back to Meta with the ad's click ID; our guide to tracking WhatsApp ad sales shows how.

Rules for health, wellness and supplement products

Meta's Health and Wellness policy applies to diet, weight-loss and supplement products:

  • Target people 18 or older.
  • No claims to cure diseases, no clickbait health claims, no close-ups of pinched fat.
  • Meta can block supplements it considers potentially unsafe.

The UAE has its own rules on top of Meta's. Our guide to advertising supplements in the UAE covers the Emirates Drug Establishment's position on medical product ads.

VAT on your Meta ad spend

When Meta started charging VAT in the UAE, it billed 5% VAT to UAE accounts that hadn't added a tax registration number. If your business is VAT-registered, add your 15-digit TRN in your ad account's payment settings; tax advisers then treat the spend under the reverse charge mechanism. Ask your accountant how to record it, and check your Meta invoices to confirm which treatment applies.

The UAE advertiser permit for influencers

Under the UAE's 2023 media law, anyone who promotes products on social media in the UAE, paid or unpaid, needs an advertiser permit. The National Media Authority now runs it, and enforcement began at the end of January 2026. Reported details:

  • The permit is free for the first three years, then AED 1,000 a year. Visiting creators can get a three-month permit for AED 500 through a licensed agency.
  • Creators must show the permit number on their profile.
  • Brands and agencies should check the creator's permit before paying and put the permit number in the contract.
  • Reported fines for posting without a permit start at AED 10,000.

Promoting your own business from your own account is reported to be exempt. Confirm the current rules with the National Media Authority before a campaign.

What actually moves results

  1. Creative. With broad targeting, the ad itself picks the audience. Test several angles, show the product in use, and refresh what fatigues.
  2. Arabic and English. Test both. Many UAE buyers respond better to the language they think in, and Gulf Arabic copy reads differently from translated copy.
  3. Speed after the click. A buyer who messages and waits an hour is usually gone. Fast replies, by a person or an AI assistant, protect the money you spent on the click.
  4. Cash on delivery and fast delivery. If you offer them, say so in the ad.

We don't publish CPM or CPC benchmarks for the UAE here: we haven't found a reliable public source, and costs vary widely by product, season (Ramadan, White Friday, DSF) and creative.

Sources: Meta's Advantage+ campaigns documentation, Conversions API documentation and Health and Wellness ad policy; reporting by Pinsent Masons and The National on the advertiser permit, and Gulf News on VAT. Read on 26 November 2026. This isn't legal or tax advice.

Common questions

What happened to Advantage+ shopping campaigns?

Meta renamed them Advantage+ sales campaigns in February 2025 and merged them into the Sales objective setup. Turning off certain automated settings makes it a manual sales campaign.

Do I need the Conversions API if I have the Meta pixel?

It's strongly recommended. The pixel misses purchases blocked by browsers and privacy settings; the Conversions API sends them from your server or store. Meta charges nothing for it when set up through Events Manager.

Do I pay VAT on Facebook and Instagram ads in the UAE?

Meta charges 5% VAT to UAE ad accounts without a tax registration number. VAT-registered businesses can add their 15-digit TRN in payment settings; check with your accountant how to account for it.

Do influencers in the UAE need a licence to promote my products?

Yes. Since early 2026, people promoting products on social media in the UAE need an advertiser permit from the National Media Authority. Check the permit before you pay and put the number in the contract.

Can I target under-18s with supplement ads?

No. Meta requires health, diet, weight-loss and supplement ads to target people 18 or older.
