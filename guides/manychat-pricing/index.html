ManyChat pricing in 2026, and when a UAE brand needs more

ManyChat is the default tool for Instagram DM automation, and it changed its pricing in March 2026. Here is what each plan includes, how the bill grows, and what changes when you add WhatsApp, from ManyChat's own pages on 28 November 2026.

By Nawaf Allahham, founder of Nuevo Automations · Updated 28 November 2026

Since 2 March 2026 ManyChat has five plans: Free (25 active contacts a month, 1 user), Essential at $17 a month or $14 billed annually (250 contacts, 2 users), Pro at $39 or $29 annually (2,500 contacts, 3 users), Business at $99 or $69 annually (7,500 contacts, 5 users) and Advanced at $199 or $139 annually (25,000 contacts, 10 users). An active contact is anyone you message or who messages you in a month, and extra contacts are charged per contact. Instagram, Messenger, TikTok and Telegram are on every plan; WhatsApp, SMS, email and AI features start on Pro. ManyChat says it adds no markup to Meta's WhatsApp message fees.

How the bill grows: active contacts

ManyChat counts an active contact as anyone you message or who messages you in a month. Go over your plan's allowance and each extra contact is charged: $0.10 on Essential, $0.05 on Pro, $0.025 on Business and $0.004 on Advanced on monthly billing, a little less on annual. Extra inbox seats cost $25 each. A brand running comment-to-DM campaigns can pass a plan's allowance quickly, so estimate contacts, not just messages.

Channels and AI by plan

  • Every plan: Instagram, Facebook Messenger, TikTok and Telegram.
  • Pro and above: WhatsApp, SMS, email, broadcasts and ManyChat's AI features, which are now included rather than sold as an add-on.

Adding WhatsApp to ManyChat

  • WhatsApp needs a paid plan from Pro up. You can connect your own number or buy one through ManyChat, and keeping the WhatsApp Business app on the same number is supported.
  • Meta's message fees are paid on top, from a ManyChat wallet. ManyChat says it adds no markup to Meta's rates.
  • ManyChat must be the only billing method on the WhatsApp account, or templates won't send.
  • ManyChat lists marketing and utility templates; we didn't find authentication templates in its documentation.

Meta's WhatsApp rules apply in full, including charges for service replies beyond 1,000 a month per number from 1 October 2026. See our WhatsApp API pricing guide.

ManyChat or a WhatsApp-first platform?

  • Instagram is your main sales channel: ManyChat is built around Instagram and Messenger flows, and it's hard to beat on price for comment-to-DM and story automations.
  • WhatsApp is your main sales channel: WhatsApp-first platforms such as Wati, Respond.io, SleekFlow or Gallabox put more into the team inbox, templates and WhatsApp reporting. Compare them in our Wati alternatives guide.
  • Either way: the tool doesn't write the answers, run the ads or follow up for you. That's the part M2S covers.

Common questions

How much does ManyChat cost in 2026?

ManyChat has a free plan for 25 active contacts a month. Paid plans are Essential at $17 a month ($14 annually), Pro at $39 ($29), Business at $99 ($69) and Advanced at $199 ($139), with more contacts and users at each step.

What is an active contact in ManyChat?

Anyone you message or who messages you in a given month. Each plan includes a number of active contacts, and extra ones are charged per contact, from $0.10 on Essential down to $0.004 on Advanced.

Does ManyChat support WhatsApp?

Yes, from the Pro plan up. You connect your own number or buy one through ManyChat and pay Meta's message fees from a ManyChat wallet; ManyChat says it doesn't mark them up.

Is ManyChat's AI free?

In the plans introduced in March 2026, ManyChat's AI features are included from the Pro plan up instead of being sold as a separate add-on.

Is ManyChat good for UAE businesses?

For Instagram-led selling, yes: comment-to-DM and story automations are its strength. If WhatsApp is where you close sales, compare WhatsApp-first platforms with stronger team inboxes and template tools.
