WhatsApp ordering for UAE restaurants: next to the delivery apps

Delivery apps bring restaurants new customers. WhatsApp keeps the regulars. Here is how WhatsApp ordering works in 2026, what is publicly known about delivery app fees in the UAE, and how to run both side by side.

By Nawaf Allahham, founder of Nuevo Automations · Updated 28 November 2026

UAE restaurants can take direct orders on WhatsApp with a catalog of up to 500 items and a cart that sends the whole order as one message; WhatsApp's cart doesn't take payment, so you add a payment link or cash on delivery. No delivery platform publishes a standard UAE commission rate. Figures on record vary widely, from Noon Food's Dubai SME offer starting at 10% to roughly 25% reported in 2020 press, and Deliveroo UAE introduced a monthly platform fee from February 2026. Dubai's 2025 circular on delivery platforms requires notice of changes and itemised statements but sets no commission cap.

How WhatsApp ordering works

  • Catalog: up to 500 items in the WhatsApp Business catalog, each with up to 10 photos or videos.
  • Cart: customers add items and send the whole order as one message, a feature WhatsApp launched in December 2020 with restaurants as a named use case. On the API, a sent cart can't be edited and allows up to 99 units per item.
  • Payment: the cart doesn't take payment. Send a payment link or confirm cash on delivery. See taking payments on WhatsApp.

What delivery app fees look like, on the record

None of the big platforms publishes a standard UAE commission rate, and deals vary by restaurant. What is publicly documented:

  • Noon Food: launched in 2021 at 15% plus 2%; its Dubai SME programme for eligible Emirati-owned businesses starts at 10% and rises to 20% by year five.
  • Deliveroo UAE: its help centre confirms a monthly platform fee for all restaurants from 1 February 2026, without stating the amount.
  • Careem: replaced a commission of about 25% with a fixed monthly fee in February 2021; we couldn't confirm the current model.
  • The often-quoted 25 to 30% range comes from 2020 press coverage.

Ask your own account manager for your rate in writing, including payment and delivery fees.

Dubai's 2025 rules for delivery platforms

Dubai's consumer protection authority issued Circular No. 2 of 2025 on delivery platforms. It requires 30 days' notice of changes, monthly itemised statements, optional advertising fees and no hidden costs. It does not cap commissions, and we found no commission cap anywhere in the UAE.

Running WhatsApp next to the apps

  1. Use the apps for discovery, WhatsApp for repeat orders. Put your WhatsApp number on menus, bags and receipts.
  2. Answer fast. An order chat that waits ten minutes goes back to the app.
  3. Confirm every order in writing: items, total, address, time, payment method.
  4. Send order updates as utility templates and offers only to customers who opted in.
  5. Keep one menu. Prices and availability in WhatsApp should match the kitchen's, or orders go wrong.

Common questions

Can restaurants take orders on WhatsApp in the UAE?

Yes. The WhatsApp Business catalog holds up to 500 items and customers can send a whole cart as one message. Payment is handled with a payment link or cash on delivery.

How much commission do delivery apps charge restaurants in the UAE?

No platform publishes a standard UAE rate. Documented figures range from Noon Food's Dubai SME offer starting at 10% to about 25% reported in 2020, and Deliveroo UAE added a monthly platform fee from February 2026. Ask for your own rate in writing.

Is there a cap on delivery app commissions in Dubai?

No. Dubai's 2025 circular on delivery platforms requires transparency, such as 30 days' notice of changes and itemised statements, but sets no commission cap.

Does WhatsApp take payment for restaurant orders?

Not in the UAE. WhatsApp's cart sends the order but doesn't take payment, so restaurants send a payment link or confirm cash on delivery.

Should restaurants leave the delivery apps for WhatsApp?

Usually not. The apps bring new customers; WhatsApp works best for regulars and repeat orders, without a per-order commission.
