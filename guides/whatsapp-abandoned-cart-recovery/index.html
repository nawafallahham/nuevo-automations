WhatsApp abandoned cart recovery, step by step

A cart reminder on WhatsApp gets read in a way an email often doesn't. Here is how to set one up properly: the consent, the timing, the wording, the cost and the way to tell if it's working.

By Nawaf Allahham, founder of Nuevo Automations · Updated 26 November 2026

WhatsApp abandoned cart recovery means sending an approved WhatsApp template to a shopper who left items in their cart, with a way to finish the order. You need the WhatsApp Business API, the shopper's number and consent from checkout, a Meta-approved marketing template, and a link from your store that tells the system a cart was left. Send the first reminder within about an hour, at most one more the next day, stop the moment they reply or buy, and never at night.

What you need before the first reminder

  1. The WhatsApp Business API on your brand's number. The free app can't send automated messages. See WhatsApp API setup on your own number.
  2. The shopper's number and consent. Ask for the WhatsApp number at checkout with a clear tick box or line saying you'll message them about their order.
  3. A cart signal from your store. Shopify, WooCommerce and most platforms can send an "abandoned checkout" event to your system, directly or through a tool like n8n, Make or Zapier.
  4. An approved template. Outside a 24-hour window, WhatsApp only lets businesses start a chat with a template Meta has approved. Cart reminders are classed as marketing.

When to send

MessageWhenGoal
Reminder 130–60 minutes after the cart is leftBring them back while they still remember
Reminder 2 (optional)The next day, during the dayAnswer the question that stopped them
StopAs soon as they reply, buy or say stopRespect, and cost control

Two messages is usually enough. Never send at night, and don't put a customer into more than one sequence a week.

What to write

Name the product, make finishing easy and invite a reply. A reply reopens the 24-hour window, so the conversation can continue normally.

Hi Sara, you left the Magnesium Glycinate 120 caps in your cart. Want me to hold it for you? I can send a payment link here, or you can pay cash on delivery.

مرحباً سارة، تركتِ مغنيسيوم جلايسينات ١٢٠ كبسولة في السلة. هل أحجزه لكِ؟ أقدر أرسل لك رابط الدفع هنا، أو الدفع عند الاستلام.

The second reminder works best when it removes a reason not to buy: delivery time to their emirate, how to take the product, or a smaller size. Avoid fake urgency; Meta reviews templates and customers can block or report you, which hurts your number's quality rating.

Cash on delivery and payment links

Many UAE shoppers abandon at the card step, not because they changed their mind. Offering cash on delivery or a payment link inside the chat often recovers those orders. If you offer cash on delivery, confirm the address and delivery slot in the chat to cut refusals at the door.

What it costs

Each reminder is a marketing template, charged per delivered message at the rate for the customer's country. There's no charge for the customer's reply. If the customer replies, your answers are service messages, covered by 1,000 free a month per number from October 2026. Full details are in our WhatsApp API pricing guide.

How to measure it honestly

  • Count only orders from carts that got a reminder, placed after the reminder, within a set window (we use 72 hours).
  • For cash on delivery, count delivered orders, not placed ones.
  • Compare with a period, or a slice of carts, that got no reminder. Some of those shoppers would have come back anyway.

In M2S's monthly report, recovered carts are listed separately from new sales so you can see exactly what the follow-ups brought back.

Common questions

Can I send cart reminders from the free WhatsApp Business app?

Not automatically. Automated reminders need the WhatsApp Business API and a Meta-approved template.

Is an abandoned-cart message marketing or utility?

Meta treats cart reminders as marketing messages, so they need a marketing template and are charged at the marketing rate.

How many reminders should I send?

One within about an hour, and at most one more the next day. Stop as soon as the shopper replies, buys or asks you to stop.

Do I need consent to message shoppers on WhatsApp?

Yes. WhatsApp requires opt-in. Ask for the number at checkout with a clear statement that you'll message them about their order, and honour every stop request.
