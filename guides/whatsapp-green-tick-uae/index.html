How to get the WhatsApp green tick (verified badge) in the UAE

The "green tick" is now a blue verified badge, and there are two ways to get one. Which applies depends on whether you use the WhatsApp Business app or the API. Here are Meta's requirements as of November 2026.

By Nawaf Allahham, founder of Nuevo Automations · Updated 28 November 2026

In 2026 a UAE business can get a verified badge on WhatsApp in two ways. On the WhatsApp Business API, Meta's Official Business Account status is free: your business portfolio must be verified, the number must have been on the platform for at least 30 days, two-step verification must be on, your display name must be approved, and you must follow WhatsApp's policies; you request it in WhatsApp Manager and can reapply after 30 days if refused. On the free WhatsApp Business app, the verified badge comes with Meta One, Meta's paid subscription launched on 15 September 2026, which UAE press reported from AED 46.99 a month.

Green tick, blue tick: what changed

WhatsApp used to show a green tick next to verified businesses. Meta has since moved to a blue verified badge across its apps. People in the UAE still search for the "WhatsApp green tick", but what you can apply for today is one of the two badges below.

Option 1: Official Business Account (free, API only)

If your number runs on the WhatsApp Business Platform (the API), you can request Official Business Account status at no cost. Meta's requirements:

  • Your number complies with the WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy.
  • It has been registered on the platform for at least 30 days.
  • Your Meta business portfolio is verified.
  • Two-step verification is turned on for the number.
  • Your display name has been approved.

To apply, open WhatsApp Manager, go to the phone number's profile and submit a request for an official business account. Meta doesn't grant it to employees' personal accounts, test accounts or numbers on the WhatsApp Business app. If you're refused, you can apply again after 30 days.

One provider's documentation says the badge isn't available on numbers using coexistence, where the same number runs on both the app and the API. If the badge matters to you, ask your provider before you connect.

Option 2: Meta One (paid, works on the Business app)

On 15 September 2026 Meta launched Meta One, a subscription that is absorbing Meta Verified. Its business plans include a verified badge on the WhatsApp Business app, a verified channel and impersonation protection once your business is verified, plus access to Meta's AI agent. Higher plans add more linked devices and business broadcast credits.

Khaleej Times and Gulf News reported these UAE prices for the business plans in September 2026: Essential from AED 46.99 a month, Advanced from AED 119, Expert from AED 359 and Max from AED 1,199. Meta says prices vary by region, and existing Meta Verified subscribers keep their plan for now.

Which one should you get?

  • You only use the free Business app: Meta One is the only route to a badge.
  • You're on the API, or moving there to automate: apply for the free Official Business Account once you meet the 30-day and verification rules.
  • Either way: verify your business in Meta Business Manager first. It's also what lifts your messaging limit from 250 to 2,000 people a day on the API.

Does the badge help you sell?

It helps customers trust that they're talking to the real brand, which matters in a market with copycat accounts. It doesn't change Meta's message prices, your template approvals or your messaging limits. What sells is answering fast and well, which is what M2S is built to do.

Sources: Meta developer documentation on Official Business Accounts; Meta newsroom, "Introducing Meta One" (15 September 2026); WhatsApp Business page on Meta One; Khaleej Times (17 September 2026) and Gulf News (16 September 2026); 360dialog documentation on coexistence. Read 28 November 2026.

Common questions

How do I get the green tick on WhatsApp in the UAE?

WhatsApp now shows a blue verified badge. On the WhatsApp Business API, request a free Official Business Account in WhatsApp Manager once your business is verified, your number has been on the platform for 30 days and two-step verification is on. On the Business app, subscribe to Meta One.

Is the WhatsApp verified badge free?

On the API, yes: the Official Business Account badge costs nothing if you meet Meta's requirements. On the WhatsApp Business app, the badge comes with a paid Meta One plan, reported from AED 46.99 a month in the UAE.

Do I need to be a famous brand to get verified?

Meta's current Official Business Account requirements are a verified business portfolio, 30 days on the platform, two-step verification, an approved display name and policy compliance. Its documentation doesn't list a separate notability test.

What happens if my request is rejected?

You can submit a new request after 30 days. Use the time to complete business verification and make sure your display name matches your business.

Does a verified badge increase my WhatsApp messaging limit?

No. Messaging limits depend on business verification and message quality. Verifying your business, which you need for the badge anyway, is what raises the limit from 250 to 2,000.
