WhatsApp Flows: forms and screens inside the chat

WhatsApp Flows let a customer fill in a form, pick a date or choose a product without leaving the chat. Here is how they work on the WhatsApp Business Platform in 2026, and where they help a UAE brand sell.

By Nawaf Allahham, founder of Nuevo Automations · Updated 28 November 2026

WhatsApp Flows are forms and screens that open inside a WhatsApp chat, with text fields, dropdowns, date pickers and file uploads, and they can pull live data such as available slots from your system. A business sends a Flow either as a Meta-approved template with a Flow button, which needs a verified business and good message quality, or as an interactive message after the customer writes in. Flows work on Android 6.0 and iOS 12 or later, not on WhatsApp Web. They are part of the WhatsApp Business API; we found no separate Flow fee, so you pay for the message that carries it.

What a Flow looks like

The customer taps a button in the chat and a screen opens over it, like a small app: fields to fill, options to pick, a date to choose. When they submit, the answers come back to your system and the chat continues. Meta lists lead capture, offers, quotes and appointment booking as typical uses, and publishes a case study that reports 2.6 times more appointments.

How Flows are sent

  • As a template with a Flow button: for starting a conversation, for example after a lead form or a purchase. Needs an approved template, a verified business and good message quality.
  • As an interactive message: inside an open conversation, for example when a customer asks to book.

Limits to plan around

  • Phones only: Android 6.0+ and iOS 12+. Customers on WhatsApp Web or desktop can't open them.
  • Meta recommends no more than 3 screens in a sub-flow; long forms lose people.
  • Customers can upload up to 10 files, 100 MiB in total.
  • Flows are a WhatsApp Business API feature; we found no documentation of them in the free WhatsApp Business app.

You may see "10 screens" quoted as a limit; that's one provider's own limit, not Meta's.

Where Flows help a wellness or supplement brand

  • Product finder: three quick questions (goal, age group, preference) and the assistant recommends the right product.
  • Consultation booking: pick a date and time for a call with a nutritionist or the brand's team.
  • Delivery details: collect area, building and preferred delivery time in one screen instead of five messages.
  • Subscription sign-up: choose the product, quantity and frequency for a monthly refill.

A good AI assistant does much of this in plain conversation too. Flows earn their place where a structured answer matters, such as an address or a booking slot.

What Flows cost

We found no Flow-specific fee in Meta's documentation. A Flow travels in a message, so you pay what that message costs: a marketing or utility template if you start the conversation, a service message inside an open window. See our WhatsApp API pricing guide.

Sources: Meta developer documentation for WhatsApp Flows (overview, sending Flows, components, limits) and Meta's Flows business page and case study. Read 28 November 2026.

Common questions

What are WhatsApp Flows?

Forms and screens that open inside a WhatsApp chat, with fields, dropdowns, date pickers and file uploads, so customers can book, sign up or choose products without leaving WhatsApp.

Do WhatsApp Flows cost extra?

We found no Flow-specific fee in Meta's documentation. You pay for the message that carries the Flow, at Meta's normal per-message rates.

Can I use WhatsApp Flows in the WhatsApp Business app?

Flows are a WhatsApp Business API feature. We found no documentation of them in the free WhatsApp Business app.

Do WhatsApp Flows work on desktop?

No. Flows open on Android 6.0 or later and iOS 12 or later; customers on WhatsApp Web or desktop can't use them.

What can I build with WhatsApp Flows?

Meta lists lead capture, offers, quotes and appointment booking. Brands also use them for product finders, delivery details and subscription sign-ups.
