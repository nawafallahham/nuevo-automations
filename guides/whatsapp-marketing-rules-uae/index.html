WhatsApp marketing rules in the UAE: what to follow in 2026

Two sets of rules apply when a UAE brand sends marketing on WhatsApp: Meta's, which decide whether your number keeps working, and the UAE's, which decide whether you get fined. Here is what each says as of November 2026, and where the law leaves room for interpretation.

By Nawaf Allahham, founder of Nuevo Automations · Updated 28 November 2026

To send WhatsApp marketing in the UAE, you need the customer's number and their opt-in, which must name your business and make clear they are agreeing to messages from you. You must honour opt-out requests made on or off WhatsApp. Messages outside the 24-hour customer service window must use Meta-approved templates, new accounts can message 250 people a day until Meta raises the limit, and Meta can cap marketing messages to people who don't engage. Under UAE law, Cabinet Resolution No. 56 of 2024 defines telemarketing to include marketing messages through social media applications, and the data protection law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) requires consent to process personal data, with some exceptions.

This guide is general information, not legal advice. Where the law isn't explicit about WhatsApp, we say so.

Meta's rules: opt-in and opt-out

WhatsApp's Business Messaging Policy (last updated 23 September 2026) lets you message people only if they gave you their number and agreed to hear from you. Meta's developer documentation adds that the opt-in must:

  • clearly state that the person is opting in to receive messages from the business;
  • clearly name the business;
  • comply with local law. It can be general rather than WhatsApp-specific.

You must respect every request to stop, "either on or off WhatsApp", and remove the person from your list. Meta's documentation doesn't require a STOP keyword, but templates can carry an optional opt-out button, and customers can also turn off "offers and announcements" from your business in WhatsApp itself. Good practice: separate opt-ins for order updates and for offers, so someone can keep one and drop the other.

Templates and the 24-hour window

When a customer messages you, a 24-hour customer service window opens and you can reply freely. Outside it, you can only send pre-approved templates in three categories:

  • Marketing: offers, announcements, cart reminders, anything promotional. Mixed content counts as marketing.
  • Utility: non-promotional messages about something the customer did, such as an order confirmation.
  • Authentication: one-time passcodes only.

If you submit a marketing message as utility, Meta relabels it as marketing, and repeat misuse leads to rate limits and restrictions on utility templates for 7 to 30 days.

Messaging limits in 2026

Meta caps how many different people you can start conversations with in a rolling 24 hours. Since 7 October 2025 the limit is shared by every number in your business portfolio.

TierHow you get there
250The default for a new business portfolio
2,000Verify your business, be verified by your partner, or send 2,000 high-quality template messages to unique people within 30 days

The old 1,000 tier and the "Flagged" quality state no longer exist, so older guides that mention them are out of date.

Per-user marketing caps

Separately, WhatsApp may limit how many marketing templates one person receives from all businesses, when that person is unlikely to engage. There is no published number. A blocked message fails with error 131049, and Meta says to wait at least 24 hours before trying again. Marketing messages inside an open 24-hour window don't count towards the cap.

What happens when customers block or report you

Templates get a quality rating (green, yellow or red) from blocks, reports and read rates, and low-quality templates can be paused. Policy breaches follow a ladder: a warning, then 1 to 3 days without templates, then 5 to 30 days without sending anything, then an indefinite account lock, then a permanent ban. Scams and illegal drug sales lead straight to removal. Appeals go through Business Support Home and usually take 24 to 48 hours.

UAE law: the telemarketing rules

Cabinet Resolution No. 56 of 2024 on telemarketing has applied since August 2024. Its definition of telemarketing includes "marketing text messages and marketing messages through social media applications". WhatsApp isn't named, and the Ministry of Economy's summary talks only about calls, so whether it covers WhatsApp is a matter of interpretation. Its main rules, written mostly for calls, are:

  • prior approval from the authority, and only local numbers registered to the company;
  • marketing only to consumers who asked for it, and never to numbers on the Do Not Call Register;
  • marketing calls only between 9am and 6pm, and at most once a day and twice a week to someone who didn't answer;
  • saying who you are and why you are contacting the person at the start.

According to the Ministry of Economy, the accompanying penalties resolution sets fines of AED 10,000 to 150,000 for companies. Khaleej Times reported in August 2026 that AED 19.19 million in fines had been issued to individuals and 9,433 numbers disconnected.

The safe reading for a brand: message only people who opted in, identify yourself in every first message, keep marketing to sensible hours, and stop immediately when asked.

UAE law: personal data

Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on personal data protection has been in force since 2 January 2022. It prohibits processing personal data without consent, with listed exceptions, and lets people restrict or stop processing. Secondary sources say its executive regulations still hadn't been issued as of August 2026, and fine amounts quoted online don't trace to an official source. DIFC and ADGM have their own data protection laws.

In practice: record when and how each customer opted in, keep that record, and delete people who ask you to.

A compliant setup, step by step

  1. Collect opt-in at checkout, in your forms or in the chat, naming your brand and the kind of messages.
  2. Store the opt-in with a date and source.
  3. Use utility templates only for order updates; everything promotional is marketing.
  4. Add an opt-out button to marketing templates and honour it automatically.
  5. Send fewer, better messages. Engagement keeps your quality rating, and your limits, healthy.

Common questions

Is WhatsApp marketing legal in the UAE?

Yes, with consent. Meta requires an opt-in that names your business, and UAE law requires consent to process personal data. The 2024 telemarketing resolution's definition also covers marketing messages through social media applications, so treat its rules as applying.

What counts as a valid WhatsApp opt-in?

Meta says the opt-in must clearly state that the person agrees to receive messages from your business and must name the business. It can be collected anywhere, such as at checkout, in a form or in the chat, and doesn't have to mention WhatsApp specifically.

How many WhatsApp messages can I send per day?

A new business portfolio can start conversations with 250 unique people in 24 hours. Verifying your business unlocks 2,000, and Meta raises the limit to 10,000, 100,000 and then unlimited as you use it with high-quality messages.

What time can I send marketing messages in the UAE?

Cabinet Resolution No. 56 of 2024 limits marketing calls to 9am to 6pm. It doesn't spell out hours for messages, but keeping WhatsApp marketing in that window is the safe choice.

What happens if customers report my WhatsApp messages as spam?

Your template and number quality drops, and Meta can pause templates, block sending for 1 to 30 days, lock the account or ban it permanently. Message only people who opted in, and make it easy to stop.
