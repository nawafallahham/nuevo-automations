How much does a WhatsApp chatbot cost in the UAE?

A WhatsApp chatbot is never one price. You pay Meta for messages, a platform for the inbox and automation, someone for the AI, and someone to set it up. Here is each part for a UAE business in 2026, with list prices from the platforms themselves.

By Nawaf Allahham, founder of Nuevo Automations · Updated 27 November 2026

A WhatsApp chatbot in the UAE has four costs. First, Meta's per-message fees on the WhatsApp Business Platform, priced by message type at the UAE rate; customer messages are free, and chats from click-to-WhatsApp ads open a free window. Second, a platform subscription: on their own pricing pages in November 2026, Wati starts at $59 a month (billed annually), Respond.io at $79 a month (annually) or $99 monthly, Gallabox at AED 412 a month (annually), and Twilio charges $0.005 per message with no subscription. Third, AI usage, usually as platform AI credits. Fourth, setup: connecting your number, templates, store integration and training the bot.

Cost 1: Meta's message fees

Every WhatsApp chatbot that runs on the official API pays Meta per delivered message. The price depends on the message category (marketing, utility, authentication or service) and on the country code of the customer receiving it. The UAE is its own market on Meta's rate card, and Meta lists the dirham as a billing currency from 1 April 2026.

  • Messages customers send you are free.
  • Service replies: from 1 October 2026 each business number gets 1,000 free service messages a month, then pays per message.
  • Click-to-WhatsApp ads: if you reply within 24 hours, Meta opens a free entry point window of up to 7 days in which template and service messages to that customer aren't charged.
  • Meta Business Agent (Meta's own AI) is billed separately at $2 per million tokens.

Rates change several times a year, so check Meta's pricing page before you budget. Our WhatsApp Business API pricing guide explains the rules in full.

Cost 2: the platform

The API has no inbox or chatbot builder of its own, so almost every business uses a platform on top. These are the list prices on each platform's own pricing page on 26 November 2026. They exclude Meta's message fees.

Two things change the real cost more than the headline price: how many team members need the inbox (extra users cost $12 to $89 each a month depending on platform and plan) and whether the platform adds a margin to Meta's message fees.

Cost 3: the AI

A rules-based bot with buttons costs nothing extra. A bot that understands free-text questions, in English and Arabic, uses a language model, and someone pays for it:

  • Platform AI credits. Wati, Respond.io and Gallabox include a monthly allowance and sell more: Wati charges $25 for 2,000 extra credits, Respond.io $15 per 1,000.
  • Meta Business Agent tokens, if you use Meta's own AI, at $2 per million tokens.
  • Your own model, if a developer builds the bot on the API directly.

The cheapest AI is the one that answers correctly the first time. A bot that guesses at prices or stock costs you sales, not credits, so make sure it is trained on your real catalogue and hands hard questions to a person.

Cost 4: setup and running it

Someone has to connect your number to the API, verify your business with Meta, write and get templates approved, connect your store (Shopify, WooCommerce or Zid), load your products and policies, write the flows for carts and payment links, and then watch the first weeks of chats and fix what the bot gets wrong. You can do this yourself on a platform, or pay an agency or developer to do it. We haven't found a credible public benchmark for setup fees in the UAE, so ask for a fixed quote in writing.

A simple way to compare quotes

  1. Estimate your monthly messages by type (cart reminders, order updates, replies).
  2. Price them at Meta's current UAE rate, then ask each platform whether it adds anything per message.
  3. Add the platform fee for the plan and number of users you actually need.
  4. Add AI usage at your expected volume, not the free allowance.
  5. Add setup and any monthly management.

Then compare that total with the sales it brings back. A bot that recovers a handful of abandoned carts a week usually pays for itself; one that only answers FAQs often doesn't.

Common questions

Is there a free WhatsApp chatbot?

The free WhatsApp Business app has quick replies and a greeting message but no real chatbot or AI. A chatbot needs the WhatsApp Business API, which means Meta's per-message fees plus a platform or developer.

What is the cheapest way to run a WhatsApp chatbot?

Building directly on the API with a provider like Twilio has no subscription ($0.005 a message plus Meta's fees) but needs a developer. For most small brands, a platform's entry plan is cheaper overall once you count build and maintenance time.

Do WhatsApp platforms charge more than Meta for messages?

Some do. Respond.io says it doesn't mark up Meta's rates. Interakt and Gallabox list markup discounts on enterprise plans, which implies a markup on lower plans. Wati shows its rates only after login. Ask each provider in writing.

Can a WhatsApp chatbot reply in Arabic?

Yes. AI-based bots on the main platforms can reply in Arabic and English. Check how well the bot handles Gulf dialect and mixed Arabic-English messages before you commit.

Does the chatbot cost more if customers come from ads?

Usually less. Chats that start from a click-to-WhatsApp ad on a phone open Meta's free entry point window when you reply within 24 hours, so Meta doesn't charge for messages to that customer for up to 7 days.
