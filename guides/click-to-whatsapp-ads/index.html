Click-to-WhatsApp ads: how they work and how to make them sell

A click-to-WhatsApp ad sends the buyer straight into a chat with your business instead of to a web page. Here is when that sells better, how to set one up, and the part most brands get wrong: what happens after the tap.

By Nawaf Allahham, founder of Nuevo Automations · Updated 26 November 2026

A click-to-WhatsApp ad is an Instagram or Facebook ad whose button opens a WhatsApp chat with your business, often with a pre-filled first message. It works best when buyers have questions before they pay, such as dosage, delivery or authenticity, which is common for premium wellness products in the UAE. The ad only starts the conversation: the sale depends on replying within seconds and on reporting chat sales back to Meta so it can find more buyers.

What a click-to-WhatsApp ad is

It's a normal Meta ad on Instagram, Facebook or Messenger whose call to action is "Send message" or "WhatsApp". Tapping it opens WhatsApp on the buyer's phone with your business number and a first message you wrote, such as "Hi, I'd like the price of the 60-capsule pack". The buyer presses send and the conversation starts.

Inside WhatsApp, the chat carries a small card showing which ad it came from, and the API passes your system a click ID for that ad. That ID is what lets you report the sale back to Meta later.

When a chat beats a landing page

Send to WhatsApp when…Send to your website when…
Buyers ask questions before paying (ingredients, dosage, authenticity)The product is cheap and self-explanatory
Orders are often cash on delivery or need a delivery dateYour checkout is fast and buyers rarely need help
Many buyers prefer to talk in ArabicYou can't reply within minutes, day and night
Bundles or subscriptions need a recommendationYour buyers are mostly on desktop

The second column matters. A WhatsApp ad that gets a reply three hours later usually performs worse than a plain product page, because the buyer has already scrolled on.

How to set one up in Meta Ads Manager

  1. Connect your WhatsApp number to your Facebook Page or Instagram account through Meta's Accounts Center. Use the number customers already know.
  2. Pick the objective. Engagement, Leads or Sales can all send people to a chat; choose "Messaging apps" and then WhatsApp as the conversion location.
  3. Write the pre-filled message so it tells you what they want: "Price for the 60-capsule pack, please" beats "Hi".
  4. Add a greeting and two or three suggested questions if you use Meta's message template in the ad.
  5. Build the creative around one product and one question. The ad's job is to start a specific conversation, not to explain the whole brand.
  6. Target adults. Meta requires ads for dietary, health and herbal supplements to target people aged 18 or over. See our guide to advertising supplements in the UAE.

The first minute decides the sale

Someone who taps your ad is interested right now and has WhatsApp open. If the first reply takes hours, the moment has passed. What a good first reply does:

  • Answers the exact question in the pre-filled message, with the price, in the buyer's language.
  • Asks one question back that moves toward an order: "Is it for you, or a gift?", "Which emirate shall we deliver to?"
  • Offers a way to pay in the chat, by payment link or cash on delivery, as soon as they're ready.

This is the job M2S does: an AI assistant trained on your own products and prices replies in seconds, in English or Arabic, and hands the chat to your team with a summary when it should.

Why click-to-WhatsApp chats can cost less in message fees

When a chat starts from one of these ads on a phone and you reply within 24 hours, Meta opens a free entry point window in which it doesn't charge message fees for that customer. Details and the October 2026 pricing change are in our WhatsApp API pricing guide.

Reporting sales back to Meta

By default, Ads Manager reports "messaging conversations started" and a cost per conversation. It has no idea which chats bought. If you optimise on conversations, Meta learns to find people who like to chat, not people who pay.

The fix is to send each sale made in a chat back to Meta through the Conversions API, tied to the ad's click ID, with the order value. Then you can see real return on ad spend per ad and let Meta optimise for buyers. We cover how in how to track sales from WhatsApp ads.

Common mistakes

  • Sending ads to a personal phone that one person checks when they can.
  • A vague pre-filled message, so the first reply has to be "how can I help?"
  • Judging the campaign on cost per conversation instead of cost per sale.
  • No follow-up: most chats that go quiet never hear from the business again.
  • Claims in the ad (or the chat) that the product's approval doesn't allow.

Common questions

Do click-to-WhatsApp ads work on Instagram?

Yes. They run on Instagram, Facebook and Messenger placements. Tapping the button opens WhatsApp on the buyer's phone with your business number.

Do I need the WhatsApp Business API for click-to-WhatsApp ads?

No, the ads can open chats on the WhatsApp Business app. You need the API to reply automatically, tag each chat with its ad, run follow-ups and report sales back to Meta.

Which campaign objective should I use?

Engagement, Leads or Sales can all use WhatsApp as the conversion location. Once you send sales back through the Conversions API, a Sales objective lets Meta optimise for buyers rather than for conversations.

How fast do I need to reply?

As fast as possible, ideally within seconds. The buyer has just tapped the ad and has WhatsApp open; a reply hours later usually loses the sale.
