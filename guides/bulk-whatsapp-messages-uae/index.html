How to send bulk WhatsApp messages in the UAE, the legal way

Sending one message to hundreds of customers is easy to do badly. Unofficial "bulk sender" tools get numbers banned, and messages to people who never opted in break both Meta's rules and UAE law. Here are the three legitimate ways to do it in 2026.

By Nawaf Allahham, founder of Nuevo Automations · Updated 28 November 2026

There are three legitimate ways to send bulk WhatsApp messages in the UAE. The free WhatsApp Business app has broadcast lists of up to 256 contacts, which only reach people who saved your number. The app also offers paid business broadcasts that reach customers who haven't saved it, in the countries where Meta offers them. For larger volumes, the WhatsApp Business API sends Meta-approved marketing templates to anyone who opted in, starting at 250 people a day for a new account and rising to 2,000 after business verification, with Meta charging per delivered message. Every method needs the customer's opt-in, and you must stop messaging anyone who asks.

Method 1: broadcast lists in the Business app (free)

  • Up to 256 contacts per list.
  • Only people who saved your number in their phone receive it. Everyone else silently misses it.
  • Each person gets it as a private message, and replies come back to you one to one.
  • Linked devices can't create broadcast lists; you need the main phone.

Fine for a small store with loyal customers. Not a way to reach new leads from ads, most of whom won't have saved your number.

Method 2: business broadcasts in the app (paid)

Since April 2025 Meta has offered paid business broadcasts in the WhatsApp Business app. They reach customers even if they haven't saved your number, and you pay per message delivered, at a rate set by the customer's country. Meta says the feature is only available in limited countries, and we couldn't confirm UAE availability in November 2026, so check in your app under the broadcast tools.

Method 3: marketing templates on the WhatsApp Business API

This is how brands send to thousands of customers. You write a template, Meta approves it, and your platform sends it to your opted-in list.

Avoid unofficial bulk senders

Tools that automate the normal WhatsApp app or WhatsApp Web to blast messages break WhatsApp's terms. Numbers used this way are regularly restricted or banned, and you lose the number and its chat history. Use the app's own broadcast tools or the official API.

The consent rules

  • Meta: the customer must have opted in to messages from your business, with your business named. You must honour opt-outs made on or off WhatsApp.
  • UAE telemarketing rules: Cabinet Resolution No. 56 of 2024 defines telemarketing to include marketing messages through social media applications, bans contacting numbers on the Do Not Call Register, and allows marketing calls only from 9am to 6pm. Applying the same discipline to WhatsApp is the safe reading.
  • UAE data protection law: processing personal data needs consent, with listed exceptions.

The full picture is in our WhatsApp marketing rules guide.

How to get replies, not blocks

  1. Segment: send the restock message only to people who bought that product.
  2. Lead with something useful, not "Dear customer".
  3. One clear action per message, with a button.
  4. Add an opt-out button and respect it automatically.
  5. Have someone, or an AI, ready to answer the replies within minutes. A broadcast that gets questions and no answers wastes the send.

Common questions

How many people can I send a WhatsApp broadcast to?

In the WhatsApp Business app, up to 256 contacts per broadcast list, and only those who saved your number receive it. On the API, a new account can reach 250 people a day, rising to 2,000 after business verification and higher as Meta scales you.

Is bulk WhatsApp messaging legal in the UAE?

Yes, to people who opted in, through WhatsApp's own tools or the official API. UAE telemarketing rules cover marketing messages through social media applications, and data protection law requires consent, so never message bought or scraped lists.

Why didn't some people receive my broadcast?

In the Business app, broadcasts only reach contacts who saved your number. On the API, Meta may hold back marketing messages to people who rarely engage, which shows as error 131049.

How much does it cost to send bulk WhatsApp messages?

Broadcast lists in the Business app are free. Business broadcasts in the app and marketing templates on the API are charged per delivered message at a rate for the customer's country, plus any platform fee for the API.

Will my number get banned for sending bulk messages?

Not if you use official tools and message only people who opted in. Numbers get restricted when many recipients block or report them, or when unofficial bulk-sending tools are used.
