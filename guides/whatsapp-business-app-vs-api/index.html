WhatsApp Business app vs WhatsApp Business API in 2026

The free WhatsApp Business app is where most UAE businesses start. The WhatsApp Business Platform, usually called the API, is what you need to automate. Since 2025 you can even run both on the same number. Here is how to choose, with Meta's own limits as of November 2026.

By Nawaf Allahham, founder of Nuevo Automations · Updated 28 November 2026

The WhatsApp Business app is free, runs on one phone plus up to four linked devices (ten with multi-agent), and has quick replies, greeting messages and broadcast lists of up to 256 people who must have saved your number. The WhatsApp Business API (Cloud API) has no inbox of its own, so you use it through a platform, but it allows unlimited agents, chatbots and AI, approved templates to anyone who opted in, and integrations, with Meta charging per message. Coexistence lets one number use both: the app for one-to-one chats, the API for automation. The free blue tick is available only on the API; Meta One sells a verified badge for the app.

When the free app is enough

  • One or two people answer every chat.
  • Fewer than a few dozen conversations a day.
  • You don't run ads that send people to WhatsApp at volume.
  • You don't need messages to go out automatically when someone abandons a cart or doesn't pay.

Note that the app's linked devices sign out if the main phone is idle for more than 14 days, and linked devices can't create broadcast lists.

When you need the API

  • You run click-to-WhatsApp ads and chats arrive faster than people can answer.
  • You want an AI assistant replying in seconds, at night and in Arabic and English.
  • You want automatic follow-ups on carts, unpaid payment links and quiet leads.
  • You want sales from chats reported back to Meta to measure real return on ad spend.

What the API costs

Since 1 July 2025 Meta charges per delivered message, priced by category (marketing, utility, authentication, service) and by the customer's country code. The UAE has its own rate card, billable in dirhams since 1 April 2026. Each number gets 1,000 free service messages a month, and chats that start from a click-to-WhatsApp ad are free for up to 7 days when you reply within 24 hours. Our API pricing guide covers the details, and the chatbot cost guide adds platform prices.

Coexistence: the app and the API on one number

Meta now lets a business connect its existing WhatsApp Business app number to the Cloud API through a partner, and keep using the app. Per Meta's documentation:

  • You need app version 2.24.17 or later, and you onboard through a Meta partner.
  • Up to 180 days of one-to-one chat history is imported, and chats stay in sync between the app and the API.
  • Messages you send from the app stay free; API messages are charged at the normal rates.
  • Limits: throughput is fixed at 20 messages a second, broadcast lists become read-only, group chats don't sync, and up to 4 companion devices are supported.
  • If the main phone is inactive for about 14 days, the connection drops.

This is what lets an AI assistant such as M2S run on a brand's own number while the team keeps answering in the WhatsApp app they already use.

The blue tick and Meta One

  • Official Business Account (the free blue tick) is only for API numbers. Meta requires a verified business portfolio, at least 30 days on the platform, two-step verification and an approved display name. If refused, you can reapply after 30 days. One provider's documentation says it isn't available on coexistence numbers.
  • Meta One, launched on 15 September 2026, is Meta's paid subscription that is absorbing Meta Verified. Its business plans include a verified badge on the WhatsApp Business app. Khaleej Times and Gulf News reported UAE prices from AED 46.99 a month for Essential and AED 119 for Advanced.

Common questions

Is the WhatsApp Business API free?

No. Meta charges per delivered message, by category and country, and most businesses also pay a platform for the inbox and automation. Each number gets 1,000 free service messages a month, and chats from click-to-WhatsApp ads open a free window of up to 7 days.

Can I use the WhatsApp Business app and the API on the same number?

Yes, through Meta's coexistence feature, set up via a Meta partner. You keep chatting in the app while the API handles automation; there are limits, such as read-only broadcast lists and 20 messages a second.

How many people can use one WhatsApp Business app account?

One phone plus up to 4 linked devices, or up to 10 devices with multi-agent. The API has no such limit; it depends on your platform.

How do I get the green or blue tick on WhatsApp?

The free Official Business Account badge is only for API numbers with a verified business, 30 days on the platform and two-step verification. App users can get a verified badge through a paid Meta One subscription.

Why can't my broadcast reach some customers?

In the WhatsApp Business app, broadcasts only reach people who have saved your number, up to 256 per list. To reach everyone who opted in, you need API templates or the app's paid business broadcasts where available.
