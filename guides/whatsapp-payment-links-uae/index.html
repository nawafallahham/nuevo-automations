How to take payments on WhatsApp in the UAE

Customers in the UAE are happy to buy in a WhatsApp chat, but WhatsApp itself can't take their money here. The fix is a payment link from your payment provider. This guide covers what works, what each provider charges, and how to keep the sale in the chat.

By Nawaf Allahham, founder of Nuevo Automations · Updated 28 November 2026

WhatsApp's built-in payments, including the "Review and Pay" order message, are only documented by Meta for India, Brazil and Singapore, not the UAE. UAE businesses take payment in WhatsApp by sending a payment link from their own provider: Stripe charges 2.9% plus AED 1 for domestic cards, Ziina lists 2.6% plus AED 1 on its business page, and Telr has monthly plans with unlimited payment links. Tabby and Tamara can also create payment links, though their documentation describes sending them by SMS or email. The customer taps the link, pays on the provider's page, and your team or AI confirms the order in the chat.

WhatsApp Pay isn't available to UAE businesses

Meta does have native payments on the WhatsApp Business Platform: an order message where the customer taps Review and Pay and pays without leaving WhatsApp. But Meta's documentation covers only three markets. In India it works with UPI and payment gateways in rupees only, in Brazil with Pix, boleto and payment links, and in Singapore through Stripe for Singapore businesses. We found no UAE version as of November 2026.

So a UAE brand can't use the native order and payment messages. What it can do is send a normal link, or a template with a link button, that opens a secure payment page from a licensed provider.

Buy now, pay later in the chat: Tabby and Tamara

Instalments help with higher-priced orders, such as a three-month supplement bundle. Both big UAE providers can create payment links:

  • Tabby lets merchants create payment links in its merchant dashboard and business app. Its help pages describe sending them by SMS or email.
  • Tamara documents a payment link that is sent to the customer by SMS and expires after 15 minutes by default.

Neither documents WhatsApp as a channel. Some payment gateways offer Tabby or Tamara inside their own links, which you can send in WhatsApp. Ask your provider how they want it done before you build a flow around it.

How a sale in WhatsApp should run

  1. Answer the questions first. Price, ingredients, delivery time. Buyers who ask before they pay are the ones a landing page loses.
  2. Confirm the order in writing: product, quantity, price including VAT, delivery address and date.
  3. Send the link with the amount in the message, so the customer knows exactly what they are paying for.
  4. Watch for the payment. Most providers send a webhook or email when a link is paid. Connect it so the chat updates automatically.
  5. Follow up on unpaid links. A polite reminder a few hours later recovers a share of buyers who got distracted. Inside the 24-hour customer service window this is a normal reply; outside it you need an approved template.
  6. Offer cash on delivery as a fallback if your logistics support it, for customers who won't pay online.

What WhatsApp messages cost along the way

Meta charges per delivered message. From 1 October 2026 utility messages, such as an order confirmation, are charged even inside the 24-hour window, and service replies are free only up to 1,000 a month per number. Chats that start from a click-to-WhatsApp ad still open a free entry point window when you reply within 24 hours. Our WhatsApp Business API pricing guide has the details.

Common questions

Is WhatsApp Pay available in the UAE?

Not for businesses, as of November 2026. Meta documents WhatsApp's native business payments only for India, Brazil and Singapore. UAE businesses send a payment link from their own provider instead.

What is the cheapest way to take a payment in WhatsApp?

It depends on volume. Stripe has no monthly fee and charges 2.9% plus AED 1 for domestic cards. Telr's higher-volume plans go down to 2.25% plus AED 0.50 with no monthly fee. Compare the total on your real monthly sales, including international cards.

Can I send a Tabby or Tamara link on WhatsApp?

Both can create payment links, but their own documentation describes sending them by SMS or email, not WhatsApp. Some gateways offer BNPL inside links you can share in WhatsApp. Ask the provider before building it into your flow.

Do I need the WhatsApp Business API to send payment links?

No, you can paste a link in the free WhatsApp Business app. You need the API to automate it: sending links from an AI assistant, tracking which are paid, and following up on unpaid ones.

Is it safe for customers to pay through a WhatsApp link?

Yes, when the link opens a page from a licensed payment provider on its own domain. Tell customers which provider you use, and never ask for card details inside the chat itself.
