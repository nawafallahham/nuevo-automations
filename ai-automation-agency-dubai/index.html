Short answer

Nuevo Automations is an AI marketing and automation agency for Dubai and UAE businesses. We run Meta ads on Instagram and Facebook that open a WhatsApp chat, and M2S replies in seconds, in English or Arabic, qualifies the lead, closes the sale or books the appointment in the chat, and follows up with people who went quiet. One team owns the whole path from ad to sale, and a monthly report shows which ads made money.

Why an agency like this

The click isn't the sale

A normal ad agency is judged on clicks, leads and cost per message. Its job ends when the customer opens WhatsApp. What happens next is your problem: the reply that comes three hours later, the price that gets sent and never answered, the lead nobody follows up because the team is busy. The agency reports a good month, you see few sales, and nobody can say which side is wrong.

A chatbot tool fixes part of that, but only if someone runs it. You write the answers, keep the prices current, design the follow-ups, connect it to your ads and check it every week. Most Dubai business owners don't have the time, so the bot sits half set up, answering the easy questions and dropping the rest.

We put both halves under one team. We run the ads, and we run the system that answers, qualifies, closes and follows up. Because every chat is tagged with the ad that started it, we can see which ads bring buyers and which only bring questions, and we change the ads based on sales, not clicks.

What it looks like

The ad and the answer, run by the same team

The customer clicked a Facebook ad at ten at night and was booked three minutes later. The assistant quoted from your price list, asked only what it needed and offered real open times. Your team saw the booking, with the ad that brought it, the next morning.

That last part is why it matters that one team runs both sides. In the monthly report, this booking counts for that ad. If an ad brings plenty of chats and few bookings, we see it and change it, instead of reporting a cheap cost per message and calling it a win.

Working with us

Audit, setup, launch, report

It starts with a free 20-minute audit. We look at your current ads, how fast your WhatsApp gets answered, where chats stop and what happens to people who go quiet. You leave with a clear picture of where the sales are being lost, whether or not you work with us.

If it's a fit, we load your products or services, prices and policies, connect your WhatsApp number, set up your pipeline and follow-ups, and agree with you which questions always go to a person. You check the assistant's answers before anything goes live. Then we launch the ads, watch the first conversations closely and adjust both sides.

After that, we keep running it. We update the ads, keep the assistant's information current when your prices or offers change, and send you an M2S Results report every month: conversations, qualified leads, sales, reply speed and revenue recovered by follow-ups.

FAQ

Common questions about working with us

What does an AI marketing and automation agency do?

We run your Meta ads and the system that turns the chats they bring into sales. That means the ads, an AI sales assistant on your WhatsApp, follow-ups for people who went quiet, a sales pipeline and a monthly report showing which ads made money.

How is this different from a normal ad agency?

A normal ad agency stops at the click or the message. We also own what happens in the chat: the reply, the qualification, the order or booking and the follow-up. So we judge the ads on sales, not on cheap messages.

Why not just buy a chatbot tool?

A tool only works if someone sets it up, keeps it current and connects it to the ads. We do that for you, and we look after the ads that feed it, so nothing sits half finished.

Do I keep my WhatsApp number?

Yes. The assistant runs on your own WhatsApp number and you can still use the WhatsApp app. Anything the assistant can't answer goes to your team with an instant WhatsApp alert and a summary of the chat.

Do you only work with businesses in Dubai?

No. We're based in the UAE and work with businesses in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and the other emirates. The audit is a 20-minute call, so location doesn't matter.

How much does it cost?

M2S is one fixed monthly fee based on your ad budget, your channels and how much of the sale happens in the chat, quoted in writing after a free audit. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta.
