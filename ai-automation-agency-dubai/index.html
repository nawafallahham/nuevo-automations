An AI marketing agency in Dubai that owns the ad, the chat and the report

Ad agencies hand over at the click. Chatbot vendors hand over a tool. We run the ads, run the conversation they start, and report on the sales that came out of both.

An AI marketing agency combines paid advertising with AI-driven customer conversations and reports on the sales they produce. Nuevo Automations runs Meta click-to-WhatsApp ads and Google Ads, the M2S AI sales assistant that replies to the chats those ads start, and a monthly report linking each sale back to the ad behind it.

3 ways to buy this, and what each leaves to you

Ads-only agency

Runs your campaigns and reports clicks and cost per message. What happens in the chat is left to your team.

Chatbot vendor

Sells you software and a setup. Writing the answers, running the ads and reading the results are left to you.

Our approach

One team runs the Meta and Google ads, the AI conversation on WhatsApp and the follow-ups, and reports sales by ad.

Why the ad and the answer belong with one team

When the ads and the conversation are run separately, each side can only optimise its own number: cheaper clicks, or more replies. Run together, the question becomes which ad brought the sale, and that's the number we report on.

Sales made in the chat go back to Meta through the pixel and Conversions API, so the campaigns learn from buyers, not only from people who tapped.

Run by our team

  • Click-to-WhatsApp campaigns on Instagram and Facebook
  • The M2S assistant on your own number, in English or Arabic
  • Checkout by payment link, cash on delivery or booking
  • Follow-ups with quiet hours and opt-out handling
  • A monthly M2S Results report

What our team runs, and what stays with yours

We run the Meta campaigns, the assistant on your WhatsApp, the follow-up sequences, the tracking and the monthly report. You provide what only you can: your products, prices and policies, any approvals your industry needs for ads, and a person to take over the chats the assistant hands on.

Your team fulfils the orders and serves the customers. We make sure they arrive with the details needed to do that.

What your team provides

  • Products, services and prices
  • Policies, delivery or booking rules
  • Access to your Meta ad account and WhatsApp number
  • A contact for hand-offs and alerts

Questions about working with our agency

How is an AI marketing agency different from a software vendor?

A software vendor sells you the tool. We run the Meta and Google ads, the AI conversation on WhatsApp and the follow-ups ourselves, and report on the sales they produce.

What does the engagement process look like?

A free 30-minute audit, a written fixed monthly quote, setup and testing, launch of the ads and assistant, then a monthly M2S Results report.

Do you have case studies from customers?

Yes. Partner brands report 50–60% more revenue from the same traffic after full rollout. Khaleej ParaPharma has seen up to 30x return on ad spend, and Peak Labs gets 53% more orders a month with 74% of chats handled without a person. See the results page

Who pays for the ads, and is it in the price?

You do, directly to Meta or Google, so you always see exactly what the ads cost. Our fee is separate and fixed.
