Khaleej ParaPharma: 19.6x average return on ad spend from WhatsApp ads

A UAE wellness brand that sent its Meta ads to a landing page and answered every enquiry by hand. Since May 2026 we've run its ads into WhatsApp, with M2S replying, closing the sale and following up.

Return on ad spend is Meta's own estimate, across 14 campaigns since May 2026. The 29.9x campaign is the best we've run, not a typical result, and none of this is a guarantee.

Before: ads to a landing page, replies by hand

Khaleej ParaPharma already ran Meta ads, and they sent people to a landing page. Buyers in this category ask before they pay: what's in it, how to use it, how fast it arrives. A landing page can't answer that, so many of them messaged instead.

Every one of those messages was answered by hand, so a buyer who wrote while the team was busy had to wait, and a buyer who waits often buys somewhere else. Without proper tracking, Meta also couldn't tell which ads led to sales.

What we set up

Live since May 2026.

  1. A funnel that ends in WhatsAppWe rebuilt the ads to open a WhatsApp chat instead of a landing page, so buyers with a question ask it and pay in the same place.
  2. Pixel and conversion trackingWe set up the Meta Pixel and conversion tracking properly, so Meta learns from real sales and finds more people who buy.
  3. Better Meta adsNew campaign structure, new creative and steady testing, with English and Arabic versions run separately.
  4. M2S, the AI sales assistantIt answers every chat in seconds in English or Arabic, sends the payment link and follows up with buyers who go quiet. The team steps in only when a chat needs a person.

Why it worked

The ads didn't need a bigger budget. They needed a place where the buyer's question gets answered in seconds and the payment link arrives in the same chat. WhatsApp is that place, and M2S makes sure no chat waits.

With tracking set up properly, Meta could see which conversations became sales and spend more on the ads that brought them. That's how one campaign reached 29.9x and the average across all 14 stayed near 20x.
