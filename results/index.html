Three revenue leaks between the ad and the sale

More traffic is the expensive way to grow. Converting more of the messages you already pay for is usually cheaper, and each of these leaks shows up in the report.

The night-time leak

Enquiries that arrive when nobody's working wait until morning, and some of those customers message the next business. M2S answers every one in seconds, at 2 AM on a Friday, in English or Arabic. The report shows how many came in out of hours.

The "maybe later" leak

Many leads don't say no. They go silent, and nobody chases them. Timed follow-ups restart the conversation and stop the moment the customer replies. The report shows what the follow-ups brought back.

The checkout leak

Carts left on the website and payment links never paid. A friendly WhatsApp reminder brings people back, or lets them finish the order in the chat.

The monthly M2S Results report

You don't have to take our word for it. Every M2S client gets a monthly M2S Results report: conversations, how many came from ads, qualified leads, orders, revenue, reply speed, and the revenue recovered from carts, unpaid links and follow-ups.
