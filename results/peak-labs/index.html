In the founder's words

Before: every chat answered by hand

Peak Labs ran Meta ads to a landing page, and the buyers who had questions messaged the team. Every reply was typed by a person, so first replies took as long as it took someone to get to the phone.

In supplements that wait costs sales. Buyers compare brands while they wait, and the one who answers first usually wins the order.

What we set up

Live since June 2026.

  1. A funnel that ends in WhatsAppWe rebuilt the ads to open a WhatsApp chat instead of a landing page, so buyers with a question ask it and pay in the same place.
  2. Pixel and conversion trackingWe set up the Meta Pixel and conversion tracking properly, so Meta learns from real sales and finds more people who buy.
  3. Better Meta adsNew campaign structure, new creative and steady testing, with English and Arabic versions run separately.
  4. M2S, the AI sales assistantIt answers every chat in seconds in English or Arabic, sends the payment link and follows up with buyers who go quiet. The team steps in only when a chat needs a person.

What changed for the team

First replies are now 90x faster, because M2S answers the moment a chat arrives, day or night. Three in four chats go from first question to finished without anyone on the team touching them.

The team now spends its time on the quarter of chats that need a person. Orders a month are up 53%.
