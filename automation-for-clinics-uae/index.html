Clinic marketing in the UAE: from treatment enquiry to booked consultation

Patients ask about prices, doctors and availability before they book. M2S answers the practical questions, books the consultation, and passes every medical question to your team.

Book a free audit Ask on WhatsApp

Clinic enquiry automation is an assistant that handles the practical side of patient messages, such as prices, opening hours, doctor availability and booking, while leaving medical questions to clinicians. For UAE clinics, the ads behind those enquiries must follow health-advertising rules, which can include DHA, MOH or DoH approval.

Treatment ads and UAE health-advertising approvals

Advertising medical treatments in the UAE is regulated. Depending on the emirate and the treatment, ads may need approval from the Dubai Health Authority (DHA), the Ministry of Health and Prevention (MOH) or the Department of Health Abu Dhabi (DoH) before they run.

We plan treatment campaigns around that: your clinic provides or confirms the approved wording and claims, and we build the ads from it. We don't decide what is medically or legally acceptable; your clinic and its regulator do.

2 kinds of booking, handled differently

Consultations

Booked straight into the right doctor's calendar from free times, with a reminder before the visit.

Procedures

Booked only where your clinic allows it, for example after a consultation, or passed to staff to confirm.

Deposits

If you take one for certain treatments, a payment link from your payment provider is sent before confirming.

Medical questions go to your clinic's team

The assistant answers what's in your information: prices, opening hours, which doctor does which treatment, preparation steps you've written. It doesn't give medical advice, diagnose or say whether a treatment suits someone.

Those questions go to your staff straight away, with a WhatsApp alert and a summary of the chat, so the patient doesn't have to explain twice.

Privacy in patient chats

Patients often share personal details in a chat. Chats stay on your own WhatsApp number, alerts go only to the staff you choose, and the assistant only asks for what booking needs. During the audit we agree with you which details are collected, who sees them, and what your clinic's privacy policy says.

Booking reminders, and patients who asked and went quiet

Each booked consultation gets a reminder beforehand, with a simple reply to confirm or move it. Patients who asked about a treatment and stopped replying get a polite follow-up about the treatment they asked about, never a generic promotion.

Follow-ups stop the moment a patient replies or books, are never sent at night, end for good if the patient says stop, and run at most once a week per patient.

Questions from clinic owners

Does the assistant answer medical questions?

No. It answers practical questions from your information, such as prices, hours and doctor availability, and hands every medical question to your staff with a chat summary.

Do treatment ads need approval in the UAE?

Treatment ads must follow UAE health-advertising rules, which can mean DHA, MOH or DoH approval depending on the emirate and treatment. Your clinic confirms the approved wording, and we build the ads from it.

Can it book consultations and procedures?

It books consultations into the right practitioner's calendar. Procedures are booked only where your clinic allows it, or passed to staff to confirm.

Who can see patient chats?

Chats stay on your own WhatsApp number, and alerts go only to the staff you choose. We agree what details are collected during the audit.
