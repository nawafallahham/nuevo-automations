Short answer

For dental, aesthetic, dermatology and physiotherapy clinics in the UAE, we run Meta ads for your treatments that open a WhatsApp chat, and M2S, our Message to Sale system, answers questions about treatments, prices, doctors and availability from your own information, in English or Arabic, books the consultation into your calendar and sends reminders. It never gives medical advice: symptoms, results and anything clinical go to your staff with a summary of the chat.

The problem

Where clinics lose patients they already paid for

A clinic receptionist does three jobs at once: checking patients in, answering the phone and replying to WhatsApp. WhatsApp loses. The same questions arrive all day (how much is whitening, is the dermatologist in on Saturday, do you take my insurance, where do I park), and each one waits for a free minute. Messages sent after closing wait until the morning, and by then the patient has often booked with the clinic that answered first.

Treatment ads make this worse, not better. A good ad brings more chats than the front desk can handle, and the slow replies waste the spend. Then the quieter leaks start: patients who asked the price and never replied, patients who booked and forgot, and patients who were due back for a second session nobody reminded them about. None of this needs clinical judgement. It needs fast, accurate answers and reliable follow-up, with a clear line where a person takes over.

What it looks like

A booking, with the medical question left to the doctor

A patient clicks a whitening ad late in the evening and writes in Gulf Arabic. The assistant replies in the same language, quotes the price from your treatment list and offers a consultation. When she asks whether whitening is safe for sensitive teeth, it doesn't guess: it says the doctor will answer that, sends the question to your team with a summary, and still offers two real consultation slots.

Your staff get an instant WhatsApp alert and can reply from the WhatsApp app with the full chat in front of them. You decide which topics always go to a person, such as symptoms, results, medication, insurance disputes or complaints. The assistant never invents a price or a treatment you don't offer.

Follow-ups

Patients who asked, then went quiet

Many patients ask about a treatment, get the price and stop replying. They're usually not saying no. They're comparing clinics, waiting for payday or just got busy. M2S follows up with them at sensible intervals, with a short message based on what they actually asked, and offers a consultation slot.

The same approach brings back patients who are due for their next session in a course, or who missed an appointment. Every follow-up stops the moment the patient books or replies, and the assistant picks up the conversation from where it left off. Nothing goes out at night, anyone who says "stop" is never messaged again, and each patient gets at most one sequence a week.

Good to know: the assistant never gives medical advice or a diagnosis, and it doesn't decide which treatment is right for a patient. That stays with your doctors. You keep your own WhatsApp number and can still use the WhatsApp app. On the official WhatsApp API, messages sent more than 24 hours after the patient's last message use a Meta-approved template, which we set up for you.

FAQ

Common questions from clinic owners

Will the assistant give medical advice?

No. It answers front-desk questions such as treatments offered, prices, doctors, timings, insurance and directions. Symptoms, results, medication and anything clinical go to your staff with an instant WhatsApp alert and a summary of the chat.

Are treatment ads allowed on Instagram and Facebook?

Yes, within Meta's advertising policies for health and medical services. We write and run your ads to stay within those rules, and we avoid claims about guaranteed results.

Can it book consultations into our calendar?

Yes. The assistant offers open times based on your working hours, doctors and branches, books the consultation and adds it to your calendar. If you use a clinic system, we check in the audit what it can connect to.

Does it reply in Arabic?

Yes. It replies in Arabic, including Gulf Arabic, and in English, in whichever language the patient writes.

Will patients get spammed?

No. Follow-ups stop the moment a patient books or replies, never go out at night, stop for anyone who says "stop", and run once per patient per week.

How much does it cost?

M2S is one fixed monthly fee based on your ad budget, your channels and the number of treatments and branches, quoted in writing after a free audit. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta.
