Questions before you apply

Why are there qualifying questions?

We take a small number of sprints at a time, and the free week only makes sense for stores that already sell every week, have their own Meta ad account and order values above about AED 100. The questions take under a minute and save you a call that wouldn't help.

What do I pay for the 7 days?

Only the ad spend, paid straight to Meta from your own ad account. We agree the budget with you on the audit call. There's no fee from us for the sprint.

What happens after the week?

We go through the results report with you. If you want to keep going, we move to a monthly retainer and keep the ads running with no gap. If not, you keep the report and the plan.

Which store platforms do you work with?

Most of them, including Shopify and WooCommerce. We check how your store connects on the audit call.

Do I need a new WhatsApp number?

No. We work with the number you already use, and your team can still use the WhatsApp app.

Does the assistant reply in Arabic?

Yes. It replies in English or Arabic, in the language your customer writes in.
