Questions before you book

What do I pay for the 7 days?

Only the ad spend, paid straight to Meta from your own ad account. We agree the budget with you on the audit call. There's no fee from us for the sprint.

What happens after the week?

We go through the results report with you. If you want to keep going, we move to a monthly M2S retainer and keep the ads running with no gap. If not, you keep the report and the plan.

Is my brand a fit for the sprint?

It works best for wellness and supplement brands that already sell every week, have their own Meta ad account, and have order values around AED 150 or more. We'll tell you on the call if it isn't a fit.

Will the AI make health claims about my products?

No. It only uses the product information you approve, and it never gives medical advice. Health questions go straight to your team. You stay responsible for your product claims and any ad permits your products need in the UAE.

Do I need a new WhatsApp number?

No. We work with the number you already use, and your team can still use the WhatsApp app.

Does M2S reply in Arabic?

Yes. The assistant replies in English or Arabic, in the language your customer writes in.
