FAQ

Pricing questions

Why isn't there a price on the website?

Because a store spending AED 3,000 a month on ads and a clinic spending AED 30,000 need very different setups. A fixed package would mean overcharging one and under-serving the other. The audit takes 30 minutes and you get a clear number at the end.

Is the audit really free?

Yes. No cost and no obligation. You keep the plan we put together even if you never work with us.

Is ad spend included in the fee?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta, separate from our fee. That way you always see exactly what the ads cost and what they brought back.

Do you promise a certain number of sales?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. We promise that every lead gets a reply in seconds and every quiet lead gets followed up, and we show you what came back every month.
