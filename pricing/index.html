M2S pricing: one fixed monthly fee

Every brand spends differently on ads and sells a different range, so M2S is quoted after a free audit. You get one fixed monthly fee, in writing, before anything starts.

Book a free audit

What the monthly fee covers

Done for you, from the ad to the paid order.

  • Meta ads set up and run, with click-to-WhatsApp campaigns
  • The AI sales assistant on your WhatsApp number and website chat
  • Ordering in the chat: card payment links, cash on delivery and bookings
  • Follow-ups for carts, unpaid links and chats that went silent
  • A sales pipeline and alerts for new orders and hot leads
  • Pixel and Conversions API tracking
  • A monthly M2S Results report

What you pay for

  • M2S management

    One fixed monthly fee, agreed in writing after the audit.

  • Ad spend

    Paid directly to Meta, so you always see what the ads cost.

  • WhatsApp fees

    Only on the official WhatsApp API, billed by Meta at its own rates.

  • Payment fees

    Your payment provider's normal card fees, if you take card payments.

What decides your quote

  • Your monthly ad budget and how many campaigns we run
  • The channels we connect: WhatsApp, Instagram, website chat
  • How many products the assistant needs to know
  • Your store: Shopify, or another site connected through our API

Pricing questions

Why isn't there a price on the website?

Because a store spending AED 3,000 a month on ads and a clinic spending AED 30,000 need very different setups. A fixed package would overcharge one and under-serve the other. The audit takes 30 minutes and ends with a clear number.

Is the audit really free?

Yes. No cost and no obligation. You keep the plan we put together even if you never work with us.

Is ad spend included in the fee?

No. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta, separate from our fee, so you always see what the ads cost and what they brought back.

Do you promise a number of sales?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. Every lead gets a reply in seconds and every silent lead a follow-up, and you see what came back every month.
