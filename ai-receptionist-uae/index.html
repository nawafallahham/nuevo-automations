An AI receptionist in the UAE that runs your WhatsApp bookings

For any business that sells time: consultants, studios, workshops, tutors, car services, salons. It checks your availability, books the slot, moves it when plans change, takes a deposit if you want one and sends the reminder. It starts with a free 30-minute audit.

Book a free audit Ask on WhatsApp

An AI receptionist is an assistant that handles appointment requests in chat the way a front desk would. It reads your calendar's free times, offers slots, books them, reschedules or cancels when asked, takes a deposit if you require one and sends reminders before the visit. Questions outside its information go to a person.

The rules your receptionist works by

Availability

Free times come from your calendar, so it only offers slots you can actually take, with each service's length allowed for.

Rescheduling

"Can I move to Thursday?" It offers the nearest free times, moves the booking and tells your team.

Cancellations

It cancels in the chat, applies your cancellation policy as written and frees the slot.

Deposits

If you require one, it sends a payment link from your payment provider and only confirms once it's paid.

Reminders

A reminder before the appointment, with an easy way to confirm or move it.

No-reply follow-up

If someone asked for a slot and went silent, a polite follow-up, which stops the moment they reply.

A change of plan, sorted without a phone call

In this example the customer wrote on a Sunday evening. It got an answer that evening: the assistant offered real free times, moved the booking and released the old slot. Your team saw the change in the calendar and an alert on WhatsApp.

If your cancellation policy had applied, the assistant would have said so in your words, not its own.

Bookings taken while the front desk is closed

Requests that arrive at night or on a day off are answered in seconds, in English or Arabic, and booked into real free times. Your team starts the morning with a calendar that's already filled.

Each new booking, change or cancellation also reaches the owner or front desk as a WhatsApp alert, so nobody is surprised by a new name in the diary.

Questions about the AI receptionist

Which calendar does it book into?

It reads free times from your calendar and writes confirmed bookings back to it. We connect your calendar during setup and check the rules for each service's length.

Can customers reschedule or cancel by themselves?

Yes. They ask in the chat, the assistant offers free times or cancels under your policy, and your team gets a WhatsApp alert about the change.

Can it take a deposit before confirming?

Yes, if you want one. It sends a card payment link from your payment provider and confirms the booking once it's paid.

Does it take bookings at night?

Yes. Requests that arrive after hours are answered in seconds and booked into real free times, and your team gets a WhatsApp alert for each new booking.
