Short answer

An AI receptionist answers your WhatsApp enquiries in seconds, 24/7, in English or Arabic, using your own services, timings and policies. For appointment-based businesses such as clinics, salons, spas and consultants, M2S qualifies each enquiry, offers free times from your calendar, confirms the booking in the chat, sends reminders before the visit and follows up people who asked but didn't book. Anything that needs a person goes to your team with an instant alert.

The problem

Why the front desk can't keep up with ad enquiries

Most bookings now start with a WhatsApp message, and the questions repeat all day: what does it involve, who is available, do you have anything this evening, where are you. Each one interrupts a receptionist who is also checking people in and answering the phone, so replies slip from minutes to hours.

Then the day ends. Ads keep running at night, and people comparing options message three or four places at once; the one that replies first usually gets the booking. The enquiries that do get answered often stop at "I'll let you know", and nobody has time to follow up or chase the no-shows.

FAQ

Common questions about the AI receptionist

Can an AI receptionist book real appointments?

Yes. With your calendar connected, it only offers times you're actually free, confirms the booking in the chat and adds it to your calendar. Your team sees every booking.

Does it reply in Arabic?

Yes. It replies in Arabic, including Khaleeji dialect, and in English, in whichever language the customer writes in.

Will it give medical advice to patients?

No. It handles front-desk questions such as services, timings, directions and availability. Anything clinical is passed to your staff with an instant WhatsApp alert and a summary of the chat.

Do I need a new WhatsApp number?

No. You keep your own number and can still use the WhatsApp app. On the official WhatsApp API, messages sent more than 24 hours after the customer's last message use a Meta-approved template, which we set up.

What happens when someone asks and doesn't book?

They get polite timed follow-ups. Follow-ups stop the moment a customer buys or replies, never go out at night, stop for anyone who says "stop", and run once per customer per week.

M2S is one fixed monthly fee based on your ad budget, your channels and the number of services and calendars we connect, quoted after a free audit. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta.
