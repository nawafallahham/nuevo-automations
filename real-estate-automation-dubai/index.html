Real estate marketing in Dubai that books viewings, not just leads

Property leads in Dubai go to whoever replies first with the right questions. We run the project and listing ads, and M2S answers every enquiry on WhatsApp in seconds, qualifies it the way your agents would, and books the viewing with the right agent.

Short answer

For Dubai agencies and developers, we run project and listing ads on Instagram and Facebook that open a WhatsApp chat, and connect your portal and website leads to the same place. M2S, our Message to Sale system, answers each one in seconds in English or Arabic, asks about budget, area, property type, timeline, cash or mortgage and buy or rent, books a viewing or call with the right agent and alerts them to hot leads. Leads who go quiet get polite follow-ups, and you get a monthly report showing which ads produced viewings.

The problem

Why Dubai property leads go cold

A buyer or tenant browsing projects sends the same enquiry to several agencies within a few minutes. The agency that replies first, and asks sensible questions, usually gets the viewing. But agents are at viewings, on calls or driving between communities, and the WhatsApp enquiry sits unread. When someone finally calls back, the lead has already booked with someone else or stopped answering.

The other cost is time spent on the wrong leads. Agents call every enquiry to find that many are browsing, looking in a different area or budget, or a year away from moving. Qualification is repetitive and predictable. Your agents' time should go to the leads who have already said "2 beds in JVC, around 110k a year, moving next month, can view on Thursday". And at the end of the month, it's hard to say which campaign brought the viewings and which only brought clicks.

What it looks like

Enquiry to viewing, in the buyer's language

The buyer wrote in Arabic at nine in the evening, so the assistant answered in Arabic straight away. In four messages it found out the purpose (living, not investment), the bedrooms, the budget and the payment method, checked them against the project details you gave it, and booked a viewing slot from the agent's calendar.

The agent gets a WhatsApp alert with a short summary: end-user buyer, 2 bedrooms, Dubai Hills, around AED 2M, cash, viewing Saturday 11 AM. If the buyer had asked about a unit, price or payment plan that isn't in your information, or wanted to negotiate, the assistant would not guess. The agent would get an instant alert with the chat summary and take it from there.

Follow-ups

Keeping quiet property leads warm

Many property leads aren't saying no, they're just not ready this week. M2S follows up with leads who asked about a project or listing and went silent, and with leads who were qualified but didn't book a viewing. Each message is written from what they actually asked, so a tenant looking for a 2-bed in JVC hears about JVC, not about off-plan villas.

Every follow-up stops the moment the lead replies or books, and the assistant or the agent picks up the conversation with the full history. Nothing goes out at night, anyone who says "stop" is never messaged again, and each lead gets at most one sequence a week. The assistant only mentions listings and projects you've given it, so it never offers a unit that isn't available.

Housing ads on Meta have targeting limits, so we plan audiences and creative to work within them.

FAQ

Common questions from agencies

What does it ask a property lead?

The questions your agents would ask: buy or rent, budget, area or community, property type, bedrooms, cash or mortgage, and move-in timeline. We agree the list with you and can change it any time.

Can it book viewings with the right agent?

Yes. It offers slots from the calendar of the agent who covers that area, project or language, confirms the booking, and sends the agent a WhatsApp alert with a summary of the chat.

Does it work with portal leads?

Yes, wherever the portal or your CRM can forward leads, by email, webhook or integration. We connect each source during onboarding and confirm it works before it counts in your report.

How do you handle Meta's housing ad rules?

Real estate ads on Meta run in the housing category, which limits some targeting options. We run every campaign within those rules and rely on the ad creative, the offer and the conversion data sent back to Meta to reach the right people.

Will it make up listings or prices?

No. It only answers from the projects, listings and payment plans you give us. Anything it can't answer goes to an agent with an instant WhatsApp alert and a summary of the chat.

How much does it cost?

M2S is one fixed monthly fee based on your ad budget, your channels and the number of agents and projects we route leads to, quoted in writing after a free 20-minute audit. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta.
