Short answer

Real estate lead automation is software that responds to property enquiries, qualifies them and passes the good ones to agents. In Dubai it typically means replying on WhatsApp to portal and ad leads, asking about budget, area, off-plan or ready, and timing, booking a viewing or call, and routing the lead to the agent who covers it.

Routing and hand-off

Each lead goes to the agent who covers it

We set routing rules with you: by community, by project, by language or by listing agent. The chosen agent gets a WhatsApp alert with the buyer's answers, so the first call starts from what the buyer already said.

Leads that aren't ready yet stay in the pipeline and get polite follow-ups, which stop as soon as they reply.

Longer decisions

Keeping not-yet-ready buyers warm

Property decisions take time. A buyer waiting for a launch, a bonus or a visa may not be ready for months. They stay in the pipeline with their answers saved, and get follow-ups about the areas and budget they gave, using Meta-approved templates once the 24-hour window has passed.

Follow-ups stop when the buyer replies, never go out at night, end for anyone who says stop, and run at most once a week. When a buyer comes back, the agent gets an alert with their history.

FAQ

Questions from property agencies

Can it handle leads from property portals?

Yes. Portal enquiries are sent to WhatsApp and answered in seconds, the same way as leads from our project and listing ads.

How does it qualify buyers?

It asks the questions your agents would: budget, preferred area, off-plan or ready, timing, and whether they're buying to live in or invest.

How are leads routed to agents?

By rules we agree with you, such as community, project, language or listing agent. The agent gets a WhatsApp alert with the buyer's answers and a chat summary.

Can it book viewings?

Yes. It offers free times from the right agent's calendar, books the viewing or a call, and sends a reminder.
