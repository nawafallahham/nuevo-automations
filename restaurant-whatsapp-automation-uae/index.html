Short answer

Restaurant WhatsApp automation is an assistant that answers menu questions and takes orders and reservations in WhatsApp chats. It works from your actual menu, handles many chats at once during busy periods, takes direct delivery or pickup orders alongside your delivery-app listings, books tables and passes larger catering or group orders to staff.

Direct orders

Direct WhatsApp orders, alongside your delivery apps

Keep your delivery-app listings for discovery, and give regulars a direct way to order from you on WhatsApp. The assistant takes the order, confirms the address or pickup time, and closes it with a card payment link from your payment provider or cash on delivery.

During the rush it answers every chat at once, so orders don't stack up unread while your team cooks and serves. Each confirmed order reaches the kitchen or manager on WhatsApp.

Repeat orders

Bringing regulars back between visits

A customer who ordered once is easy to invite back. With their opt-in from the original chat, M2S can send an occasional message about a new dish or a weekday offer, using a Meta-approved template, and take the repeat order straight away.

Each customer gets at most one sequence a week, nothing goes out late at night, and anyone who replies stop is never messaged again. The monthly report shows which ads and offers led to orders.

FAQ

Questions from restaurant owners

Does this replace delivery apps?

No. Many restaurants keep their delivery-app listings and add direct WhatsApp orders for regulars and ad customers. M2S handles the WhatsApp side.

Can it handle a lot of chats during the dinner rush?

Yes. It answers every chat at once, in seconds, so orders don't wait while your team is cooking and serving.

Can it answer allergen questions?

Only from what you've written in your menu information. Anything it isn't sure about goes to your staff with a chat summary.

Can it take catering and group orders?

It collects the details, such as date, guest numbers and dishes, and hands catering enquiries to your manager. Group orders can be confirmed and paid in the chat.

Can customers book a table?

Yes. It takes party size, time and any requests, books the table from your free times and confirms in the chat.
