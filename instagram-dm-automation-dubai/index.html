The problem

Why Instagram enquiries go cold in Dubai

In Dubai, a lot of buying starts on Instagram. Someone sees a reel or an ad, comments "price?", and messages two or three other businesses at the same time. The one that answers first, in the customer's language, usually gets the sale. Meanwhile your team is replying to DMs one by one between everything else, and comments pile up under posts where nobody follows them up.

Ads make it worse before they make it better. Every boost brings more comments, more DMs and more "how much?" messages, spread across Instagram and WhatsApp, with nobody sure who has answered whom. When the campaign ends, there's no clear picture of which ad brought buyers and which only brought likes.

Instagram's rules

What Instagram automation can and can't do

Instagram automation runs inside Meta's rules, and it's worth knowing them before you start. We set everything up to stay within them:

  • Professional account. You need an Instagram professional account, either Business or Creator.
  • One private reply per comment. Meta allows one private message per comment, sent within 7 days of the comment.
  • 24-hour window. DM replies go out within 24 hours of the customer's last message.
  • No Instagram broadcasts. The assistant replies to people who comment or message you. Offers and follow-ups to a list happen on WhatsApp instead.

That last rule is why we often send serious buyers to WhatsApp. Once the conversation is there, M2S can follow up politely: it stops when they buy or reply, never messages at night, stops for anyone who says "stop", and runs one sequence per customer per week.

FAQ

Common questions about Instagram automation

How do comment replies by DM work?

We set a comment rule on your posts, reels and ads: every comment, or only words like price, link or available. Each commenter gets a short public reply and a private message with the answer, and the assistant carries on the conversation in the DM.

Should my Instagram ads open WhatsApp or DMs?

It depends on what you sell. WhatsApp is better when the sale needs follow-ups, payment links or reminders; DMs suit quick questions from people who like to stay on Instagram. We choose per campaign, and both land in the same pipeline.

Can the assistant follow up on Instagram?

Only within Meta's rules. Instagram replies go out within 24 hours of the customer's last message, and there are no broadcasts on Instagram. That's why the assistant invites serious buyers to continue on WhatsApp, where polite follow-ups are possible.

Does it reply in Arabic?

Yes. It replies in English or Arabic, in the language the customer writes in, and switches if they do.
