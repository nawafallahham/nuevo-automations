Instagram DM automation in Dubai, from comment to WhatsApp checkout

Instagram is where people discover you; a "price?" comment or a story reply is where they start buying. We answer those in the DM, then carry the conversation to WhatsApp when it's time to pay or book.

Book a free audit Ask on WhatsApp

Instagram DM automation means answering direct messages, story replies and post comments automatically, usually with an AI assistant. It covers ads that open a DM, comments that trigger a private reply, and replies to stories. For businesses that close sales on WhatsApp, the DM can hand the customer over to WhatsApp for the payment link or booking.

Three ways a buyer starts talking on Instagram

Ads that open a DM

Click-to-message ads that open Instagram Direct, for audiences who'd rather stay in the app.

Comments on posts and reels

Someone writes "price?" under a reel. They get a private DM reply with the answer, not a public price war in the comments.

Replies to stories

A reaction or a question on a story becomes a conversation, answered in seconds.

When an ad should open a DM, and when it should open WhatsApp

Both are Meta ad formats, and we run both. A DM keeps someone inside Instagram, which suits browsing audiences, new followers and low-commitment questions. WhatsApp suits customers who are close to buying, because that's where the payment link, the cash-on-delivery order or the booking lives in M2S.

We usually test both and let the monthly report show which brings more sales for your products, since the click is only half the story.

Why the sale moves from the DM to WhatsApp

Checkout, follow-ups and the sales pipeline run on WhatsApp in M2S. When a DM conversation reaches the buying point, the assistant offers to continue on WhatsApp with a single tap, and the context comes too, so the customer isn't asked the same questions twice.

From there, the WhatsApp rules apply: follow-ups stop when the customer replies or buys, nothing goes out at night, anyone who says stop is left alone, and each customer gets at most one sequence a week.

The assistant never invents a price. If a DM asks about something not in your information, such as a custom colour, your team gets an alert with a summary.

DMs and WhatsApp chats in one pipeline

Whether a buyer started with a comment, a story reply, a DM ad or a WhatsApp ad, they land in the same M2S pipeline, tagged with where they came from. Your team sees one list of leads instead of checking two apps, and the monthly report compares Instagram entry points by the sales they produced.

Your team can still reply from the Instagram app or the WhatsApp app at any point; the assistant steps back when a person takes over.

Questions about Instagram DM automation

Can it reply to Instagram comments with a private message?

Yes. When someone comments a question like "price?" on a post or reel, the assistant sends them a private DM with the answer.

Should my ads open Instagram DMs or WhatsApp?

It depends on the audience and the product. DMs suit people still browsing; WhatsApp suits people ready to buy, because checkout runs there. We often test both and compare sales in the monthly report.

Why move the conversation to WhatsApp?

Because the payment link, cash-on-delivery order, booking and follow-ups run on WhatsApp in M2S. The assistant offers the move at the buying point and carries the context across.

Does it reply to story replies?

Yes. Replies and questions on your stories are answered in the DM in seconds, in English or Arabic.
