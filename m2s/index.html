What is M2S?

M2S (Message to Sale) is a done-for-you service from Nuevo Automations, a Sharjah-registered marketing agency. We run Meta click-to-WhatsApp ads, and an AI sales assistant replies to every message in seconds in English or Arabic, takes the order or booking in the chat, follows up with abandoned carts, unpaid links and quiet leads, and reports the results every month.

4 Follow-ups that win the sale back

This is where most of the money is

M2S follows up automatically with three kinds of customers who were about to buy:

  • Abandoned carts. Within 30 minutes, a friendly WhatsApp reminder with their items and a link back to checkout. If they still don't buy, a second message offers to finish the order in the chat. A third message with a discount code is optional.
  • Unpaid payment links. The customer asked for a link and didn't pay. They get a gentle reminder.
  • Quiet leads. The customer asked the price and went silent. Timed follow-ups, for example after 4 hours, 1 day and 3 days, restart the conversation.

Smart and polite by design. Follow-ups stop the moment the customer buys or replies, never go out at night, stop for anyone who says "stop", and run once per customer per week.

FAQ

Questions about M2S

Who is M2S for?

UAE businesses that sell through conversations: online stores, clinics, salons and spas, real estate, education and training, and restaurants. It suits businesses that already run Meta ads, or are ready to, and want more of those messages to turn into sales.

Do I keep my own WhatsApp number?

Yes. The AI sales assistant runs on your own WhatsApp number, and your team can keep using the WhatsApp app to step in at any time.

What does the AI do when it doesn't know an answer?

It never invents prices or products. Anything it can't answer goes straight to your team with an instant WhatsApp alert and a summary of the chat.

How is M2S priced?

One fixed monthly fee, based on your ad budget, your channels and how many products or services we load, quoted in writing after a free 20-minute audit. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta.

How do I know it's working?

Every chat is tagged with the ad it came from, and you get a monthly M2S Results report: conversations, orders, revenue, reply speed and the revenue recovered by follow-ups.
