How we turn ad messages into sales

Nuevo Automations runs your ads straight into WhatsApp instead of a landing page, and M2S answers every message they bring and works it toward a sale. An AI sales assistant replies on WhatsApp in seconds, and automatic follow-ups win back the customers who went silent or left their cart.

What is M2S?

M2S (Message to Sale) is a done-for-you service from Nuevo Automations, a Sharjah-registered marketing agency. We run Meta click-to-WhatsApp ads and Google search ads, and an AI sales assistant replies to every message in seconds in English or Arabic, takes the order or booking in the chat, follows up with abandoned carts, unpaid links and leads that went silent, and reports the results every month.

The five steps of M2S

  1. 1. Ads to WhatsApp

    Every lead lands in one place.

  2. 2. Instant AI reply

    Seconds, 24/7, English or Arabic.

  3. 3. Qualify and close

    Order, payment or booking in the chat.

  4. 4. Follow up

    Carts, unpaid links, silent leads.

  5. 5. Results

    What you paid, what came back.

Step 1: ads that open a WhatsApp conversation

We run Meta ads on Instagram and Facebook that open a WhatsApp chat with your business ("click to WhatsApp"), and Google search ads that reach people already looking for what you sell. We also connect your website chat and forms, so every lead lands in one place.

  • Every chat is tagged with the ad it came from
  • Purchases are sent back to Meta through the pixel and Conversions API, so Meta finds more people who buy
  • Creative, targeting and budget managed by us, and reported to you every month

Step 2: answered in seconds, at any hour

Your AI sales assistant answers in seconds, any time of day, in English or Arabic. It knows your products, prices, delivery areas and policies, and it talks like a real member of your team.

  • Answers questions, recommends the right product and handles objections
  • Never invents prices or products
  • Anything it can't answer goes straight to your team

Step 3: the sale happens where the conversation is

The assistant qualifies the lead, moves it through your sales pipeline, and closes the sale right in the chat.

  • Takes the order, the delivery address and the payment choice
  • Sends a secure card payment link, or confirms cash on delivery
  • For service businesses, books the appointment or consultation in your calendar
  • When a hot lead needs a human, your team gets a WhatsApp alert straight away, with a summary of the chat

Step 4: most people who go quiet haven't said no

M2S follows up automatically with three kinds of customers who were about to buy:

  • Abandoned carts

    Within 30 minutes, a friendly WhatsApp reminder with their items and a link back to checkout. If they still don't buy, a second message offers to finish the order in the chat. A third message with a discount code is optional.

  • Unpaid payment links

    The customer asked for a link and didn't pay. They get a gentle reminder.

  • Quiet leads

    The customer asked the price and went silent. Timed follow-ups, for example after 4 hours, 1 day and 3 days, restart the conversation.

Follow-up rules. Follow-ups stop the moment the customer buys or replies, never go out at night, stop for anyone who says "stop", and run once per customer per week.

Step 5: a monthly M2S Results report

You see exactly what you paid for and what came back.

  • Conversations started, and how many came from ads
  • Qualified leads, orders and revenue
  • How fast the assistant replied
  • Recovered revenue: money won back from abandoned carts, unpaid links and follow-ups

Built on our own platform

M2S runs on OrcaFlo, the WhatsApp AI platform we built and run ourselves. No patchwork of third-party tools.

Official WhatsApp numbers

On the official WhatsApp API, messages sent more than 24 hours after a customer's last message need a Meta-approved template. We set those up for you.

Switched on store by store

Cart recovery connects to your store during onboarding. We confirm it's live for your store before it counts in your report.

Questions about M2S

Message us on WhatsApp

Who is M2S for?

UAE businesses that sell through conversations: online stores, clinics, salons and spas, real estate, education and training, and restaurants. It suits businesses that already run Meta or Google ads, or are ready to, and want more of those messages to turn into sales.

Do I keep my own WhatsApp number?

Yes. The AI sales assistant runs on your own WhatsApp number, and your team can keep using the WhatsApp app to step in at any time.

What does the M2S assistant do when it doesn't know an answer?

It never invents prices or products. Anything it can't answer goes straight to your team with an instant WhatsApp alert and a summary of the chat.

How is M2S priced?

One fixed monthly fee, based on your ad budget, your channels and how many products or services we load, quoted in writing after a free 30-minute audit. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta or Google.

How do I know M2S is working?

Every chat is tagged with the ad it came from, and you get a monthly M2S Results report: conversations, orders, revenue, reply speed and the revenue recovered by follow-ups.
