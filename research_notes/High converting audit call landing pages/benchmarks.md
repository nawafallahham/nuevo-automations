# Conversion-Rate Benchmarks for Lead-Gen / Book-a-Call Landing Pages (Meta ad traffic)

Context: B2B done-for-you service (AI WhatsApp sales assistant + Meta ads) selling to UAE wellness/supplement e-commerce brands. Landing page goal is booking a free 30-minute audit call through an embedded Calendly. Research date: 20 Nov 2026.

Source-quality legend: [PRIMARY] = publisher's own data page; [VENDOR] = the publisher sells the tool/service the data flatters; [SECONDARY] = aggregator citing someone else; [STALE] = data from before 2022.

## Q1. Unbounce Conversion Benchmark Report: medians, top quartile, reading level, word count, traffic source

### Takeaway
The latest Unbounce edition is the 2024 report (data from 23 Jul 2023 to 23 Jul 2024; 41K+ pages, 464M+ visitors, 57M+ conversions). It gives a 6.6% all-industry median and a 6.1% median for Commercial & Professional Services, with the top quartile at 14.1%+. For professional services, paid social converts much worse than paid search (median about 4.4-4.7% vs 7.8%). Instagram beats Facebook by about 2x. Short, simple copy (275-745 words, 5th-7th grade reading level) roughly doubles the median. I found no newer Unbounce edition as of Nov 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **Edition and sample:** 2024 Conversion Benchmark Report. 41K+ landing pages, 464M+ unique visitors, 57M+ conversions. Data window 23 Jul 2023 to 23 Jul 2024. Pages with no conversions or fewer than 50 visitors were excluded. The report mixes medians and averages and labels each data point. [PRIMARY/VENDOR: Unbounce sells landing pages] — [Unbounce methodology](https://unbounce.com/conversion-benchmark-report/methodology/); [Unbounce report hub](https://unbounce.com/conversion-benchmark-report/)
- **All-industry median: 6.6%.** Industry medians range from 3.8% to 12.3% across nine industries. SaaS median 3.8%. Financial services about 8.3%. — [Unbounce report hub](https://unbounce.com/conversion-benchmark-report/); [Search Engine Land summary](https://searchengineland.com/how-does-your-landing-page-performance-compare-453224)
- **Commercial & Professional Services (closest category to an agency/B2B service): median 6.1%. Top 25% convert at 14.1% or higher.** Repair and maintenance pages convert about 3x better than home and renovation pages. The report has no separate "agency", "marketing services" or "B2B services" sub-category. — [Unbounce Professional Services page](https://unbounce.com/conversion-benchmark-report/professional-services-conversion-rate/)
- **Prof. services, by traffic source:** email 13.9% (best). Paid search 7.8%. Paid social 4.4% (the page also states a 4.7% paid-social median, so it is internally inconsistent). Paid search converts 77% better than paid social in this industry. 75% of Google search ad pages convert at 3.6% or higher. — [Unbounce Professional Services page](https://unbounce.com/conversion-benchmark-report/professional-services-conversion-rate/)
- **Prof. services, paid social by platform:** Instagram 11.7% (highest), nearly 2x Facebook (runner-up). Most Instagram pages convert at 3.4% or higher. YouTube 1.2%. — [Unbounce Professional Services page](https://unbounce.com/conversion-benchmark-report/professional-services-conversion-rate/)
- **All industries, by channel (labelled "average", not median, so outliers pull these up):** email 19.3%, paid social 12%, paid search 10.9%. Instagram 17.9% and Facebook 13% inside paid social. LinkedIn converts 4-5x worse than FB/IG. Google 11.3%. The Prof. Services page notes that across industries paid social converts about 10% better than paid search, which is the reverse of the professional-services pattern. — [Unbounce report hub](https://unbounce.com/conversion-benchmark-report/); [Unbounce Professional Services page](https://unbounce.com/conversion-benchmark-report/professional-services-conversion-rate/)
- **Reading level, all industries:** copy at 5th-7th grade level converts at 11.1%, 56% higher than 8th-9th grade copy. Professional-level copy converts at 5.3%. Difficult words (3+ syllables) correlate -24.3% with conversion, up from -15% in 2020. — [Unbounce report hub](https://unbounce.com/conversion-benchmark-report/)
- **Reading level, prof. services:** 5th-7th grade median 12.9% vs 8th-9th grade 6.6% (-49%). Recommendation: 7th grade or below. — [Unbounce Professional Services page](https://unbounce.com/conversion-benchmark-report/professional-services-conversion-rate/)
- **Word count:** across all industries, word count correlates -18.6% and reading time -19.4% with conversion. For prof. services the best range is 275-745 words, with about 500 words suggested. Keep difficult words to 45-120 in total and no more than about 15% of the copy. — [Unbounce report hub](https://unbounce.com/conversion-benchmark-report/); [Unbounce Professional Services page](https://unbounce.com/conversion-benchmark-report/professional-services-conversion-rate/)
- **Device:** 83% of visits across all industries were mobile, and desktop converts about 8% better. Prof. services: 81% mobile, desktop 11.6% vs mobile 8.3%. The page says "40% lower", but its own figures work out to about 28% lower. — [Unbounce report hub](https://unbounce.com/conversion-benchmark-report/); [Unbounce Professional Services page](https://unbounce.com/conversion-benchmark-report/professional-services-conversion-rate/)
- **The trend is falling:** the 2024 press release frames the edition as "attention spans are declining, and so are conversion rates". Older editions use different methods: the 2020 report gave a 3.2% median, so editions are not directly comparable. [STALE for 2020] — [PR Newswire, 2024](https://prnewswire.com/news-releases/unbounces-2024-conversion-benchmark-report-proves-that-attention-spans-are-declining-and-so-are-conversion-rates-302239407.html); [Unbounce 2020 report](https://unbounce.com/conversion-benchmark-report-2020/)

### Inferences
- Unbounce "conversion" covers any goal: email opt-ins, downloads and form fills. Booking a 30-minute call with a stranger asks far more of the visitor, so the 6.1% professional-services median is an upper reference, not a target. For Meta (paid social) traffic to a professional-services page, about 4.4-4.7% is the closer median, and 14.1% is top-quartile territory across all channels.
- The traffic will be mostly mobile and Instagram/Facebook. That makes mobile-first layout, short copy (~500 words) and plain 5th-7th grade language the evidence-backed levers. Simple language matters more for a UAE audience that often reads English as a second language. That last point is my inference and has no source.

### Gaps
- Unbounce publishes no "agency", "marketing services" or "B2B services" sub-category, and no figures specific to booking/calendar conversions.
- The Unbounce pages give no sample size per industry or channel.
- The report gives no geographic (MENA) breakdown.

## Q2. Other benchmark sets: WordStream/LocaliQ Meta ads, First Page Sage, Ruler, demo/booking benchmarks

### Takeaway
2026 WordStream/LocaliQ data puts Meta lead campaigns at 2.70% CTR, $1.80 CPC, 8.54% conversion rate and $27.39 CPL across all industries. Industrial & Commercial runs a 4.50% conversion rate at $35.87 CPL. Ruler's attribution data, which tracks real qualified leads, shows paid social converting far worse: 2.11% overall, 1.1% for Professional Services, 0.6% for Marketing & Advertising. B2B demo-request pages are usually cited at about 2-5%.

### Cited Findings
- **WordStream/LocaliQ Facebook Ads Benchmarks 2026 (page updated 23 Sep 2026; 20 industries; sample size not stated on the page) [VENDOR: LocaliQ sells ad management]:**
  - Traffic campaigns, all industries: CTR 1.93% (from 1.71%), CPC $0.60 (from $0.70).
  - Lead campaigns, all industries: CTR 2.70% (from 2.59%), CPC $1.80 (from $1.92), conversion rate 8.54% (from 7.72%), CPL $27.39 (from $27.66).
  - Business Services, traffic campaigns: CTR 1.64%, CPC $0.70. No lead-campaign row is published for Business Services.
  - Industrial & Commercial, lead campaigns: CTR 2.57%, CPC $1.55, CVR 4.50%, CPL $35.87.
  - Health & Fitness, lead campaigns (relevant to the client's own customers): CTR 3.09%, CPC $1.84, CVR 7.98%, CPL $27.11. Beauty & Personal Care: CTR 1.35%, CPC $2.97, CVR 5.63%, CPL $50.91.
  — [LocaliQ Facebook advertising benchmarks](https://localiq.com/blog/facebook-advertising-benchmarks/)
- The 2025 edition of the same study reported a 21% year-over-year rise in Facebook CPL to $27.66. — [WordStream 2025 benchmarks](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/facebook-ads-benchmarks-2025) (403 when fetched; figure confirmed through [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/facebook-ad-costs-jump-beat-google-461690) and [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/facebook-ad-costs-jump-21-as-lead-campaigns-struggle-while-traffic-ads-thrive/))
- A widely repeated "Business Services CPL $16.95, CVR 8.34%" figure is attributed to an older WordStream report. It comes only from secondary sites and is unverified. Do not use it. — [webtonic (secondary)](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/facebook-ads-benchmarks)
- **Ruler Analytics "Conversion Rate Benchmarks for 2027" (published 13 Nov 2026; 110M+ sessions, 5M+ conversions, £33.8M+ tracked spend, 13 industries; a conversion is a qualified lead or sale by form or call) [VENDOR: attribution software]:** overall average 5.13%. Paid social 2.11% overall, 1.1% for Professional Services, 0.6% for Marketing & Advertising. Paid search 5.4% overall, 6.7% for Professional Services, 8.9% for Marketing & Advertising. — [Ruler Analytics](https://ruleranalytics.com/blog/insight/conversion-rate-by-industry/)
- **First Page Sage:** about 1.1% visitor-to-lead for B2B SaaS, and about 2-5% for B2B demo-request pages (2026), with a median B2B rate of 2.9%. I could not reach a primary First Page Sage page; all figures come through secondary sites, which disagree. [SECONDARY] — [Komo glossary](https://komo.ai/glossary/landing-page-conversion); [Huemor](https://huemor.rocks/blog/b2b-website-conversion-rate-benchmarks/)
- **Form-to-meeting (booking after a form):**
  - Chili Piper: about 4M form submissions (2024 data). 66.7% of qualified submitters booked when shown instant scheduling, vs about 30% without it. [VENDOR] — [Chili Piper report](https://www.chilipiper.com/post/form-conversion-rate-benchmark-report) (figures via [ainora.lt compilation](https://ainora.lt/blog/b2b-meeting-booking-rate-every-study-2026))
  - RevenueHero 2026: median qualified-to-booked rate 62%, top 10% at 78%+. SMB 63.2%, Ecommerce Tech 68.8%. Lead response odds fall from about 80% in the first minute to about 40% the next day. No sample size is given; a related post cites 1M+ B2B SaaS form submissions. [VENDOR] — [RevenueHero](https://www.revenuehero.io/blog/median-demo-hold-rates-by-funnel-type)
  - Default: 51,219 demo requests across 100 B2B companies, 75.6% booked (67.3% for UTM-tagged traffic). The year is not stated. [VENDOR] — [Default source analysis](https://www.default.com/reports/source-analysis)
- I found no primary HubSpot, Databox, Instapage or Leadpages benchmark with a stated sample size for book-a-call pages. I did not fetch these.

### Inferences
- The Meta "lead campaign conversion rate" (8.54%) mostly measures native instant forms, which ask far less than a Calendly booking. It is not comparable to a landing-page booking rate.
- Three sources point the same way: Ruler (1.1% paid social for professional services), Unbounce (4.4-4.7% paid social median for professional services) and First Page Sage (2-5% for demo pages). For cold Meta traffic to a B2B call-booking page, a realistic visit-to-booked-call range is roughly 1-5%. About 3%+ is good, and 5%+ is strong. This range is my synthesis, not a published figure.
- Embedding the scheduler, rather than linking out or using a form followed by a later email, is supported by Chili Piper's 30% vs 66.7% gap. That gap is vendor data about qualified form submitters, not raw visitors.

### Gaps
- No primary 2025-2026 WordStream/LocaliQ lead-campaign row for "Business Services" exists. No sample size is published.
- I found no Calendly-published data on conversion rates for embedded vs linked schedulers.

## Q3. Funnel-stage benchmarks: visit to booked call, booked to show, show to close

### Takeaway
No authoritative, methodology-backed benchmark exists for show rate or close rate on high-ticket B2B strategy calls. The commonly cited ranges are about 70-85% show rate and about 15-25% close rate on held calls. These come from vendor blogs and agency playbooks. The best-supported pattern is that show rate falls steeply with booking lead time.

### Cited Findings
- **Calendly:** its only published figure is that sales users cut no-show rates by 28% on average with automated reminders. That is a relative reduction, not an absolute no-show rate. [VENDOR] — [Calendly blog](https://calendly.com/blog/reduce-no-show-rates-sales)
- **Show rate vs booking lead time (single company, anecdotal):** same-day bookings showed 82%, 1-3 days out 79%, 7+ days out 58%. The same company's no-show rate was 18% with reminders and fell to 7% later. [VENDOR anecdote] — [Cotera](https://cotera.co/articles/meeting-no-show-tracking-ai)
- **SegMetrics playbook:** sets a 70-85% show-rate target. Same-day and next-day bookings show 80%+, while bookings more than 5 days out fall below 50% without nurturing. The year is not stated. [VENDOR] — [SegMetrics PDF](https://segmetrics.io/wp-content/uploads/Playbook-Sales-Call-Show-Rate.pdf)
- **Outsourced SDR appointments (2024):** average show rate 73%, with under 65% flagged as weak. Appointment-to-close 4.8%, with under 3% flagged. These measure outbound-set meetings, not inbound bookings. [VENDOR] — [The Starr Conspiracy 2024](https://www.thestarrconspiracy.com/insights/benchmarks/outsourced-b2b-lead-generation-benchmarks)
- A 2026 "scheduling benchmarks" press piece reports a median show rate of 77% and 87-90% for the top 10%. The original publisher could not be confirmed. Unverified. — [mirror](https://lifestyle.kbew98country.com/story/63990/calendar-scheduling-benchmarks-report-on-booking-conversion-and-show-rates-in-2026)
- A compilation of B2B booking studies states that none of them report show or no-show rates, and that the commonly quoted "60-80% show rate" has no source. — [ainora.lt](https://ainora.lt/blog/b2b-meeting-booking-rate-every-study-2026)
- **Close rates:** Forrester found 9.3 vs 4.6 closed/won per 1,000 inquiries with vs without a sales-accepted-lead stage. SiriusDecisions puts inquiry-to-close at 0.5-1%; the year is not stated and the data is likely [STALE]. — via [ainora.lt](https://ainora.lt/blog/b2b-meeting-booking-rate-every-study-2026); [Forrester blog](https://go.forrester.com/blogs/measuringtheimpactofsuccessfulsaleshandoffs/)
- **Speed to lead:** HBR 2011 (100,000+ leads, 2,241 companies) found 21x higher qualification when leads were contacted within 5 minutes instead of 30. [STALE] — [HBR](https://hbr.org/2011/03/the-short-life-of-online-sales-leads)

### Inferences
- Working planning assumptions (synthesis, not published benchmarks): visit to booked call about 1-5% from cold Meta traffic, booked to held about 65-80%, held to closed about 15-25% for a done-for-you retainer. Capping Calendly availability to 1-3 days out and sending a WhatsApp confirmation plus reminder are the best-supported show-rate levers.
- The client sells WhatsApp automation, so WhatsApp booking confirmations and reminders double as a live product demo. This is my inference.

### Gaps
- I found no independent, large-sample no-show benchmark for Calendly or for agency strategy calls.
- I found no MENA-specific show-rate data.

## Q4. Click-to-WhatsApp ads vs landing-page ads

### Takeaway
Every comparison found is a Meta success story or a vendor case study. Meta flags several of them as self-reported. Reported cost-per-lead reductions for click-to-WhatsApp vs other approaches run from 12% to 56%. The only UAE case is Arabian Automobiles (Nissan): 56% lower CPL per Meta, or 23% lower CPL recorded in CRM per Campaign ME. No independent CTWA benchmark exists.

### Cited Findings
- **Arabian Automobiles Company (UAE):** 2x leads and 56% lower cost per lead vs its usual approach. Meta marks this as self-reported. — [WhatsApp Business success story](https://business.whatsapp.com/resources/success-stories/arabian-automobiles-company); [360dialog](https://360dialog.com/stories/nissan). Campaign ME reports a 23% lower cost per lead recorded in CRM. — [Campaign ME](https://campaignme.com/how-aac-increased-revenue-by-75-roi-by-55-with-whatsapp-marketing/)
- **Nutryfit (India and the Gulf; nutrition, adjacent to the client's wellness vertical):** 12% lower cost per lead in a Meta A/B test run 1-18 Apr 2025. — [WhatsApp Business success story](https://www.whatsappbusiness.com/resources/success-stories/nutryfit/) (via [Spur summary](https://www.spurnow.com/en/blogs/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks))
- **Tamam (Saudi lender):** more than 90% lower cost per qualified lead with CTWA plus deeper funnel measurement. [VENDOR: 360dialog] — [360dialog](https://360dialog.com/stories/tamam)
- **Bowaba (Gulf agency):** 15% lower CPL for clients. [VENDOR: Wati] — [Wati case study](https://www.wati.io/case-studies/bowaba)
- **Other Meta stories:** Braip (Brazil) 30% lower CPL. Learnwise (India) 31% lower cost per qualified lead. Engelife (Brazil) 37% lower cost per qualified lead. — via [Spur summary](https://www.spurnow.com/en/blogs/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks)
- **Meta platform data:** ads with more than one message destination had a 9.5% lower median cost per message than WhatsApp-only ads. Sample: 734,000 businesses over 3 weeks in H2 2024. — [Meta click-to-message ads page](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/click-to-message-ads) (via Spur)
- **No independent benchmarks:** Spur states there are "no independent public click to WhatsApp ads benchmarks" for CTR or cost per conversation. — [Spur](https://www.spurnow.com/en/blogs/click-to-whatsapp-ads-benchmarks)
- A UAE agency claims CTWA delivers 25-40% lower CPL than form lead gen. It shows no data. [VENDOR, unsupported] — [Clozer](https://clozer.pages.dev/blog/cpl-uae-2026.html)
- A "Forrester/Meta study: 92% drop in CPL vs landing pages" claim circulates on blogs. I could not verify it, so do not cite it.

### Inferences
- The case studies measure lead volume and CPL, not booked or held calls. A CTWA lead is usually cheaper but lower-intent than a visitor who self-books on Calendly. A fair test compares cost per held audit call, not CPL.
- Selling a WhatsApp AI product with CTWA ads ("message us, our AI books your audit") would demonstrate the product inside the funnel. This is my inference.

### Gaps
- I found no published B2B CTWA case study in which the conversion is a booked sales call.

## Q5. GCC/MENA Meta ads benchmarks (CPM, CPL, conversion)

### Takeaway
No credible, methodology-transparent UAE/KSA Meta benchmark was found. Published UAE CPM figures range from about $3 to $16, depending on the source. UAE CPL estimates come from tool vendors with opaque methods.

### Cited Findings
- **SuperAds insights tool:** UAE Facebook CPM averaged about $15.8 from Jul 2025 to Jul 2026, vs a global benchmark of about $20.6. UAE CPL averaged about $41.08 from Jul 2025 to Jun 2026, reported as falling from about $34 to about $14.7 over the window. That trend is inconsistent with the stated average. UAE retail CPL is about $24. Methodology and sample are not disclosed. [VENDOR] — [SuperAds UAE CPM](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cpm-cost-per-mille/united-arab-emirates); [SuperAds UAE CPL](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cost-per-lead/united-arab-emirates); [SuperAds UAE e-commerce CPL](https://www.superads.ai/facebook-ads-costs/cost-per-lead/e-commerce/united-arab-emirates)
- **Digital Boom 2025 MENA guide:** UAE Meta CPM $5-12 and KSA $4-9. A $2.5-6 UAE range on the same site contradicts this. [AGENCY] — [Digital Boom](https://adigitalboom.com/glossary/cpm)
- **upGrowth 2026:** GCC CPM about $3-8. [AGENCY] — [upGrowth](https://upgrowth.in/?p=63703)
- **Seasonality:** sources flag cost spikes around Ramadan, Eid and the Dubai Shopping Festival (mid-Dec to mid-Jan). [AGENCY, qualitative] — [Digital Boom](https://adigitalboom.com/?p=34918)

### Inferences
- For planning, treat UAE Meta CPMs as roughly $5-16 and B2B CPL from cold traffic as likely above the $27-36 US-centric WordStream figures, given the small B2B audience. Validate both against the account's own first 2-4 weeks of spend. This is my inference.

### Gaps
- I found no Meta-published GCC benchmarks.
- I found no independent UAE B2B landing-page conversion rates.
- I found no sample sizes for any GCC figure.
