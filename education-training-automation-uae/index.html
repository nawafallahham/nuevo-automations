Short answer

For training centres, tutors and course providers in the UAE, we run course and intake ads on Instagram and Facebook that open a WhatsApp chat. M2S, our Message to Sale system, answers every enquiry in seconds in English or Arabic, with schedules, levels and fees taken from your own course list, qualifies the student or parent, books a placement test, trial class or call, and sends a payment link when they're ready to enrol. Families who go quiet get polite follow-ups, and you get a monthly report showing which ads filled your classes.

The problem

How training centres lose enrolments

Course enquiries are a comparison exercise. A parent messages three tutoring centres on a Sunday evening asking about maths for Grade 7; an adult messages two language schools about IELTS preparation after work. The centre that answers clearly, with the right group, time and fee, and offers a placement test or trial class, is the one they visit. Centres that reply the next afternoon are often already out of the running.

The enquiry that doesn't convert straight away is the other gap. "Let me discuss it at home" or "we'll decide before term starts" is a real maybe, and most centres never follow it up because the admin team is busy with current students. Intake periods make it worse: enquiries peak at the same time as the admin work, and nobody can say which ad brought the students who actually enrolled.

Follow-ups

Following up with families who haven't enrolled

Most families who don't enrol on the first day are still deciding. M2S follows up with three groups: people who asked about a course and went quiet, people who booked a placement test or trial class but didn't enrol, and people who asked for a payment link and didn't pay. Each message is written from what they asked, so the Grade 7 parent hears about the Grade 7 group.

Every follow-up stops the moment the family replies or enrols, and the assistant picks up the conversation with the full history. Nothing goes out at night, anyone who says "stop" is never messaged again, and each family gets at most one sequence a week. Before a new term, a reminder can go to undecided families, using only the dates and places you've confirmed.

FAQ

Common questions from training centres

Can it book placement tests and trial classes?

Yes. It offers open slots from your timetable or a counsellor's calendar, confirms the booking, collects the student's details and sends a reminder before it.

Can it reply to parents in Arabic and students in English?

Yes. It replies in whichever language each person writes in, Arabic or English.

Can families pay to enrol inside the WhatsApp chat?

Yes. Once we connect your payment provider, such as Strabl or Ziina, the assistant sends a secure card payment link for the amount on your fee list, and your team sees when it's paid.

What goes to our staff?

Learning support questions about a particular child, fee disputes, refunds, complaints and anything you mark as staff-only. Your team gets an instant WhatsApp alert with a summary of the chat.

Will families get spammed?

No. Follow-ups stop the moment a family replies or enrols, never go out at night, stop for anyone who says stop, and run once per family per week.
