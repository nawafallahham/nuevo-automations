Short answer

Education and training automation means handling course enquiries, bookings and reminders in chat instead of by phone. For UAE centres, that covers answering parents and adult learners about courses, schedules and fees, booking a placement test or trial class, filling the next intake and sending reminders so booked students actually attend.

Two audiences

Parents and adult learners ask different questions

Parents

Ask about age groups, curriculum, class size, timing around school and who teaches. Often comparing centres for one child.

Adult learners

Ask about levels, evening and weekend times, exam dates, certificates and how long the course takes.

Courses and schedules

Course and timetable questions, answered from your list

We load your courses, levels, schedules, fees and intake dates. The assistant answers from that, in English or Arabic, and asks a couple of questions to point the person at the right course, such as the child's age or the learner's current level.

Anything not in your information, such as a special arrangement or a fee discount, goes to your admissions team with a summary.

Intakes and no-shows

Filling the next intake, and making sure they turn up

Intake ads bring a burst of enquiries before a start date. M2S answers all of them at once and books trial classes or placement tests into the slots you open.

Reminders go out before each booking, and families or learners who attended but haven't enrolled get a polite follow-up. None go out at night, and anyone who says stop is left alone.

Fees and enrolment

Taking the enrolment in the chat

Once a family or learner decides, the assistant confirms the course, schedule and fee from your information and sends a payment link from your payment provider. Instalments, sibling discounts or scholarships go to your admissions team, because those are your decisions to make.

Every enquiry is tagged with the course or intake ad that brought it, and the monthly report shows enrolments by ad.

FAQ

Questions from training centres

Can it tell a parent from an adult learner?

Yes. It asks a simple question early on, then answers with the right courses, times and details for a child or for an adult.

Can it book placement tests and trial classes?

Yes. It books them into the slots you open, takes the student's details and sends a reminder before the day.

How does it help with intakes?

When intake ads bring a burst of enquiries, it answers every one in seconds and fills the trial or test slots you've set for that intake.

What about students who book and don't show up?

Each booking gets a reminder with an easy way to confirm or move it, and those who attended but haven't enrolled get a polite follow-up.
