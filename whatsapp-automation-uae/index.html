WhatsApp sales automation · UAE

WhatsApp automation that turns ad messages into sales

Your ads bring the messages. M2S, our Message to Sale system, answers each one on WhatsApp in seconds, closes the sale in the chat and follows up with the people who went quiet, all on the number you already use.

Short answer

WhatsApp automation, the way we do it, is a sales system rather than an auto-reply. We run Meta ads that open a WhatsApp chat with your business, and M2S answers every message in seconds, in English or Arabic, from your real products and prices. It qualifies the lead, closes the sale with a card payment link, cash on delivery or a booking, and follows up with anyone who goes quiet. It runs on your own WhatsApp number, and a monthly report shows which ads made the sales.

The problem

Where WhatsApp sales get lost

In the UAE, buying conversations happen on WhatsApp. A customer taps your ad, sends "how much?", and messages two or three other businesses at the same time. Whoever replies first, clearly and in the customer's language, usually gets the sale. Many of those messages land late in the evening or at the weekend, when nobody on your team is watching the phone.

Even when someone does reply, the sale often slips away after the first answer. The customer gets the price and goes quiet. A payment link goes out and never gets paid. Nobody follows up, because the team is busy with today's customers, and the same price and delivery questions get typed out again and again. At the end of the month, you know what you spent on ads but not which ones brought the money in.

Follow-ups

Winning back the people who went quiet

Most people who stop replying on WhatsApp haven't said no. They got distracted, wanted to check with someone, or meant to pay later. M2S follows up with three groups: leads who asked a price and went silent, customers who asked for a payment link and didn't pay, and shoppers who left a cart on your website.

Each follow-up is written around what the customer actually asked, not a generic blast. The moment they reply, the sequence stops and the assistant picks up the conversation where it left off. Nothing goes out at night, anyone who says "stop" is never messaged again, and each customer gets at most one sequence a week.

Good to know: M2S connects through the official WhatsApp API on the number you already use. On the official API, messages sent more than 24 hours after the customer's last message need a Meta-approved template, which we write and set up for you. Abandoned cart recovery is connected during onboarding, and we confirm it's live for your store before it counts in your report.

FAQ

Common questions about WhatsApp automation

Can I keep my existing WhatsApp number?

Yes. M2S runs on the WhatsApp number your customers already know, and your team can still use the WhatsApp app on it to read and reply.

Does it reply in Arabic?

Yes. The assistant replies in English or Arabic, in the language the customer writes in, and switches if they do.

What happens when the AI doesn't know the answer?

It never invents a price or a product. Anything it can't answer goes to your team straight away, with an instant WhatsApp alert and a summary of the chat, so the customer never has to repeat themselves.

Can it message customers more than 24 hours after they wrote?

Yes, with a template. On the official WhatsApp API, messages sent more than 24 hours after the customer's last message need a Meta-approved template, and we write and set those up for you.

Will customers get spammed?

No. Follow-ups stop the moment a customer buys or replies, never go out at night, stop for anyone who says stop, and run once per customer per week.

How much does it cost?

M2S is one fixed monthly fee based on your ad budget, your channels and how many follow-up sequences you need, quoted in writing after a free 20-minute audit. Ad spend is paid directly to Meta.
