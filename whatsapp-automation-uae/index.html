WhatsApp Business API automation on the number you already use

Short answer

WhatsApp automation means replying to, qualifying and following up with customers on WhatsApp by software rather than by hand. For a business, it runs on the official WhatsApp Business API, which allows an AI assistant, team inboxes and Meta-approved message templates. Messages outside the 24-hour customer-service window must use those templates, and customers must be able to opt out.

App or API

The WhatsApp Business app and the Business API are different tools

The free WhatsApp Business app is built for one person on one phone. It has quick replies and an away message, but it can't run an AI assistant, send tagged ad chats into a pipeline or send follow-ups on a schedule.

The WhatsApp Business API is the official route for software to send and receive messages on a business number. It's what lets M2S answer in seconds, read your product list and alert your team. We set it up on the number your customers already have saved, and your team can still open the WhatsApp app on it to read and reply.

The 24-hour rule

Why the 24-hour window and approved templates matter

On the WhatsApp Business API, a business can reply freely for 24 hours after a customer's last message. After that, the only way to start the conversation again is a message template that Meta has approved in advance.

That rule is why so many follow-ups never get sent: nobody wrote the templates. During setup we write the templates your follow-ups need, in English and Arabic, submit them for approval and connect them to the right moment, such as a price asked and no reply, or a payment link left unpaid.

Consent

Opt-in, opt-out and quiet hours, handled by the system

Opt-in from the conversation

Follow-ups only go to people who messaged you first, about something they asked for.

"Stop" means stop

Anyone who says stop is never messaged again, without your team having to remember.

Quiet hours

No follow-up goes out at night, even if the timer says it's due.

One sequence a week

Each customer gets at most one follow-up sequence per week, and it ends the moment they reply or buy.

Your number

Keeping your own WhatsApp number

Your number is part of your brand: it's on your packaging, your Google profile and your customers' phones. We don't replace it with a new one. We connect that same number to the API through OrcaFlo, our own platform, and keep the WhatsApp app working beside it so your team can step into any chat.

Once it's connected, the click-to-WhatsApp ads we run open chats on that number, and every sale or booking made in the chat is reported back to Meta through the pixel and Conversions API.

Good to know: template wording is approved by Meta, not by us, so we write it plainly and in line with WhatsApp's rules. The assistant never invents prices; anything it can't answer goes to your team with a chat summary.

FAQ

Questions about the WhatsApp Business API

Do I lose my WhatsApp chat history when moving to the API?

Your customers keep the same number and their side of the conversation. During the audit we check your current setup and explain what moves over before anything changes.

Can my team still use the WhatsApp app?

Yes. M2S runs on the same number through the official API, and your team can still open the WhatsApp app on it to read and reply to any chat.

What is the 24-hour customer-service window?

After a customer's last message, a business can reply freely for 24 hours. After that, it can only restart the conversation with a message template Meta has approved. We write and set those up for you.

How do customers opt out of follow-ups?

They just say stop. Anyone who does is never messaged again, follow-ups never go out at night, and each customer gets at most one sequence per week.

Will my click-to-WhatsApp ads open chats on this number?

Yes. The ads we run open chats on your own number, each tagged with the ad that started it, and sales made in the chat are reported back to Meta.
