Short answer

Most chatbots are built to deflect support questions. Ours is built to sell. As part of M2S, our Message to Sale system, we set up an AI sales chatbot on your WhatsApp number and your website chat, load it with your real products, prices and policies, and run it for you. It answers in seconds in English or Arabic, recommends the right product, handles objections, closes the sale with a payment link, cash on delivery or a booking, and passes hot leads to your team with a summary of the chat.

The problem

Why most chatbots don't sell

The old kind of chatbot is a menu: press 1 for prices, press 2 for location. It breaks the moment a customer types something it didn't expect, which on WhatsApp is most of the time. UAE customers write in mixed Arabic and English, ask three questions in one message and come back two days later expecting you to remember them.

Newer AI chatbots read messages properly, but most are built for support: they answer a question and stop. They don't ask what the customer needs, don't suggest the right product, don't take the order and don't chase the person who said "let me think about it". Worse, a badly set-up bot will guess a price or a policy it was never told. A sales chatbot has to do the opposite: stick to your real information, move every conversation towards a sale, and know exactly when to hand over to a person.

Built and run for you

FAQ

Common questions about AI chatbots

Will the chatbot make up prices or answers?

No. It answers only from the products, prices and policies you give us, and we test it on realistic conversations before launch. Anything it can't answer goes to your team with an instant WhatsApp alert and a summary of the chat.

How is a sales chatbot different from a support chatbot?

A support bot tries to close the ticket. A sales chatbot tries to close the sale: it asks the questions your best salesperson would ask, recommends the right product, handles objections, takes the order or booking, and follows up when the customer goes quiet.

Which channels does it work on?

WhatsApp, on your own number with the WhatsApp app still usable, and your website chat. The same assistant can also answer Instagram DMs, and every lead lands in one pipeline.

Does it reply in Arabic?

Yes. It replies in English or Arabic, in the language the customer writes in, and switches if they do.

Do I need a developer to maintain it?

No. We build it and run it for you. When your prices, stock or offers change, we update what the chatbot knows, and we keep tuning it from real conversations.
