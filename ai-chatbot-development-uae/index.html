An AI sales chatbot in the UAE that sells without guessing

An AI sales chatbot is only as good as what it's given and the limits it's set. We build it from your real products, prices and policies, test it against real questions and launch it on WhatsApp and your website chat. It starts with a free 30-minute audit.

Book a free audit Ask on WhatsApp

An AI sales chatbot is a chat assistant that answers buying questions, recommends products and closes the sale in the conversation. A well-built one works only from the business's own catalogue, prices and policies, refuses to make anything up, replies in the customer's language, and passes anything it cannot answer to a person with a summary of the chat.

Four stages from your information to a live chatbot

  1. 1

    Gather your information

    We collect your products, prices, delivery terms, opening hours and policies, and decide with you what the assistant may and may not say.

    Without it, the bot answers from generic knowledge and gets your details wrong.

  2. 2

    Set the guardrails

    Prices come only from your list. Anything outside it, such as a custom order, goes to your team instead.

    Without it, the bot quotes a discount you never offered.

  3. 3

    Write the tone

    We set the assistant to reply in your brand's tone, in English or Arabic, matching the language the customer writes in.

    Without it, replies sound like a template.

  4. 4

    Test before launch

    We run it against real questions from your inbox and fix the answers before any customer sees it.

    Without it, the first customers find the mistakes.

What the chatbot will never do

The rule we start from: if the answer isn't in what you gave us, the assistant doesn't guess. It says a colleague will confirm, and your team gets an instant WhatsApp alert with a short summary, so the customer doesn't have to repeat themselves.

It also won't invent stock, delivery dates or discounts, and it won't keep pushing a customer who has said no. When your prices or policies change, we update its information so it stays accurate.

Chats handed to a person

  • Questions outside your product list or policies
  • Bulk, custom or trade orders
  • Complaints and refunds
  • Anything the customer asks to discuss with a person

WhatsApp and website chat, from one set of information

WhatsApp

On your own number through the official API. Your team can still use the WhatsApp app and step in at any point.

Website chat

The same assistant on your site, so a visitor gets the same answers, prices and policies as a WhatsApp customer.

Checkout in the chat

When the customer is ready: a card payment link from your payment provider, cash on delivery, or a booking.

Questions we run before it goes live

We take real messages from your inbox, in English and Arabic, and check every answer against your information: prices, delivery areas, sizes, opening hours, returns. Gaps get filled before launch, not after a customer finds them.

After launch, chats it handed to your team show us what's missing, and we add it.

What the chatbot knows when it goes live

Everything it says comes from a set of information we build with you and keep up to date. When you add a product, change a price or update a policy, tell us and we update it, so it doesn't drift away from what your shop or front desk would say.

It replies in your brand's tone: formal or relaxed, short or detailed, with or without emoji. That's agreed before launch, not left to the model.

  • Products or services, with variants and prices
  • Delivery areas, fees and times, or booking rules
  • Payment options you accept
  • Opening hours and contact details
  • Returns, exchanges and cancellation policies
  • Words and claims you never want used

Questions about how the chatbot is built

What do you need from us to build the chatbot?

Your products or services, prices, delivery or booking terms, opening hours and policies such as returns. We turn that into the information the assistant answers from and check it with you.

Can it make up a price or a discount?

No. It only quotes prices from your list. If something isn't there, it tells the customer a colleague will confirm and sends your team a chat summary.

How do you test the chatbot before launch?

We run real questions from your inbox through it, in English and Arabic, check each answer against your information and fix any gaps before customers use it.

Does it work on our website as well as WhatsApp?

Yes. The same assistant can run on WhatsApp and your website chat, from the same products, prices and policies.
