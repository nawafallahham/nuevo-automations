Follow-ups

This is where most of the money is

Most customers don't say no. They get distracted. M2S follows up for you, politely, and hands the conversation straight back to the AI the moment they reply, knowing exactly what was in their cart.

  • Stops the moment the customer buys or replies
  • Quiet hours: never messages at night
  • Stops for anyone who says "stop"
  • One sequence per customer per week, so nobody gets spammed
  1. 30 min
    Abandoned cart reminder

    "Hi Sara, you left the linen set in your cart. Here's a link back to checkout."

  2. 4 hours
    Quiet lead nudge

    A short message that restarts the conversation where it stopped.

  3. 1 day
    Offer to finish in the chat

    "Want me to place the order for you here? Card or cash on delivery."

  4. 3 days
    Last, gentle check-in

    Optional discount code, only if you want one.

  5. Reply
    Customer replies, sequence stops

    The AI picks up the chat and closes the sale.

FAQ

Questions before you book

Anything else? Ask us on WhatsApp. A real person replies.

How much does M2S cost?

It depends on your ad budget, your channels and how many products or services we load. You get a fixed monthly quote after the free audit. Ad spend is paid to Meta directly, so you always see exactly what the ads cost. How pricing works.

Do I need to change my WhatsApp number?

No. The AI sales assistant runs on your own WhatsApp number, and your team can still use the WhatsApp app.

Does the AI speak Arabic?

Yes. It replies in English or Arabic, matching the language your customer writes in.

What if the AI doesn't know an answer?

It never invents prices or products. Anything it can't answer goes straight to your team, with a summary of the chat.

Do you guarantee results?

No honest agency can promise an exact number. What we promise is that every lead gets a reply in seconds and every quiet lead gets followed up, and you see what came back in your monthly M2S Results report.
