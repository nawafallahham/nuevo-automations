Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Nuevo Automations collects when you use this website, why we collect it, and what control you have over it. Nuevo Automations FZE LLC is a free zone establishment registered in Sharjah Publishing City Free Zone, United Arab Emirates, and can be reached at nawafallahham@nuevoautomations.com.

What we collect

We only collect what we actually need:

  • Booking details. Audits and consultations are scheduled through Calendly (see below). When you book, Calendly passes us your name, email address, your chosen date and time and your answers to the booking questions (such as your WhatsApp number and business name), and we store them. We also record the time of booking and how you found us (for example the ad campaign or website that sent you). We do not store your IP address with your booking. If you accepted cookies, your IP address and browser type are held for a short time so the booking can be reported to Meta as described below, and are deleted within two days.
  • Audit questions. Before the booking calendar opens, we ask for your name and WhatsApp number, then four short questions about your brand (your role, weekly orders, average order value and how you run Meta ads). We store these with how you found us, and, if your brand is a fit, the company name you enter, so we can prepare and confirm your call. We may message you on WhatsApp about the audit you started. Your browser remembers your answers in its local storage for 30 days so you don’t have to answer again. A one-way code made from your IP address is stored to limit abuse; your IP address itself is not.
  • Messages you send us. If you contact us on WhatsApp or by email, we keep that conversation so we can answer you and follow up.
  • Anonymous usage statistics. Our own analytics count page views, clicks on our booking and WhatsApp buttons, the website or campaign that referred you, and the page you visited. It uses no cookies. Each visitor is counted with a random-looking code that changes every day, so we cannot recognise you from one day to the next, and your IP address and browser details are never stored.
  • How you found us. When you arrive from an ad or another website, your browser remembers that source (the campaign tags in the link and the referring site) in its local storage for up to 30 days. It stays on your device and is only sent to us if you book, so the booking can be credited to the right ad.
  • Advertising data. If you accept cookies, the Meta advertising tools described below collect standard technical information such as pages viewed, device and browser type.

We do not ask for payment card details on this website, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Why we use it

  • To arrange and attend your audit or consultation, and to contact you about it.
  • To respond to enquiries and provide the services you ask us for.
  • To measure whether our advertising works, and to show relevant ads to people likely to be interested.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent abuse of the booking system.

Where the law requires a lawful basis, we rely on your consent for advertising cookies, and on the necessity of performing a contract or our legitimate business interests for everything else.

Cookies and advertising tools

This website uses the Meta Pixel, a tool provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. (Facebook and Instagram). It sets cookies and reports back to Meta when you visit a page or click one of our contact buttons.

If you accept cookies, we also use Google Analytics, provided by Google LLC, to see how visitors find and use the site: pages viewed, how long you stay, the device and browser you use, your approximate location (country or city), and whether you click our booking or WhatsApp buttons, give your details for an audit or book one. It sets cookies and sends this to Google. We don't send Google your name, phone number or email address.

We use it to measure how our advertising performs and to reach people who have visited the site. Meta may combine this with information it already holds about you. You can read how Meta handles this in the Meta Privacy Policy, and adjust what you see through Meta's ad preferences.

If you accepted cookies and then give your name and number for an audit, or book one, our server also tells Meta about it directly, using the Meta Conversions API. This lets us measure which ads lead to bookings even when browsers block the pixel. We send your phone number, email address (if given), first name and country only in hashed form (scrambled with SHA-256 so the original cannot be read), together with your IP address, browser type and Meta's ad-click identifier, which Meta uses to match the booking to an ad. If you declined cookies, nothing about your booking is sent to Meta.

You can decline these cookies using the banner shown when you first visit, change your mind at any time using the link at the bottom of this page, or block cookies in your browser settings. Declining does not affect your ability to browse the site or book an audit.

The booking calendar on this site is provided by Calendly and loads from calendly.com. Calendly may set the cookies it needs to run the calendar and handles the details you type into it under its own privacy notice.

We also load fonts from Google Fonts. That provider receives your IP address as a normal part of serving those files.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the service providers that make this website work:

  • Cloudflare — hosting for the website, the database that stores your booking, and our anonymous usage statistics.
  • Calendly, Inc. — our booking calendar. Bookings are scheduled through Calendly, which stores your booking, sends you its confirmation and reminder emails, and passes the booking on to us.
  • Cloudflare Email Service — sends our internal booking alert email.
  • OrcaFlo — our own WhatsApp messaging platform, which receives your booking details so we can send your confirmation and reminders on WhatsApp.
  • Meta Platforms — advertising measurement through the Meta Pixel and Conversions API, only if you accepted cookies, as described above.
  • Google — website analytics through Google Analytics, only if you accepted cookies, as described above.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) — when we message you about your booking, or if you choose to message us there.

Some of these providers store data outside the UAE, including in the United States and Europe. We only use established providers that offer appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

How long we keep it

Booking records are automatically deleted about 90 days after the appointment date. Audit question answers, names and companies are deleted after about 180 days. Anonymous usage statistics are deleted after about 13 months. Enquiries and correspondence are kept for as long as we have an active relationship, and then removed when no longer needed. Advertising cookie data is retained by Meta according to their own policy. Google Analytics data is kept for 14 months.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it for marketing. Email nawafallahham@nuevoautomations.com and we will respond within 30 days. If you are in the UAE, you may also raise a concern with the UAE Data Office; if you are in the UK or EU, with your local data protection authority.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
